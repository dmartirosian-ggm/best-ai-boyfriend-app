Best AI Boyfriend App is an independent review site for people looking for a male AI companion. We're written by **The BestAIBoyfriend Team**, a small group of writers and researchers who follow the AI companion space closely and compare the apps on the things that matter in a relationship-style chat.

## Why this site exists

AI boyfriend apps have gone from a niche curiosity to a crowded market in a few short years. The trouble is that a lot of what you find when you search for them is written by the apps themselves, or by sites that rank whoever pays the most. Prices hide behind token systems, privacy policies are vague, and "boyfriend mode" sometimes turns out to be an afterthought in an app built for AI girlfriends.

We wanted a place that answers the questions people actually ask:

- Will he remember me next week?
- Can I hear his voice, or see him?
- What will this really cost each month?
- What happens to my chats?
- Is this app actually built for someone who wants a boyfriend?

## How we review

Every app is scored out of 10 using the same weighted criteria: conversation and memory, boyfriend features, value, privacy and safety, and ease of use. We check prices and policies against the apps' own pages and app-store listings, note where information is uncertain, and update pages when apps change. The full details are on our [how we test](/how-we-test/) page.

## How we make money

This site is free to read. Some apps pay us a commission if you sign up through our links. That never decides where an app ranks: apps without affiliate programs are reviewed and scored exactly the same way, and several of them rank above apps that pay us. Read our [affiliate disclosure](/affiliate-disclosure/) for specifics.

## Our editorial principles

- **No made-up facts.** Prices, features and policies come from verifiable sources, and we say so when sources disagree.
- **Honest recommendations.** We point you to the app that fits your needs, even when it earns us nothing.
- **Privacy first.** We flag data breaches, regulatory fines and poor privacy policies prominently.
- **Wellbeing matters.** AI companions can be comforting, but they aren't a substitute for human connection or professional help. If you're struggling, in the US you can call or text 988 to reach the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline.

## Corrections and feedback

AI companion apps change prices, features and policies often. If you spot something out of date or wrong, we want to know, and we'll correct it. You can also read how we handle your data in our [privacy policy](/privacy/).
