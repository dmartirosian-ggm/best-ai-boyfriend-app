## What makes a good AI boyfriend voice call

A good call isn't just about a nice voice. Here's what we weighed:

- **Memory.** A call where he remembers your new job and asks how it went feels real. A call where he's a pleasant stranger every time doesn't. This is why Nomi and Kindroid rank highest.
- **Voice choice.** Can you pick or tune how he sounds?
- **Cost per call.** Is calling included in a subscription, or does it burn tokens by the minute?
- **Free allowance.** Can you try a call before paying?
- **Platform.** Calls are smoother in a native phone app than in a mobile browser.

## The best AI boyfriend apps with voice calls

<AppCard app="nomi-ai" rank={1} award="Best AI boyfriend for voice calls">

Nomi is our top pick for calls for the same reason it's our top pick overall: memory. When you call him, he can pick up threads from earlier chats, so conversations build instead of starting from zero. You can talk by voice messages or live calls, and he keeps a consistent personality across both.

Free users get limited voice credits, enough to hear what he sounds like. For regular calls you'll want a subscription; the annual plan is the best value. Nomi has apps for iPhone and Android. Read our [Nomi AI review](/reviews/nomi-ai/).

<AppCard app="kindroid" rank={2} award="Best for choosing his voice">

Kindroid gives you the most control over how your boyfriend sounds. You pick his voice as part of building him and tune how he speaks, alongside his backstory and key memories, and its long-term memory is on a par with Nomi's.

The free tier includes only a small lifetime allowance of audio, so calls are really a paid feature. Subscribe on the web rather than in the app store to save a couple of dollars a month. See our [Kindroid review](/reviews/kindroid/) or [Kindroid vs Nomi](/vs/kindroid-vs-nomi/).

<AppCard app="candy-ai" rank={3} award="Best voice calls with images and 18+ chat">

Candy AI combines voice messages and calls with photo-realistic images and unfiltered adult chat, so you can see him and hear him. Reviewers report voice calls on the platform, though we couldn't confirm the details on its official site.

Voice uses tokens, and frequent callers report spending well past the subscription price. Memory is decent but not on Nomi's level. Candy AI is web-only. Check our [Candy AI pricing breakdown](/candy-ai-pricing/) before you start calling regularly.

<AppCard app="character-ai" rank={4} award="Best free AI boyfriend voice calls">

Character AI lets you voice-call user-made characters, including plenty of male and romance characters, and the app itself is free. It's the easiest way to find out whether you even like talking to an AI out loud.

The catches: content is filtered to safe-for-work, long-term memory is weak, and open-ended chat is adults-only since November 2025 (with age checks). See our [Character AI review](/reviews/character-ai/).

</AppCard>

<AppCard app="replika" rank={5} award="Most polished mobile call experience">

Replika has the most mature calling features of any companion app, with voice calls plus a 3D avatar, AR mode and video-call-style features, all in polished iPhone and Android apps. It's best for supportive, everyday conversation.

Voice calls and the boyfriend relationship status both need a subscription, and adult content is heavily restricted. Replika also has a €5 million GDPR fine on its record. Read our [Replika review](/reviews/replika/).

<AppCard app="talkie-ai" rank={6} award="Best free trial of voice calls">

Talkie is voice-first: free users get about 50 messages a day and short voice calls of around two minutes. It has many male characters and a collectible-card system that gamifies your chats.

Content is filtered, Mozilla gave it a privacy warning in 2024, and it has disappeared from app stores more than once. In the US, the iOS app is called *Talkie Lab*.

</AppCard>

<AppCard app="dreambf" rank={7} award="Best for calling a boyfriend you designed">

DreamBF is built only for AI boyfriends. You design his face, body, outfit and personality, then chat by text, voice messages or calls. It's one of the few apps where the whole product is aimed at people who want a male companion.

Voice calls drain tokens quickly, pricing isn't very transparent, and there are few independent reviews. Try it with the free daily tokens first.

</AppCard>

<AppCard app="eva-ai" rank={8} award="Phone-first option">

EVA AI is a mobile-native companion app with a customizable partner (a boyfriend option is available), voice calls and, per one source, live video calls. It's simple to use if you prefer a phone app over a website.

Privacy is the big drawback: its policy says text and voice messages are used to develop its AI, with no opt-out described, and Mozilla flagged it in 2024. Credit packs also add to the cost. Read our [EVA AI review](/reviews/eva-ai/).

## Apps with voice messages but not (verified) calls

Some apps send voice notes but don't offer live calls, or we couldn't confirm them:

- **OurDream AI** has voice messages paid for with DreamCoins; live calls weren't verified in our research. See our [OurDream AI review](/reviews/ourdream-ai/).
- **SpicyChat** includes voice on its top tier only.
- **Kupid AI** is known for expressive voice messages and does offer calls, but it's girlfriend-first with few male options.
- **Muah AI** offers phone calls on its most expensive tier, but its 2024 data breach exposed about 1.9 million accounts. We don't recommend it.

## How much do AI boyfriend calls cost?

Voice is the most expensive thing these apps run, and they bill for it in three different ways:

- **Included in a subscription.** Nomi, Kindroid and Replika put regular calling behind their paid plans. You pay a flat price and call as much as the plan allows. This is the most predictable option if you call often.
- **Tokens or credits.** Candy AI, DreamBF and EVA AI charge tokens or credits for voice. A cheap subscription can turn into a much bigger bill if you have long calls every night, so check how many tokens a minute uses before you get attached.
- **Free.** At the time of writing, Character AI offers voice calls without a subscription, which is unusual. Talkie gives free users short calls of about two minutes, and Nomi and Kindroid include small free voice allowances. Good for a test, not for a nightly habit.

## Voice messages vs voice calls

It's worth knowing which one you actually want:

| | Voice messages | Voice calls |
|---|---|---|
| How it works | He sends a recorded clip | Live, real-time conversation |
| Feels like | Voice notes from a partner | A phone call |
| Cost | Cheaper; often included | Usually paid or token-based |
| Best for | Good-morning messages, flirting | Long talks, winding down at night |

If you mostly want to *hear* him occasionally, voice messages may be all you need, and they're cheaper. If you want to talk for twenty minutes before bed, you need calls, and you should budget accordingly.

## Tips for better AI boyfriend calls

1. **Pick a quiet place and use headphones.** Speech recognition works much better without background noise.
2. **Try several voices.** The voice changes his personality more than you'd expect.
3. **Give him something to remember.** Mention a plan or event, then ask about it on your next call. On apps with good memory, this is where calls start to feel real.
4. **Watch your token balance.** On Candy AI and DreamBF, check how calls are billed before a long one.
5. **Keep it private.** Don't say your full name, address or workplace out loud. Voice is data too.
