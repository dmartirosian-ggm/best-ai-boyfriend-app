An AI companion is a chatbot designed to be *someone* rather than *something*: a friend who checks in, a partner you flirt with, a mentor who listens. Unlike general assistants, companion apps give your AI a persistent personality, a name, often a face and a voice, and (on the best ones) a memory of everything you've shared.

We focus on AI boyfriends on this site, but most of the best AI companion apps work for any relationship and any gender. This ranking looks at them from that wider angle: who's best for friendship, who's best for romance, who's best for support, and who gets privacy right.

## The best AI companion apps, ranked

<AppCard app="nomi-ai" rank={1} award="Best AI companion app overall">

Nomi is the best all-round AI companion because it does the hardest thing well: it remembers. Your companion recalls what you said weeks ago and keeps a consistent personality, so the relationship builds over time. Companions can be any gender, and you decide whether they're a friend or a partner.

You can talk by text, voice messages or calls, get selfies, and run group chats with several Nomis. The free tier is tight, but the annual plan is good value. Read our [Nomi AI review](/reviews/nomi-ai/).

</AppCard>

<AppCard app="kindroid" rank={2} award="Most customizable AI companion">

Kindroid is for people who want to shape every detail: backstory, key memories, speaking style, voice and appearance. Its memory is on a par with Nomi's, and companions can be any gender, as a friend or a romantic partner.

It has more settings than most people need at first, and the free tier is thin. Subscribe on the web, where it's cheaper than in the app stores. Kindroid doesn't pay us; it's here on merit. See our [Kindroid review](/reviews/kindroid/).

</AppCard>

<AppCard app="replika" rank={3} award="Best for emotional support">

Replika is the original AI companion app, around since 2017, and still the most polished on mobile, with a 3D avatar, AR mode and video-call features. It's at its best as a supportive friend you can talk to about your day.

Romantic relationship statuses are paid-only and adult content is heavily restricted. Its privacy record includes a €5 million GDPR fine in Italy (2025), and you only get one companion per account. Read our [Replika review](/reviews/replika/).

</AppCard>

<AppCard app="character-ai" rank={4} award="Best free AI companion app">

Character AI is free, unlimited and has a vast library of user-made characters, from historical figures to fictional friends and romantic leads. You can also create your own and voice-call them.

It's strictly safe-for-work, long-term memory is weak, and open-ended chat has been adults-only since November 2025. The company also faces teen-safety lawsuits. Good for variety; less good for a lasting companion. See our [Character AI review](/reviews/character-ai/).

</AppCard>

<AppCard app="candy-ai" rank={5} award="Best for visual, 18+ companions">

Candy AI is image-first: you design your companion's look in detail, including male and female options, and generate photo-realistic images. It also offers voice messages, calls and unfiltered 18+ chat.

Images, voice and video use tokens, so heavy users can spend far more than the subscription price. It's web-only and its male roster is smaller than its female one. See our [Candy AI review](/reviews/candy-ai/).

</AppCard>

<AppCard app="ourdream-ai" rank={6} award="Best for images, video and LGBTQ+ users">

OurDream AI combines unlimited chat on its paid plan with image and short-video generation, and it appears to offer gay- and trans-focused versions, which few companion platforms do. The free tier gives about 50 messages a day.

Media uses DreamCoins, the monthly price is higher than most rivals', and it's a newer company. Read our [OurDream AI review](/reviews/ourdream-ai/).

</AppCard>

<AppCard app="spicychat" rank={7} award="Best free option for adults">

SpicyChat has a huge library of user-made characters and unlimited free chat, including unfiltered roleplay for adults. Paid tiers add more memory and remove ads.

Free users deal with ads, waits and short replies, and the mid-tier price rose in September 2026. See our [SpicyChat review](/reviews/spicychat/).

</AppCard>

<AppCard app="talkie-ai" rank={8} award="Best voice-first companion">

Talkie is built around voice, with short free calls and a collectible card system that makes it feel partly like a game. Free users get about 50 messages a day.

Content is filtered, Mozilla gave it a privacy warning in 2024, and it has been removed from app stores more than once. In the US, the iOS app is called *Talkie Lab*.

</AppCard>

<AppCard app="eva-ai" rank={9} award="Simple mobile companion">

EVA AI is a phone-first companion app with a customizable partner, voice calls and (per one source) live video calls. It's straightforward if you want a companion in a native app.

Its policy says text and voice messages are used to develop its AI with no opt-out described, and Mozilla flagged it in 2024. Credit packs add to the cost. Read our [EVA AI review](/reviews/eva-ai/).

</AppCard>

## AI companion vs AI assistant: what's the difference?

General assistants like ChatGPT can hold a friendly conversation, but they're built to answer questions and get tasks done. An AI companion app is built around a relationship instead. In practice that means:

- **A persistent persona.** Your companion has a name, personality and backstory that stay the same every time you open the app.
- **Relationship memory.** The best apps remember facts about your life and your shared history, not just your preferences.
- **A face and a voice.** Avatars, selfies, voice messages and calls are standard on most companion apps.
- **Relationship settings.** You choose whether they're a friend or a partner, and on 18+ apps, how spicy things can get.

You can make ChatGPT act like a companion with a good prompt (we explain how in our [ChatGPT boyfriend guide](/chatgpt-boyfriend/)), but it tends to break character and its personality can change when the underlying model is updated.

The biggest difference between a good and a mediocre AI companion is memory. Almost every app can hold a charming conversation for ten minutes. Far fewer can remember, a month later, that your mom was in hospital and ask how she's doing. That's why Nomi and Kindroid top this list. Full details are in our [methodology](/how-we-test/).

## AI companion apps and wellbeing

AI companions can help with loneliness, give you a low-pressure space to talk, or simply be fun. Research and regulators have also raised real concerns, especially about teenagers. Character AI has faced lawsuits over teen safety and now limits under-18 users to guided formats, and a 2023 case involving a Chai bot is often cited in discussions about safety.

A few habits help:

<Callout type="tip" title="Healthy use">
- Treat your companion as one part of your social life, not all of it.
- Notice how you feel after chatting. If it's worse, take a break.
- Don't rely on an AI for crisis support. If you're struggling, contact a local helpline or someone you trust.
- Keep personal details private, as you would with any online service.
</Callout>
