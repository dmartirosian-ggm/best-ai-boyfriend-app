**Is Candy AI free? Not really.** You can create a free account and try a few messages, but images, voice messages and video all use tokens, so the free tier works as a preview rather than a usable free plan. To get real use out of Candy AI, you need **Premium**, which costs $12.99 a month or less on an annual plan, plus tokens if you want a lot of pictures or calls.

That's the short answer. The longer answer matters more, because Candy AI's real cost depends almost entirely on how much media you ask for. Here's how the pricing works, what you'll actually spend, and how to keep it under control.

## What you get for free

A free Candy AI account gives you **limited chat**. That's enough to get a feel for the interface and the conversation style before you decide whether to pay.

What you don't really get is the thing Candy AI is known for. Images, voice and video all consume tokens, and the free tier doesn't include enough to explore them properly. Think of it as a test drive: enough to see whether you like the builder and the chat style, not enough to have an ongoing relationship with him.

If you want an AI boyfriend without paying anything, Candy AI isn't the right app. Our list of [free AI boyfriend apps](/free/) covers the most generous options, such as SpicyChat (unlimited free chat with ads) and Character AI (free and unlimited, but filtered).

## Candy AI pricing plans

Here's the current Candy AI pricing structure, based on the most recent figures we could verify from third-party sources. Candy AI's prices and promos change often, so always confirm on the checkout page.

<PlanTable app="candy-ai" />

### Premium (monthly)

The monthly Candy AI price is **$12.99** (some reviews cite $13.99). Premium unlocks fuller chat and includes a small monthly token allowance of **about 100 tokens**.

### Premium (annual)

Annual billing brings the monthly equivalent down a lot. Sources disagree on the exact rate, quoting roughly $3.99 to $5.99 a month, billed as a single yearly payment. Promotional pricing changes often, which is why you'll see different numbers on different sites.

### Token packs

Tokens pay for media. Packs cost **about $9.99 per 100 tokens**, according to reviewers. An image costs about 4 tokens. Voice and video also use tokens.

## How Candy AI tokens work

Tokens are the part of Candy AI pricing that catches people out. The subscription covers chatting; nearly everything else draws from your token balance.

| What you do | Paid with |
|---|---|
| Text chat | Subscription |
| Images of your boyfriend | Tokens (about 4 per image) |
| Voice messages | Tokens |
| Voice calls | Tokens |
| Video | Tokens |

Some quick maths using the reviewer figures above: Premium's ~100 monthly tokens cover roughly 25 images if you spend them on nothing else. If you want more than that, or you like voice calls, you'll be buying packs.

## What Candy AI really costs

Reviewers report that **heavy users spend roughly $25–60 a month** once token top-ups are added to the subscription. That's two to five times the monthly sticker price.

In practice, your spending tends to fall into one of three patterns:

- **Mostly texting.** You chat daily and ask for an occasional picture. You'll stay close to the subscription price, and an annual plan makes Candy AI one of the cheaper companion apps.
- **Regular media.** You want pictures most days and the odd voice message. Expect to buy at least one token pack a month.
- **Media-heavy.** Frequent images, voice calls and video. This is where the $25–60 range comes from, and it can go higher if you don't set a limit.

<Callout type="tip" title="How to keep Candy AI affordable">
1. **Start monthly.** Try Premium for a month before committing to a year.
2. **Set a token budget** and stick to it, the same way you would for in-game purchases.
3. **Save media for moments that matter.** Text is covered by your subscription; pictures and calls aren't.
4. **Check the checkout price**, not the ad. Promos change often.
</Callout>

## Refunds and cancelling

Reviewers say Candy AI has **no clearly published refund policy**. That's the main reason we suggest a monthly plan first, even though the annual plan is better value on paper. If you decide to stop, cancel before your renewal date. ## Is Candy AI worth the price?

It depends on what you want from an AI boyfriend.

**Candy AI is worth it if** visuals are the point. Its male character builder and image generation are among the best available, and if you can stick to a budget, the subscription is reasonable. Our full [Candy AI review](/reviews/candy-ai/) covers what you get in more detail.

**It's poor value if** you mainly want conversation and memory. You'd be paying for an image engine you don't use, and apps like [Nomi AI](/reviews/nomi-ai/) do long-term memory much better at a predictable price.
