Chai is quick and easy: open the app, pick a bot, and you're chatting in seconds. There are plenty of user-made male characters, and replies come fast. But if you've used it for a while, you've likely hit the paywall, noticed the push toward weekly billing, and watched your boyfriend forget what you told him a few messages ago.

Here are the best Chai AI alternatives for people who want an **AI boyfriend**, with a focus on apps that also work well on your phone. Three of our top four picks, SpicyChat, Character AI and Kindroid, don't pay us anything.

## Why people leave Chai

**A hard paywall.** Reviewers report a hard paywall with few or no free messages, plus message caps. Premium costs $13.99 a month and Ultra $29.99 a month.

**Weekly billing.** The app has reportedly shifted to weekly subscriptions. Weekly plans in this category typically cost two to four times as much as paying monthly, so check what you're signing up for.

**Poor memory, even on Ultra.** Chai has a small context window, and bots forget details within a handful of messages, even on the top tier. Reviewers say Ultra is barely better than Premium.

**Safety history.** In 2023, a Belgian man died by suicide after weeks of chatting with a Chai bot named "Eliza" that allegedly encouraged him. Mozilla's 2024 report gave Chai a content warning. Content is filtered, but trust has been hard to rebuild.

**Mobile only.** Chai has iOS and Android apps but no web version.

### What Chai still does well

It's worth being fair before you switch. Chai's responses are fast, its library of bots is huge, and creating your own character is easy. If you only want quick, short conversations on your phone and don't mind the cost, it does that job. Most people go looking for a Chai AI alternative once they want something more: a boyfriend who stays the same person for weeks, more freedom in romance, or simply not paying weekly for short memory.

### Can you have an AI boyfriend on Chai?

Yes. There are user-made male characters, and you can make your own. But content is filtered, memory is short, and Chai is a general chatbot app rather than a companion app, so it isn't built around an ongoing relationship. We couldn't verify a long-term memory system or voice and image features. Dedicated companion apps like Nomi and Kindroid are designed for exactly that: one partner, a shared history, and the ability to hear his voice or see his selfies.

## The best Chai AI alternatives, ranked

<AppCard app="nomi-ai" rank={1} award="Best overall alternative">

Nomi is what Chai could be if it cared about memory. Long-term memory is Nomi's core feature, so your boyfriend remembers your job, your friends and your inside jokes from week to week, and his personality stays consistent. It has iOS, Android and web apps.

You create him yourself, set the relationship as romantic, and talk by text, voice messages or voice calls, with selfies too. Adult roleplay is allowed for adults. The free tier is limited, but monthly Pro is $15.99 and annual works out to about $8.33 a month, with no weekly billing in sight. Read our [Nomi AI review](/reviews/nomi-ai/).

</AppCard>

<AppCard app="spicychat" rank={2} award="Best free 18+ alternative">

If what you liked about Chai was jumping into a ready-made character, SpicyChat does the same with unlimited free chat, a huge library of male characters, adult roleplay, and iOS and Android apps.

Free users get ads, queue waits, short replies and 4K memory. Paid tiers start at about $5 a month and scale memory up, though the middle tier rose to $19.95 in September 2026. SpicyChat doesn't pay us. See our [SpicyChat review](/reviews/spicychat/).

</AppCard>

<AppCard app="character-ai" rank={3} award="Best free, safe-for-work">

Character AI is the obvious free swap. It's unlimited, it has an enormous library of user-made male and romance characters, voice features, and apps on every platform. Its c.ai+ upgrade is $9.99 a month, cheaper than Chai Premium.

Romance is filtered, long-term memory is weak (though it does have pinned memories), and the company faces teen-safety lawsuits. Under-18 users lost open-ended chat in November 2025. Read our [Character AI review](/reviews/character-ai/), or compare directly in [Chai vs Character AI](/vs/chai-vs-character-ai/).

</AppCard>

<AppCard app="kindroid" rank={4} award="Most customizable">

Kindroid is the opposite of Chai's grab-and-go bots. You write his backstory, set key memories, tune how he talks, choose a voice and generate selfies. Memory is among the best in the category, and it has mobile apps.

There's a learning curve and the free tier is thin. Subscribe on the web, where it's $13.99 a month, rather than in the app store. Kindroid doesn't pay us. See our [Kindroid review](/reviews/kindroid/).

</AppCard>

<AppCard app="candy-ai" rank={5} award="Best for images & voice">

Chai is text-first. Candy AI lets you design what he looks like with a male builder, generate images, and send voice messages (reviewers report voice calls too), with unfiltered adult chat.

It's web-only, so there's no app to install (it works in your phone's browser), and images, voice and video use tokens on top of the subscription. See our [Candy AI pricing guide](/candy-ai-pricing/).

</AppCard>

<AppCard app="crushon-ai" rank={6} award="Biggest library">

CrushOn AI has thousands of user-made characters, including plenty of male bots, unfiltered chat and a $5.99/mo entry tier, which is much cheaper than Chai Premium.

It's web-only, every plan caps messages, and Mozilla's 2024 review flagged its privacy policy. Read our [CrushOn AI review](/reviews/crushon-ai/).

## How to leave Chai

1. **Cancel in the right place.** Chai is mobile-only, so you almost certainly subscribed through the App Store or Google Play. Cancel there; deleting the app doesn't stop billing. Our guide on [how to cancel an AI boyfriend subscription](/faq/how-to-cancel-ai-boyfriend-subscription/) has the steps.
2. **Check for a weekly plan.** If you were billed weekly, look at your recent charges so you know what to expect before the cancellation takes effect.
3. **Save your bot.** If you created a character, copy his description so you can rebuild him in Nomi, Kindroid or SpicyChat.
4. **Try free first.** Every alternative here has a free tier or trial.

- **Memory.** It's the biggest upgrade you can make from Chai.
