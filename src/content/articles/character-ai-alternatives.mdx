Character AI is where a lot of people first discover they like talking to a fictional boyfriend. It's free, the library is enormous, and there's a romance bot for every taste. Then the cracks show: the filter cuts scenes off mid-sentence, he forgets your name after a long session, and since late 2025 the app has become stricter about who can do what.

If you've hit that wall, you have good options. Below are the best Character AI alternatives for people who want an **AI boyfriend** specifically, ranked on memory, how far romance can go, voice, and what you'll really pay.

## Why people leave Character AI

**The filter.** Character AI does not allow adult content, and romantic scenes are filtered. You can flirt, but the moment a conversation turns intimate the reply gets cut or redirected. For adults who want a real relationship-style chat, this is the number one reason to switch.

**Weak long-term memory.** Character AI has a limited context window. Pinned memories help, but long-term recall is weak, so a boyfriend you've talked to for weeks can still forget your job, your dog or the argument you had yesterday.

**The November 2025 under-18 change.** On 25 November 2025, Character AI removed open-ended chat for users under 18 and limited them to guided formats like Stories. To enforce it, the app estimates age from behavior and can ask for a Persona ID or face check. That's a reasonable safety step, but it means some adults get caught up in verification prompts they find intrusive.

**Lawsuits and trust.** Character AI faces several teen-harm lawsuits, and Google and Character.AI agreed in January 2026 to settle some of them (terms confidential, pending court approval). That doesn't make the app unsafe for adults, but it explains why the company keeps tightening its rules.

## How to switch from Character AI

You can't export a chat history from Character AI into another app, but you can bring *him* with you.

1. **Copy his definition.** If you made the character, save his description, greeting and example dialogue somewhere safe.
2. **Rebuild him in a builder app.** Paste the personality into Nomi's or Kindroid's backstory fields. Kindroid's key memories are ideal for the facts you want him to never forget.
3. **Seed the memories.** In the first few chats, tell him the important things: how you met in the story, your name, your inside jokes. Apps with strong memory will hold onto them.
4. **Start on the free tier.** Every app here has a free tier or trial. Test chat quality for a few days before paying, and if you subscribe, check whether it's cheaper on the web than in the app store.

## What to look for in an alternative

- **Memory first.** It's the difference between a chatbot and a boyfriend. Look for long-term memory, not just a big context window.
- **Real price, not sticker price.** Token and coin systems (Candy AI, OurDream) can cost far more than the headline subscription if you generate lots of images.
- **Privacy.** Use a separate email, avoid sharing your real name or workplace, and favor apps without a breach history.
- **18+ means 18+.** Every alternative here is for adults only. Character AI accounts start at 13, with the under-18 restrictions above.
