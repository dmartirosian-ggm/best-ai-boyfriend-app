CrushOn AI is great at one thing: giving you thousands of ready-made characters to choose from, with no filter. If you've found a few male characters you love there, you've probably also run into its limits. Messages are capped on every plan, unlimited costs $49.90 a month, memory is short on the free tier, and Mozilla's privacy review is hard to unread.

Here are the best CrushOn AI alternatives for people who want an **AI boyfriend**, whether you want another big library, fewer limits, or one companion who actually remembers you. We've included three apps that don't pay us, SpicyChat, Kindroid and Janitor AI, because for some readers they're simply the better fit.

## Why people leave CrushOn AI

**Message caps on every tier.** The Standard plan ($5.99/mo) gives about 2,000 messages a month and Premium ($14.99/mo) about 6,000. Only Deluxe at $49.90/mo is unlimited. For a daily romance, 2,000 messages can go faster than you'd think.

**Short memory unless you pay more.** Free users get recent-message memory only; higher tiers get a larger context. Long-running storylines suffer.

**Privacy concerns.** In February 2024, Mozilla's *Privacy Not Included* review gave CrushOn AI a warning label. It noted the privacy policy allows collecting sensitive data such as sexual health, medication and gender-affirming care information, and flagged disturbing user-generated content. The operating company also isn't clearly identified.

**Ads on the free tier.** Free users see ads and get a limited number of messages.

**Web only.** There are no native mobile apps.

To be fair: its entry price is low, the library is huge, it's unfiltered, and you can choose between several AI models. A 30% discount for annual billing was reportedly introduced in April 2026. If caps and privacy don't bother you, it's still a solid pick.
</Callout>

## The best CrushOn AI alternatives, ranked

<AppCard app="spicychat" rank={1} award="Closest alternative">

SpicyChat is the most similar app to CrushOn AI: a huge user-made library with lots of male characters, adult roleplay allowed, and group chats. The big difference is that free chat is unlimited, and it has iOS and Android apps.

Free users get ads, queue waits, short replies and 4K memory. Paid tiers scale memory clearly (8K, then 16K to 32K), but prices rose in September 2026, so the middle tier now costs $19.95/mo. SpicyChat isn't an affiliate partner; it's here because it's the honest closest match. Read our [SpicyChat review](/reviews/spicychat/).

</AppCard>

<AppCard app="nomi-ai" rank={2} award="Best for one long-term boyfriend">

If you keep coming back to the same CrushOn character, you may be happier building him in Nomi. Its long-term memory is among the best in the category, so he stays consistent and remembers your history. You get voice messages, voice calls and selfies, and adult roleplay is allowed.

You trade a library of thousands for one companion you shape yourself (you can have more than one, and group chats are supported). It's a small company with no reported breaches. The annual plan is about $8.33 a month. See our [Nomi AI review](/reviews/nomi-ai/).

OurDream AI's Premium plan includes unlimited messages, so you won't watch a counter tick down. It also generates images and short videos, and has gay- and trans-focused versions. The free tier allows about 50 messages a day.

Media uses DreamCoins, the monthly price ($19.99) is high, though the annual plan brings it to $9.99 a month, and video can be slow at peak times. It's a new company. Read our [OurDream AI review](/reviews/ourdream-ai/).

Media costs tokens on top of the subscription, the male roster is smaller than the female one, and it's web-only. Check our [Candy AI pricing guide](/candy-ai-pricing/) and [safety review](/is-candy-ai-safe/).

## How to switch from CrushOn AI

1. **Save your favorite characters' details.** Copy their descriptions and greetings; there's no export.
2. **Find or rebuild him.** Search SpicyChat's or Janitor AI's libraries for a similar character, or rebuild him in Nomi or Kindroid with your notes as his backstory.
3. **Delete what you don't want stored.** Before you go, clear chats you'd rather not leave behind and check the account deletion options.
4. **Cancel before renewal.** Our [subscription cancellation guide](/faq/how-to-cancel-ai-boyfriend-subscription/) has the steps.

## What to look for in an alternative

- **Caps you can live with.** Check messages per month, not just price. Unlimited on paid (OurDream) or unlimited on free (SpicyChat) changes how you chat.
- **Memory that matches how you use it.** Hopping between characters needs less memory than one long relationship.
- **A cleaner privacy record.** Look for a named operator, no breach history and a clear policy on training with your chats. Our guide to [whether AI boyfriends are safe](/faq/are-ai-boyfriends-safe/) explains what to check.
- **18+ only.** Every app on this page is for adults.
