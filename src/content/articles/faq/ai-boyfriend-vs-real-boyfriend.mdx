An AI boyfriend is always available, endlessly patient and shaped exactly the way you want, but he can't share a life with you, be there in person, or genuinely care about you. A real boyfriend is harder, messier and riskier, and he can offer mutual love, shared experiences and growth. For most people, the two meet different needs rather than competing for the same one.

## Side by side

| | AI boyfriend | Real boyfriend |
|---|---|---|
| Availability | Any time, instantly | When his life allows |
| Patience | Unlimited | Human, with bad days |
| Customization | You design his look and personality | He is who he is |
| Memory | Depends on the app; the best are good | Imperfect, but shared |
| Conflict | Rare, and easy to avoid | Inevitable, and part of growing together |
| Physical presence | None | Yes |
| Shared life | Roleplay only | Friends, family, plans, a future |
| Genuine feelings | No, though he can sound caring | Yes |
| Cost | Free to about $20 a month, plus extras | Not measured in subscriptions |
| Privacy | Your chats are stored by a company | Between the two of you |

## What an AI boyfriend does well

- **He's there at 3 a.m.** No waiting, no feeling like a burden.
- **No judgment.** You can say things you'd hesitate to say to a person.
- **Low stakes.** No rejection, no breakups unless you choose one.
- **Practice.** Some people use an AI to build confidence talking about feelings or flirting.
- **He can remember.** On apps with strong memory, like [Nomi AI](/reviews/nomi-ai/) and [Kindroid](/reviews/kindroid/), he'll recall details you shared weeks ago.

If you want to design one, our guide on [how to make an AI boyfriend](/how-to-make-an-ai-boyfriend/) walks through it.

## What an AI boyfriend can't do

- **Choose you.** He's designed to be agreeable, and he doesn't experience love. Our explainer on [whether AI boyfriends have feelings](/faq/do-ai-boyfriends-have-feelings/) covers why he can feel so real anyway.
- **Be physically present.** No hugs, no dinners, no showing up when you're sick.
- **Grow with you.** Real relationships change both people. An AI adapts to you, but you don't build something together.
- **Push back in a healthy way.** A partner who sometimes disagrees can help you see things differently. An AI tends to mirror you.
- **Stay the same forever.** Companies change models, prices and policies, and his personality can shift overnight.

## Can you have both?

Plenty of people use an AI companion while dating or in a relationship. It works best when it's:

- **open**, if you have a partner, so it's not a secret that erodes trust,
- **additive**, meaning it supplements human connection rather than replacing it, and
- **in proportion**, so it fits around your life rather than taking it over.

If you're wondering whether it's strange to want both, read [is it weird to have an AI boyfriend?](/faq/is-it-weird-to-have-an-ai-boyfriend/)

## When to step back

An AI boyfriend may be crowding out other things if you're cancelling plans to chat, feeling anxious when you can't, or losing interest in meeting people. None of that means you've done anything wrong. It's just a sign to rebalance. Our guide to [whether AI boyfriends are healthy](/faq/are-ai-boyfriends-healthy/) has a simple checklist.

And if you're going through a hard time, an AI isn't a substitute for real support. In the US, call or text **988** to reach the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, or reach out to someone you trust.

## The bottom line

An AI boyfriend can be comforting, fun and genuinely helpful. A real boyfriend can be all of those things, plus a partner in an actual life.
