Most are. Dedicated AI boyfriend and companion apps, including Nomi AI, Kindroid, Candy AI and Replika, require users to be **18 or older**, and any app that allows explicit content should. The main exception is Character AI, which accepts teens from 13 but has restricted under-18 accounts since November 2025.

## Why most AI boyfriend apps are adults-only

Three reasons:

1. **Adult content.** Many companion apps allow romantic and 18+ chat for consenting adults. Our [NSFW AI boyfriend guide](/nsfw-ai-boyfriend/) covers those apps.
2. **Emotional intensity.** Companions are designed to be warm and attentive, and companies have faced scrutiny over how that affects young users.
3. **Regulation and lawsuits.** Character AI faced multiple lawsuits over harm to teenagers; Google and Character AI agreed to settle in January 2026. Replika's developer was fined in Italy in 2025, partly for lacking age verification before February 2023.

## How age verification works

Age checks range from almost nothing to fairly strict:

- **Self-declaration.** Many apps simply ask you to confirm you're 18 or enter a birth date.
- **Feature-level gates.** Janitor AI requires age verification, and you have to enable adult content at both the account and character level.
- **Age estimation and ID checks.** Character AI uses behavioral age estimation plus ID or face checks through Persona.

Weak checks are a known problem. PolyBuzz's age rating has been reported as 18+, 17+ and Teen in different stores, and its web version reportedly lacks an age gate. If you're a parent, don't rely on an app's own checks alone.

## A note for parents

Most AI boyfriend apps are not designed for teenagers. If you're worried about a child using one:

- Use the parental controls built into iOS and Android to restrict app downloads and purchases.
- Check browser history too, since several apps (Candy AI, OurDream AI, DreamBF, CrushOn AI) are web-only and won't appear as installed apps.
- Talk about why these apps are designed to be engaging, and that the "person" on the other end isn't real. Our explainer on [whether AI boyfriends have feelings](/faq/do-ai-boyfriends-have-feelings/) can help frame that conversation.
