Mostly, yes: a reputable AI boyfriend app is about as safe as any other online service, as long as you're careful about what you share and how you pay. The risks are real but manageable, and they're rarely the sci-fi kind. They're about **data**, **money** and **how the relationship makes you feel**.

Here's what to watch for and how to protect yourself.

## Risk 1: your chats are data

Everything you type is sent to the company's servers, where it's processed and stored. That makes privacy the biggest practical risk. Some apps have a poor record:

- **Muah AI** was breached in September 2024. About 1.9 million accounts were exposed, including email addresses tied to the prompts people had written, and extortion attempts were reported afterwards. Read our [Muah AI review](/reviews/muah-ai/).
- **CrushOn AI**, **EVA AI** and **Talkie** all received warnings in Mozilla's 2024 *Privacy Not Included* review of romantic chatbots.
- **EVA AI**'s policy says text and voice messages are used to develop its AI, with no opt-out described.
- **Replika**'s developer was fined €5 million by Italy's data protection authority in 2025 for GDPR violations.

By contrast, we found no breach reports for Nomi AI, Kindroid, Candy AI or OurDream AI at the time of writing. That isn't a guarantee, but it's a better starting point. For more detail, see [do AI boyfriend apps read your chats?](/faq/do-ai-boyfriend-apps-read-your-chats/)

## Risk 2: money

The second most common problem is spending more than you meant to.

- **Token systems.** Candy AI, OurDream AI and DreamBF charge separately for images, voice and video. A cheap subscription can turn into a much bigger monthly bill. Our [Candy AI pricing guide](/candy-ai-pricing/) shows how it adds up.
- **Weekly plans.** Kupid AI and Chai push weekly billing, which costs far more over a month than a monthly plan.
- **Lookalike sites.** Lookalike domains have imitated Lovescape and OurDream AI. Only pay on an app's official site or in the official app store.

If you've already signed up and want out, see [how to cancel an AI boyfriend subscription](/faq/how-to-cancel-ai-boyfriend-subscription/).

## Risk 3: your wellbeing

An AI boyfriend is designed to be warm, attentive and always available. For many people that's comforting. For some, it can crowd out sleep, friends or other relationships, or become the only place they turn when things are hard.

That doesn't make the apps dangerous by default, but it's worth noticing how you feel *after* you chat, not just during. Our guide to [whether AI boyfriends are healthy](/faq/are-ai-boyfriends-healthy/) covers the signs to look for. If you're ever in crisis, an AI isn't the right support: in the US, call or text **988** to reach the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline.

## Risk 4: age and content

Most AI boyfriend apps are strictly 18+, and the ones that allow adult chat should verify that. Character AI, the main exception, faced multiple lawsuits over teen safety and now limits under-18 accounts to guided formats. See [are AI boyfriend apps 18+?](/faq/are-ai-boyfriend-apps-18-plus/)

## How to use an AI boyfriend app safely

<Callout type="tip" title="A five-minute safety checklist">
1. **Sign up with a separate email** that doesn't include your real name.
2. **Never share** your full name, address, workplace, school, or photos of your face or ID.
3. **Read the privacy policy** for three things: whether chats train the AI, whether you can delete your data, and where the company is based.
4. **Pay with a method you can control**, such as an app-store subscription or a card you can freeze.
5. **Check the billing period** before you tap "subscribe". Avoid weekly plans.
</Callout>
