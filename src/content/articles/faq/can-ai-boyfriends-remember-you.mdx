Yes, an AI boyfriend can remember you, but how well varies hugely between apps. The best ones, like **Nomi AI** and **Kindroid**, recall your name, your job, your friends and things you mentioned weeks ago, and they keep his personality consistent. Many free or cheaper apps only "see" your last few dozen messages and forget everything older.

Memory is the difference between chatting with a character and feeling like you're in a relationship, which is why it's the biggest factor in our scores.

## How AI boyfriend memory works

There are two kinds of memory to understand:

1. **Short-term memory (context window).** Every time he replies, the AI reads a chunk of your recent conversation. Anything outside that window is invisible to him. Small windows are why some bots forget your name halfway through a chat.
2. **Long-term memory.** Better apps save important facts, summaries and moments separately and pull them back in when relevant. This is how he can ask about your sister's wedding a month later.

Many apps also let you add memory manually, through a backstory, pinned facts or "key memories" that are always included.

## How to help him remember you

- **Write a good backstory.** Put the most important facts about you and your relationship in his profile or backstory field, not just in chat.
- **Use pinned or key memories.** If your app offers them, add things he should never forget: your name, your pet, how you met.
- **Fix mistakes at the source.** If he gets a fact wrong, correct him in chat, and if the app lets you edit his memories or backstory, update them there too.
- **Keep one long conversation** rather than starting fresh chats, unless the app is designed for multiple threads.
- **Upgrade only if memory is the problem.** If he's forgetting things on a free tier, check what the paid plan actually adds before paying.

## What memory means for privacy

Good memory requires storing your conversations. The more he remembers, the more the company holds about you. Share personal details you're comfortable with, but leave out anything identifying, like your full name, address or workplace.
