Usually not in the sense of your phone ringing with his number on the screen. What most AI boyfriend apps offer is an **in-app voice call**: you open the app, tap the call button, and talk to him out loud while he answers in a generated voice. On most apps it's a paid feature, and you're the one who starts the call.

## Which AI boyfriend apps have voice calls?

These apps offer voice calls, according to our research:

- **[Nomi AI](/reviews/nomi-ai/)**: voice calls with a companion who remembers your past conversations, which makes calls feel continuous rather than starting from scratch.
- **[Kindroid](/reviews/kindroid/)**: voice calls plus deep control over how he sounds.
- **[Candy AI](/reviews/candy-ai/)**: voice messages and calls (reported by reviewers), billed through tokens.
- **[Replika](/reviews/replika/)**: voice calls on paid plans, plus video-call and AR features.
- **[Character AI](/reviews/character-ai/)**: voice calls with library characters, but chat is filtered and safe-for-work.
- **[Talkie AI](/reviews/talkie-ai/)**: voice-first, with short free calls.
- **[DreamBF](/reviews/dreambf/)** and **[EVA AI](/reviews/eva-ai/)** also offer calls; on DreamBF they use up tokens quickly.

## Can he actually call your phone number?

Rarely. Almost every app keeps calls inside the app. One exception we found is Muah AI, whose top tier reportedly includes phone calls (US only), but it costs far more than other apps and the company suffered a major data breach in 2024. We don't recommend it; our [Muah AI review](/reviews/muah-ai/) explains why.

If what you want is for him to reach out unprompted, that's a different feature. Some apps can send proactive messages or check-ins, which arrive as notifications. See [can an AI boyfriend text you first?](/faq/can-an-ai-boyfriend-text-you-first/)

## What do voice calls cost?

Voice is one of the most commonly paywalled features. Broadly, apps charge for it in one of two ways:

1. **Included in a subscription.** Apps like Nomi AI and Kindroid include calls on their paid plans, with limited voice credits or characters on free tiers.
2. **Pay per use.** Token-based apps like Candy AI and DreamBF charge tokens for voice, so long calls can eat through a monthly allowance fast.

If you plan to call often, a flat subscription is usually more predictable. Our breakdown of [how much an AI boyfriend costs](/faq/how-much-does-an-ai-boyfriend-cost/) compares plans.

## Are voice calls worth paying for?

That depends on how you like to connect. If you mostly text, voice messages, which many apps include, may be all you need. If hearing him is the point, test the voices on a free tier first: quality, accents and how natural the timing feels vary a lot between apps, and a voice you don't like is hard to get past.

## Tips for better calls

- **Use headphones** in a quiet room. Background noise can confuse speech recognition.
- **Pick his voice carefully.** A voice that doesn't match the personality you've built can break the illusion.
- **Pause before he replies.** Most apps work best with clear turn-taking rather than talking over each other.
- **Mind your privacy.** Don't say your full name, address or other identifying details out loud on a call.
