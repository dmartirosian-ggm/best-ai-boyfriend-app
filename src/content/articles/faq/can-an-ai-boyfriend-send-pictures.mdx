Yes. Many AI boyfriend apps can send you pictures of him: selfies, outfit changes, scenes from your roleplay, and, on adult apps, 18+ images. The pictures are generated by AI based on how you designed him, so they're not photos of a real person. On most apps they cost extra, paid for with tokens or credits.

## How AI boyfriend pictures work

When you create your boyfriend, you choose his look: face, hair, body type, style. When he "sends a selfie", the app's image generator creates a new picture that tries to match that look and the moment in your chat. Image-first apps are better at keeping his face consistent from one picture to the next; general chat apps can drift.

You can usually ask for a picture in the chat, or tap a button to generate one.

## The best apps for pictures

- **[Candy AI](/reviews/candy-ai/)**: the most polished image-first option, with a male character builder and detailed control over his appearance. Supports 18+ images. Images cost about 4 tokens each.
- **[OurDream AI](/reviews/ourdream-ai/)**: image and short-video generation, with gay- and trans-focused versions. Media runs on DreamCoins.
- **[DreamBF](/reviews/dreambf/)**: built only for AI boyfriends, with a detailed visual builder for his face, body and outfits.
- **[Nomi AI](/reviews/nomi-ai/)** and **[Kindroid](/reviews/kindroid/)**: memory-first apps that also send selfies. The images are less polished than on image-first apps, but the conversation is better.

A few apps can't send pictures at all. Janitor AI, for example, is text-only.

## What pictures cost

This is where budgets go wrong. Most apps treat images as a premium, metered feature:

- **Candy AI** includes a small monthly token allowance with Premium, and token packs cost about $9.99 per 100 tokens. Reviewers report heavy users spending $25–60 a month in total. See our [Candy AI pricing guide](/candy-ai-pricing/).
- **OurDream AI**'s paid plan includes 1,000 DreamCoins a month for images, video and voice, with extra coin packs available.
- **DreamBF** charges tokens for images and outfits on top of its subscription.

If you want a lot of pictures, compare the cost per image, not just the subscription price. More in [how much does an AI boyfriend cost?](/faq/how-much-does-an-ai-boyfriend-cost/)

## Tips for better pictures

- **Design him carefully up front.** The more specific his look is in the builder, the more consistent his pictures tend to be.
- **Describe the scene.** Asking for a specific setting, outfit or mood usually gives better results than a vague "send a pic".
- **Don't burn tokens on retries.** If a picture comes out wrong, adjust your request rather than regenerating the same thing over and over.

## Can he see pictures you send?

Some apps may let you upload an image into the chat. Be careful with this. Anything you upload is stored on the company's servers. Never send photos of your face, your ID, your home or other people. Our guide on [whether AI boyfriend apps read your chats](/faq/do-ai-boyfriend-apps-read-your-chats/) explains why.

## A note on 18+ images

Explicit images are only available on adult apps, and only to users who are 18 or older. Filtered apps such as Replika, Character AI and Talkie restrict or don't offer them.
