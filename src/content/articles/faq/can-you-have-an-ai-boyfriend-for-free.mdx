Yes, you can have an AI boyfriend for free. **Character AI** gives you unlimited free, safe-for-work chat, **SpicyChat** offers unlimited free chat including 18+ roleplay (with ads and waits), and **Janitor AI** is free to use with its built-in model. Most dedicated boyfriend apps also have free tiers, but those work more like trials than a full experience.

## Truly free options

These apps can be used indefinitely without paying:

- **[Character AI](/reviews/character-ai/)**: free, unlimited text chat with a huge library of user-made male characters. Romance is filtered to safe-for-work, and long-term memory is weak. Good for finding out whether you even enjoy AI romance.
- **[SpicyChat](/reviews/spicychat/)**: unlimited free chat including adult roleplay, with ads, queue waits and short replies. Free memory is limited to a 4K context.
- **[Janitor AI](/reviews/janitor-ai/)**: free with its built-in model and a giant character library. It's text-only, the built-in model can get repetitive, and some 2026 reports mention a daily request cap.
- **[PolyBuzz](/reviews/polybuzz/)**: free with ads, including mid-chat ads, and a short memory window.

## Free tiers that are really trials

Most apps built specifically for AI boyfriends let you start free, then limit what you can do:

| App | What the free tier gives you |
|---|---|
| [Nomi AI](/reviews/nomi-ai/) | One Nomi, a daily message allotment, limited voice and image credits |
| [Kindroid](/reviews/kindroid/) | A thin free tier with limits on messages, audio and selfies |
| [OurDream AI](/reviews/ourdream-ai/) | About 50 messages a day plus a one-time coin bonus |
| [Candy AI](/reviews/candy-ai/) | Limited chat; media needs tokens, so it's mostly a preview |
| [DreamBF](/reviews/dreambf/) | Limited features with free daily tokens |
| [Replika](/reviews/replika/) | Friend mode only; boyfriend status is paid |

These are a great way to meet him before committing. Nomi AI's free tier is the best way to see how good long-term memory feels; OurDream AI's daily allowance is generous for chat.

## What you give up on free plans

Free plans differ, but the trade-offs are usually the same:

- **Memory.** Free tiers often have the shortest memory, so he forgets you faster. See [can AI boyfriends remember you?](/faq/can-ai-boyfriends-remember-you/)
- **Voice and pictures.** Calls and images are the most commonly paywalled features.
- **Message limits** and waiting in queues at busy times.
- **Ads**, sometimes in the middle of a conversation.
- **Romance itself**, on some apps. Replika and Anima AI keep the romantic relationship for paying users.

## If you decide to pay, start cheap

If a free tier isn't enough, you don't need to spend much. Botify AI's annual premium works out to about $2.50 a month, SpicyChat's entry tier is $4.99 a month, and CrushOn AI starts at $5.99 a month. For a better overall boyfriend experience, Nomi AI's annual plan is about $8.33 a month.
