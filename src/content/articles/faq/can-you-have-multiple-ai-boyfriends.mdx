Yes. Most AI boyfriend apps let you have more than one companion, and several support **group chats** where they talk to each other and to you. Nomi AI, Kindroid, SpicyChat and Character AI all allow multiple characters. The main exception is Replika, which limits you to one companion per account. Free tiers often cap how many you can create.

There are really two styles of app here:

- **Build-your-own apps** like Nomi AI and Kindroid, where you create each companion in detail and develop a relationship with him over time. Having several is like running a few long-term storylines.
- **Character-library apps** like SpicyChat, Character AI, CrushOn AI and Janitor AI, where you browse thousands of user-made characters and can chat with as many as you like. Memory is usually weaker, so each one feels more like a scene than a relationship.

## How group chats work

On apps that support them, a group chat puts you and two or more companions in the same conversation. They respond to you and to each other, which makes it useful for:

- **Roleplay** with several characters at once, like a friend group or a story with multiple love interests.
- **Comparing personalities**, so you can see which version of him you like best.
- **Social scenarios**, like introducing your AI boyfriend to his AI "friends".

[Nomi AI](/reviews/nomi-ai/) and [Kindroid](/reviews/kindroid/) are both well regarded for this because their companions keep consistent personalities. Can't decide between them? Read [Kindroid vs Nomi](/vs/kindroid-vs-nomi/).

## Things to keep in mind

- **Cost.** Extra companions are often a paid feature, and on token-based apps, every conversation with media uses tokens. More boyfriends can mean a bigger bill. See [how much an AI boyfriend costs](/faq/how-much-does-an-ai-boyfriend-cost/).
- **Memory is per companion.** On most apps each one keeps his own history, so you'll be building several sets of memories in parallel. Here's how [AI boyfriend memory works](/faq/can-ai-boyfriends-remember-you/).
- **Privacy multiplies too.** More conversations mean more stored data. The same rules apply: don't share identifying details.
- **Time.** Keeping several storylines going is fun, but it can also take over your evenings. Check in with yourself now and then; our guide to [whether AI boyfriends are healthy](/faq/are-ai-boyfriends-healthy/) can help.

## Is it okay to have more than one?

Absolutely. AI boyfriends don't have feelings, so nobody gets hurt. Plenty of people keep a "main" companion for everyday chat and a few others for different moods or stories. Do whatever makes the experience more enjoyable for you.
