Most AI boyfriend apps cost **$10–20 a month**, or roughly **$4–10 a month** if you pay for a year up front. Many have a free tier, and a few are free to use indefinitely. The catch is that several popular apps charge separately for images, voice and video through tokens, so what you actually spend can be well above the headline price.

## The hidden costs

### Tokens and credits

Candy AI, OurDream AI, DreamBF, Joi AI and Lovescape use tokens or credits for media. A subscription might include a small allowance, but frequent pictures, calls or videos mean buying more. On Candy AI, token packs cost about $9.99 per 100 tokens and an image costs about 4 tokens; reviewers report heavy users spending $25–60 a month. Our [Candy AI pricing breakdown](/candy-ai-pricing/) runs the numbers.

### Add-ons and tiers

Some apps sell upgrades on top of the base plan. Kindroid's Ultra add-on is +$24.99 a month on the web, and its Max add-on is +$59.99. SpicyChat's best memory is on its top tier, and PolyBuzz's permanent memory requires its $29.90 plan.

### Weekly billing

Watch for weekly plans. Kupid AI's weekly plan is $12.99 a week, which works out to about $52 a month, far more than its monthly option. Chai has also reportedly shifted to weekly billing.

### App store markups

Subscribing through the App Store or Google Play can cost more than paying on the website, as with Kindroid. Replika's prices are in-app only and vary by region.

## How to get the best value

1. **Start free.** Use the free tier for a week to see whether you like his personality and memory. Our guide to [getting an AI boyfriend for free](/faq/can-you-have-an-ai-boyfriend-for-free/) lists the best options.
2. **Pick the plan type that fits your use.** If you mostly chat, a flat subscription beats a token app. If you mostly want pictures, budget for tokens.
3. **Go annual only once you're sure.** The savings are real, but refunds are often hard to get.
4. **Set a monthly limit** for token purchases and stick to it.
5. **Know how to leave.** Read [how to cancel an AI boyfriend subscription](/faq/how-to-cancel-ai-boyfriend-subscription/) before you sign up.
