Cancel where you paid. If you subscribed inside an iPhone or Android app, cancel through the **App Store** or **Google Play**, not the app itself. If you paid on the company's **website**, cancel in your account or billing settings on that site. Deleting the app does not stop billing.

Not sure where you paid? Search your email for the receipt. An Apple or Google receipt means an app-store subscription; a receipt from the company or a payment processor means a web subscription.

## How to cancel on iPhone or iPad (App Store)

1. Open the **Settings** app.
2. Tap **your name** at the top.
3. Tap **Subscriptions**.
4. Choose the AI boyfriend app.
5. Tap **Cancel Subscription** and confirm.

You keep access until the end of the current billing period. If you don't see the app in the list, you probably subscribed on the web or with a different Apple account.

## How to cancel on Android (Google Play)

1. Open the **Google Play Store** app.
2. Tap your **profile icon** in the top right.
3. Tap **Payments & subscriptions**, then **Subscriptions**.
4. Choose the AI boyfriend app.
5. Tap **Cancel subscription** and follow the prompts.

Make sure you're signed in with the Google account you used to subscribe.

## How to cancel a web subscription

Several popular apps, including Candy AI, OurDream AI, DreamBF and CrushOn AI, are web-only, so you'll have subscribed on their site. Menus differ by app and change often, but the general process is:

1. **Log in** to the app's website on a computer or phone browser.
2. Look for **Account**, **Settings**, **Billing**, **Subscription** or **Plan**, usually under your profile picture.
3. Choose **Cancel** or **Turn off auto-renew** and confirm.
4. **Keep the confirmation email** or take a screenshot.

If you can't find a cancel option:

- **Contact support** by email or the help center, and ask for written confirmation that renewal is off.
- **Check your payment method.** If you paid with PayPal, you can cancel the recurring payment from PayPal's automatic payments settings. Some card issuers and banking apps also let you block a recurring merchant.
- **As a last resort**, contact your card issuer about the charge. Do this only after you've tried the company, and keep records of your attempts.

## Before you cancel

- **Use up your tokens.** Token or coin balances may not survive cancellation. Check the terms.
- **Check for annual renewal dates.** If you're on a yearly plan, cancel well before it renews.
- **Watch for weekly plans.** Apps like Kupid AI and Chai offer weekly billing, which can renew several times before you notice.
- **Decide what to do with your data.** Cancelling stops payment, but it doesn't delete your chats. To remove them, delete your account through the app or website, or contact the company.
