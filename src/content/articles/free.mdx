## Free, freemium or trial? How to tell

Before the list, here's how we grouped the apps. It will save you from downloading something that asks for a card five messages in.

- **Truly free:** you can chat every day without paying, indefinitely. Expect ads, waits, a filter or short memory. Character AI, SpicyChat and Janitor AI fit here.
- **Generous free tier:** a daily allowance that's enough for a real conversation. OurDream AI (about 50 messages a day) and Talkie (about 50 messages a day) are the main examples.
- **Free trial in disguise:** a free account exists, but the good parts (memory, romance, images, voice) are paid. Candy AI's free plan is mostly a preview, Replika's free plan is friend-mode only, and Chai is close to a hard paywall.

Nomi and Kindroid sit between the last two groups: their free tiers are real but thin, and they're there to show you what you'd get by subscribing.

## AI boyfriend with no sign up: does it exist?

Searches for "AI boyfriend no sign up" and "AI boyfriend free no sign up" are growing fast, and we understand why: nobody wants to hand an email address to a romance app on a whim.

The honest answer is that none of the apps we recommend let you have a full conversation without an account. The 18+ apps need one to check your age, and every app needs one to save your boyfriend and your chat history. Sites that promise "no sign up" usually ask for an account after a few messages, and anonymous sites with no visible company behind them deserve extra caution.

A safer way to stay private:

1. **Use a separate email** that doesn't include your real name.
2. **Skip "Sign in with Google/Apple"** if you don't want the account tied to your main identity.
3. **Don't share identifying details** in chat: your full name, address, workplace or photos of yourself.
4. **Avoid apps with a breach history.** Muah AI exposed about 1.9 million accounts in 2024, including email addresses linked to what people typed.

## Free AI boyfriend app for Android

If you want a native Android app with a usable free plan, your best choices are:

- **Talkie** – about 50 messages a day and short free voice calls.

Web-only apps (OurDream AI, Candy AI, CrushOn AI, DreamBF) aren't in the Play Store, but they run in Chrome on your phone.

## Free AI boyfriend app for iPhone

On iPhone, the strongest free picks are:

- **Character AI** – the most complete free experience, SFW only.
- **SpicyChat** – free with ads, queue waits and short replies.
- **Talkie Lab** – Talkie's name on the US App Store since 2025.
- **Nomi AI** and **Kindroid** – free tiers to try before subscribing. With Kindroid, subscribe on the web rather than in the App Store; it's cheaper.

For web-only apps, open the site in Safari, tap Share and choose **Add to Home Screen**. It then behaves much like an app.

## Is a free AI boyfriend good enough?

For casual chat, yes. If you want a light, flirty conversation now and then, Character AI or SpicyChat costs nothing and does the job.

Where free plans fall down is **memory**. A boyfriend who forgets your name, your job or last week's conversation stops feeling like a boyfriend quickly, and long-term memory is the most expensive feature to run. That's why the best free tiers have the weakest memory, and why the apps with the best memory (Nomi and Kindroid) keep their free plans tight.
