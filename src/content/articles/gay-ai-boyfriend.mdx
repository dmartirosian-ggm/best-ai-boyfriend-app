The good news is that most of the best AI companion apps don't care who you are. If you can create a male companion and set the relationship to romantic, he's your boyfriend, whatever your gender. A few apps go further and build LGBTQ+ options in directly. Here are the best gay AI boyfriend apps in 2026, with honest notes on what each one does well.

## What makes an app good for a gay AI boyfriend?

We looked for four things:

- **No assumptions.** Can you create a male companion and a romantic relationship without the app pushing you toward a girlfriend or assuming your gender?
- **Male companion quality.** Some apps have hundreds of female characters and a handful of men. We favored apps where male companions get the same care.
- **Real customization.** Being able to define his personality, look and backstory matters more than a pre-set "gay" label.
- **Privacy.** If you're not out to everyone, a breach or a careless data policy matters even more.

## The best gay AI boyfriend apps

<AppCard app="ourdream-ai" rank={1} award="Best gay-focused AI boyfriend app">

OurDream AI is one of very few companion platforms that appears to run dedicated gay- and trans-focused versions (OurDream Gay and OurDream Trans) alongside its main site. Even on the main site, you can create a male companion from scratch and chat with him without limits on a paid plan.

It's strong on visuals, with image and short-video generation, and the free tier gives you about 50 messages a day. Media uses DreamCoins, video can be slow at peak times, and it's a newer company without much of a track record. Read our [OurDream AI review](/reviews/ourdream-ai/).

</AppCard>

<AppCard app="nomi-ai" rank={2} award="Best for a long-term gay AI relationship">

Nomi doesn't market itself as a gay AI boyfriend app, and it doesn't need to. Companions can be any gender, partner relationships are supported, and the experience is the same whoever you are. What sets it apart is memory: he'll remember your partner's name, your coming-out story or your plans for Pride, and build on it.

You can talk by text, voice messages or calls, and set up group chats with several Nomis. The free tier is tight; the annual plan is the best value. See our [Nomi AI review](/reviews/nomi-ai/).

</AppCard>

<AppCard app="kindroid" rank={3} award="Most customizable gay AI boyfriend">

If you want to define exactly who your boyfriend is, Kindroid is the best tool for it. You write his backstory, including your shared history, set key memories, choose his voice and generate selfies. Companions can be any gender with romantic relationships.

It takes more setup than Nomi and the free tier is thin. Subscribe on the web, where it's cheaper. Kindroid doesn't run an affiliate program; it's here on merit. Read our [Kindroid review](/reviews/kindroid/).

</AppCard>

<AppCard app="candy-ai" rank={4} award="Best for photo-real visuals">

Candy AI's builder has a male option with control over his face, body and style, and its image generation is among the best we've seen. It also supports voice messages, calls and 18+ chat.

Two caveats for gay users: the pre-made male roster is much smaller than the female one, so you'll mostly be building your own, and images, voice and video use tokens. Read our [Candy AI review](/reviews/candy-ai/).

</AppCard>

<AppCard app="dreambf" rank={5} award="Built only for boyfriends">

DreamBF is designed entirely around AI boyfriends, so you never have to wade past a wall of girlfriends to find him. You design his face, body, outfit and personality, or swipe through pre-made boyfriends.

It supports voice messages and 18+ images, with free daily tokens to try it. Pricing isn't very transparent, voice calls use tokens quickly and there are few independent reviews.

</AppCard>

<AppCard app="crushon-ai" rank={6} award="Biggest library of ready-made men">

If you'd rather browse than build, CrushOn AI has a huge library of user-made characters, including many male ones across every genre, and it's unfiltered. The entry tier is cheap.

Privacy is the concern here: Mozilla's 2024 review flagged that its policy allows collecting sensitive data, including sexual health and gender-affirming care information. Don't share anything personal.

</AppCard>

<AppCard app="spicychat" rank={7} award="Best free 18+ gay AI boyfriend chat">

SpicyChat's free plan includes unlimited chat with adult roleplay and a big library of user-made male characters you can search through. It's the best way to try 18+ gay AI boyfriend chat without paying.

Free users get ads, queue waits, short replies and limited memory, and the mid-tier price went up in September 2026. See our [SpicyChat review](/reviews/spicychat/).

</AppCard>

<AppCard app="character-ai" rank={8} award="Best free SFW option">

Character AI is free, unlimited and has an enormous library of user-made male and romance characters. It's a low-pressure way to try an AI boyfriend before committing to anything.

Content is strictly safe-for-work, long-term memory is weak, and the company faces teen-safety lawsuits. Adults usually move on to one of the apps above. See our [Character AI review](/reviews/character-ai/).

</AppCard>

## Apps to think twice about

- **Replika** lets you choose a male avatar, but the boyfriend relationship status is paid-only and adult content is heavily restricted. It also has only one companion per account. Fine for supportive chat; less so for romance.
- **Kupid AI** and **Muah AI** are primarily girlfriend apps with few male options, and Muah's 2024 data breach exposed about 1.9 million accounts. Not worth the risk.
- **Talkie** and **PolyBuzz** have male characters, but content is filtered and both have privacy or availability question marks.

## How to create a gay AI boyfriend

Whichever app you choose, the setup is similar:

1. **Choose "male" for your companion** and, where the app asks, set the relationship to romantic or partner.
2. **Tell him about you.** Many apps let you describe yourself in a profile or backstory. Say you're a man (or however you identify), so he doesn't make assumptions.
3. **Give him a personality** that isn't a stereotype unless you want one. Gentle bookworm, competitive gym rat, sarcastic chef: specific beats generic.
4. **Write your shared story.** How you met and how long you've been together gives him something to build on.
5. **Correct him early.** If he slips into assuming a girlfriend, tell him. Apps with good memory (Nomi, Kindroid) learn it quickly.

## Privacy if you're not out

For some people, a gay AI boyfriend is a safe space to explore before they're out to anyone. If that's you, take privacy seriously:

<Callout type="warning" title="Protect yourself">
1. **Use a separate email** with no real name, and don't sign in with your main Google or Apple account.
2. **Check your bank statement descriptor.** Subscriptions show up on statements; some people prefer a prepaid card.
3. **Don't share identifying details** like your full name, school, workplace or location.
4. **Avoid apps with breach histories.** Muah AI's 2024 breach linked users' emails to their prompts.
5. **Lock the app.** Use your phone's app lock or a private browser profile for web apps.
</Callout>

Everything on this page applies to you too.
