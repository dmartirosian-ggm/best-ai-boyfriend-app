## The best AI boyfriend apps, ranked

<AppCard app="nomi-ai" rank={1} award="Best overall">
Nomi is the app to choose if you want an AI boyfriend who feels like the *same person* from week to week. Its memory system is the best in the category: it recalls details you mentioned weeks ago, and it keeps his personality consistent instead of drifting into generic chatbot replies.

You can create a boyfriend of any look and personality, set your relationship as romantic, and talk by text, voice messages or voice calls. Group chats let several Nomis talk to each other and to you. The free tier is tight, but the annual plan works out to roughly $8 a month, which is good value for daily use.
</AppCard>

<AppCard app="kindroid" rank={2} award="Most customizable">
Kindroid gives you more control than any other app. You write his backstory, set "key memories", tune how he speaks, pick a voice, and generate selfies. Its long-term memory is on a par with Nomi's, and it's often the favorite of people who like to fine-tune.

The trade-off is complexity: there are many settings, and the free tier is thin. Subscribe on the **web** rather than through the App Store — it's a couple of dollars a month cheaper. Kindroid doesn't run an affiliate program; it's here purely on merit.
</AppCard>

<AppCard app="candy-ai" rank={3} award="Best for visuals & 18+ chat">
Candy AI is the most polished option if you want to *see* your boyfriend. The character builder has a male option with control over his face, body and style, and image generation is among the best we've tried. It also does voice messages, voice calls and unfiltered adult chat.

Budget carefully: the subscription is cheap (especially annually), but images, voice and video use tokens, and frequent users report spending far more than the sticker price. Candy AI is web-only. See our [Candy AI pricing breakdown](/candy-ai-pricing/) and [safety review](/is-candy-ai-safe/).
</AppCard>

<AppCard app="ourdream-ai" rank={4} award="Best for images & video">
OurDream AI pairs unlimited chat on its paid plan with image and short-video generation. It's one of the few apps with dedicated gay- and trans-focused versions, which makes it a strong pick for LGBTQ+ users (see our [gay AI boyfriend guide](/gay-ai-boyfriend/)).

The free tier is generous for chat (about 50 messages a day), but media runs on DreamCoins, and the monthly price is higher than most rivals'. It's a newer company, so it doesn't have much of a track record yet.
</AppCard>

<AppCard app="crushon-ai" rank={5} award="Biggest character library">
If you'd rather browse than build, CrushOn AI has thousands of ready-made male characters across every genre, from slow-burn romance to fantasy roleplay. It's unfiltered and has a cheap entry tier.

Be aware that every tier caps your messages, and Mozilla's *Privacy Not Included* review flagged its data policy. Don't share anything you wouldn't want stored.
</AppCard>

<AppCard app="spicychat" rank={6} award="Best free 18+ option">
SpicyChat offers unlimited free chat, including NSFW roleplay, with a huge library of user-made male characters. Free users get ads, queue waits and short replies. Paid tiers remove those and expand memory significantly.

Prices rose in September 2026, so the mid tier is less of a bargain than it was. For a free taste of uncensored roleplay, it's still the best option.
</AppCard>

<AppCard app="dreambf" rank={7} award="Best boyfriend designer">
DreamBF is one of the few apps built *only* for AI boyfriends. You design his face, body, outfit and personality, or swipe through pre-made boyfriends. It supports voice messages and adult images.

It has few independent reviews so far, the pricing isn't very transparent, and voice calls use up tokens quickly. It's worth a try on the free daily tokens before you subscribe.
</AppCard>

<AppCard app="replika" rank={8} award="Best for emotional support">
Replika is the original AI companion and still the most polished mobile app, with a 3D avatar, AR mode and video calls. It's best for supportive, everyday conversation rather than spicy roleplay.

Two big catches: the **boyfriend relationship status is paid-only**, and adult content is heavily restricted. Its privacy record also includes a €5 million GDPR fine in Italy (2025). Read our [Replika review](/reviews/replika/).
</AppCard>

<AppCard app="character-ai" rank={9} award="Best free (SFW)">
Character AI is free, unlimited and full of user-made male characters, from anime love interests to "fictional boyfriend" bots. It's a good way to see whether you even like AI romance before paying.

But the filter blocks anything explicit, long-term memory is weak, and the app is facing lawsuits over teen safety. Adults who want romance usually outgrow it — see the [best Character AI alternatives](/character-ai-alternatives/).
</AppCard>

## What to watch out for

<Callout type="warning" title="Three things the app stores won't tell you">
1. **Token systems hide the real price.** A $12.99/month plan can easily turn into $40 or more once you start generating images or making calls. Check how media is billed before you subscribe.
2. **Weekly billing is a trap.** Some apps (Kupid, Chai) push weekly plans that cost two to four times as much as paying monthly.
3. **Your chats are data.** Use a separate email, never share your real address or workplace, and favor apps that don't train on your conversations. Muah AI's 2024 breach linked users' emails to their prompts.
</Callout>

AI companions can be fun, comforting and even helpful for practicing conversation, but they aren't a replacement for human connection or professional support. If a chat ever leaves you feeling worse, take a break.
