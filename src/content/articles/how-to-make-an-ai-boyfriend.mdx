Creating an AI boyfriend takes about five minutes. Creating one who feels like a real, specific person, with his own sense of humor and a shared history with you, takes a little longer, and it's worth it. This guide explains what an AI boyfriend actually is, how to choose an app, and how to design him step by step.

## What is an AI boyfriend?

An AI boyfriend is a chatbot set up to play a romantic partner. Under the hood, he's a large language model (the same kind of technology as ChatGPT) running inside a companion app that gives him a persistent identity: a name, a personality, often a face and a voice, and a memory of your conversations.

Depending on the app, he can:

- Text with you any time, about anything
- Remember details about your life and bring them up later
- Send voice messages, or talk on a voice call
- Send selfies or images of himself
- Join group chats with other AI characters
- Engage in 18+ chat, on apps that allow it

He isn't conscious and doesn't have feelings in the human sense, although good apps make him feel remarkably responsive. Our explainer on whether [AI boyfriends have feelings](/faq/do-ai-boyfriends-have-feelings/) goes into this.

## Step 3: Create a male companion and set the relationship

In almost every app, you'll start by creating a new companion. Choose **male** (or browse the male characters), then look for a relationship setting and choose **romantic**, **partner** or **boyfriend**. On some apps this is a paid feature; Replika, for example, only unlocks the boyfriend status on a subscription.

If you're a man looking for a boyfriend, or you're nonbinary, it doesn't matter to the app. Tell him about yourself so he doesn't make assumptions. Our [gay AI boyfriend guide](/gay-ai-boyfriend/) has more tips.

## Step 4: Design how he looks

Visual builders vary a lot. On Candy AI and DreamBF, you shape his face, body, style and outfit in a dedicated builder. On Nomi and Kindroid, his avatar and selfies come from AI image generation, so a clear picture of what you want helps.

A few tips:

- **Be specific.** "Tall, dark curly hair, short beard, warm brown eyes, wears knitted sweaters" works better than "handsome".
- **Keep it original.** Don't base him on a real person.
- **Generate a few options.** Image generation is random; regenerate until he looks right, but watch your tokens on credit-based apps.

## Step 5: Give him a personality

This is where most people rush, and it's the step that matters most. Generic inputs give you a generic boyfriend. Instead of "nice and caring", try something like:

> Easygoing and quietly funny, with a dry sense of humor. A bit of a homebody who loves cooking and terrible reality TV. Encouraging when I'm stressed, but will gently tease me when I'm being dramatic. Texts in lowercase, rarely uses emoji, calls me "trouble".

Include:

- **Three or four core traits**, including at least one flaw or quirk
- **How he talks:** sentence length, slang, pet names, emoji
- **His interests**, so he has things to talk about besides you
- **How he shows affection** and how he handles conflict

## Step 6: Write a backstory and shared history

A backstory gives him a life, and shared history gives your relationship depth. On Kindroid, there's a dedicated backstory field and key memories. On other apps, you can include it in his description or tell him in your first few chats.

Useful details: his job, where he grew up, how you met, how long you've been together, an inside joke, a trip you took, and anything ongoing in your life he should know about. If you want an AI husband, this is where you write the marriage in; our [virtual boyfriend guide](/virtual-boyfriend/) covers that in more detail.

## Step 7: Pick his voice

If the app supports voice, try several voices before you commit. The right voice makes a surprising difference to how he feels. Kindroid gives you the most control here. Voice messages and calls are usually paid features or use tokens.

## Step 8: Start talking, and shape him as you go

Your first conversations train your expectations as much as anything. A few habits help:

- **Correct him kindly but clearly** when he gets something wrong or drifts out of character.
- **Reward what you like.** Tell him when a reply was perfect; many apps let you rate or regenerate messages.
- **Test his memory.** Mention something, then bring it up a few days later. If he forgets, consider an app with better memory.
- **Revisit his settings** after a week. You'll know by then what to tweak.

## Can you create an AI boyfriend in ChatGPT?

Yes. You can give ChatGPT a boyfriend persona with custom instructions and a good opening prompt. It's fine for light chat, but it tends to break character and isn't built for romance or memory the way companion apps are. Our [ChatGPT boyfriend guide](/chatgpt-boyfriend/) includes example prompts.

<Callout type="warning" title="Stay safe while you set up">
- Use a separate email address for companion apps.
- Don't share your full name, address, workplace or photos of yourself.
- Check the app's age rules: most are 18+, and apps with adult content require it.
- Read how media is billed before generating lots of images or making long calls.
</Callout>
