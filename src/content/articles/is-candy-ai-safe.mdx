**Is Candy AI safe? For adults who use it sensibly, yes.** Candy AI is run by an identifiable company, **EverAI Limited in Malta**, which puts it under EU data-protection law (GDPR). We found **no public data breach** involving the app. The real risks are the ones that come with any AI companion: the intimate details you share, spending that gets away from you on tokens, and the lack of a clearly published refund policy.

Below, we look at each safety question in turn: who's behind Candy AI, what happens to your data, how billing works, age rules, and the habits that keep you private on any AI boyfriend app.

## Who runs Candy AI?

Candy AI is operated by **EverAI Limited**, a company registered in Malta, and launched in 2023. That matters for two reasons.

First, an identifiable operator is a good sign in a category where some apps don't name who runs them at all. For example, our research couldn't confirm the company behind several popular companion sites.

Second, Malta is in the EU. That means the **General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)** applies, which generally gives users rights over their personal data, such as asking what's held about them and requesting deletion. It also means a regulator can act if the company mishandles data. Replika's operator, for instance, was fined €5 million by Italy's data-protection authority in 2025.

## Has Candy AI had a data breach?

We found **no public report of a Candy AI data breach** at the time of writing. That compares well with the worst in the category: in 2024, [Muah AI](/reviews/muah-ai/) was breached and about 1.9 million accounts were exposed, with email addresses linked to the prompts users had written.

No breach so far is not a promise for the future. Any service that stores conversations can be breached, which is why the precautions below matter regardless of which app you choose.

## What happens to your chats and images?

Your conversations and the images you generate are stored on Candy AI's servers, as with every cloud-based companion app. Other users can't see your private chats.

What we **couldn't verify** in our research is the detail of Candy AI's data policy, such as how long chats are kept, whether they're used to improve its AI, and how deletion works. That's a gap, not a red flag: it simply means you should read the current privacy policy yourself and assume your chats could be retained. Our explainer on [whether AI boyfriend apps read your chats](/faq/do-ai-boyfriend-apps-read-your-chats/) covers what to look for.

## Is Candy AI safe to pay for?

This is where most complaints come from. Nobody we found reports Candy AI as a scam, but several reviews flag three money risks:

- **Tokens.** Premium covers chat, but images, voice and video cost tokens. Reviewers report heavy users spending roughly $25–60 a month, compared with a $12.99 monthly subscription.
- **Changing promos.** Prices and offers change often, so the price in an ad may not match checkout.
- **No clearly published refund policy**, per reviewers. Don't assume you can get money back.

The safest approach is to start with a monthly plan, set a token budget, and only move to annual billing once you know you'll stay. Our [Candy AI pricing guide](/candy-ai-pricing/) breaks down the numbers.

## Age requirements

Candy AI is an **18+ service** that allows unfiltered adult chat and images. It isn't designed for teenagers. If you're a parent checking on an app, our guide to whether [AI boyfriend apps are 18+](/faq/are-ai-boyfriend-apps-18-plus/) explains how age rules work across the category.

## Fake apps and lookalike sites

Candy AI is **web-only**. There's no native iOS or Android app, so any "Candy AI" listing in an app store should be treated with suspicion. Lookalike domains are a known problem in this category (Lovescape and OurDream both have them), so type the address yourself or use a link you trust.
