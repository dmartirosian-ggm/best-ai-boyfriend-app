Janitor AI earned its fans for three reasons: it's free, the character library is massive, and adult roleplay isn't filtered on the web. If you've spent time there with a favorite male character, you also know the downsides. The built-in model forgets, the site has uptime issues, there's no voice or images, and getting good replies can mean fiddling with API keys.

This guide ranks the best Janitor AI alternatives for people who want an **AI boyfriend**, whether that's a ready-made character to roleplay with or a companion who stays the same person every day.

## Why people look for a Janitor AI alternative

**Short memory and repetition.** Janitor AI's free built-in model, JLLM, has a short context. Reviewers note it starts repeating itself after 20 to 30 messages, which is hard to ignore in a long romance plot.

**The API-key workaround is technical.** You can connect your own model for better replies, but it means setting up an account with a model provider and paying per token, which can run anywhere from a few dollars to $15 to $50 a month depending on the model. Your chats also go to that provider.

**Text only.** There are no voice messages, no voice calls and no images. If you want to hear him or see him, you need a different app.

**Reliability and changing limits.** Reviewers report JLLM quality and uptime issues. Some 2026 sources mention a cap of about 50 requests a day on the free model, and a paid tier reportedly launched in June 2026, though reports on its name and price conflict.

**Mobile restrictions.** Adult content is not available in Janitor AI's mobile apps; it's web-only.

### What you give up by leaving

To be fair to Janitor AI: no single alternative matches it on every point. It's free to start, the library is enormous, and bringing your own model means you can pick exactly how smart (and how expensive) your chats are. Most alternatives below either charge for the features you care about or cap how much you can chat. The question is which trade-off you mind least: money, message limits, or memory.

## A Janitor AI alternative for every budget

- **Free:** SpicyChat (unlimited, with ads and queues) or OurDream AI's free tier (about 50 messages a day). Character AI is free too, but it filters romance.
- **Under $10 a month:** CrushOn AI's Standard tier starts at $5.99/mo for about 2,000 messages. SpicyChat's entry tier removes ads and queues for about $5.
- **Around $8 to $16 a month:** Nomi AI ($15.99/mo, or about $8.33/mo billed yearly) and Kindroid (from $13.99/mo on the web) are where memory gets genuinely good.
- **Media on top:** Candy AI, OurDream AI and DreamBF have reasonable subscriptions, but images, voice and video use tokens or coins, so your real bill depends on how much you generate.

## How to move a character over

Janitor AI doesn't sync with other apps, but your character's personality is just text.

1. **Copy his card.** Save his description, personality, scenario and first message.
2. **Paste it into the new app.** CrushOn AI and SpicyChat have character creators that take similar fields. In Nomi or Kindroid, use the backstory and memory fields.
3. **Tell him the key facts early.** Names, how you met, ongoing storylines. Memory-focused apps will keep them.
4. **Test on the free tier first,** and check message caps and token costs before you commit.
