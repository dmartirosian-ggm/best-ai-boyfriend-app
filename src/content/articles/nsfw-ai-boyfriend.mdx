## Age verification

Every spicy AI boyfriend app requires you to be 18 or older, but how seriously they check varies. Janitor AI requires age verification before you can turn adult content on. Character AI, which doesn't allow explicit content at all, uses behavioral age estimation plus ID and face checks to keep under-18 users out of open-ended chat.

Weak age gates have caused real problems in this industry. Replika's €5 million GDPR fine in Italy cited a lack of age verification before February 2023, and in 2025 MIT Technology Review found Botify AI hosting sexually charged bots of underage celebrity characters. Stricter checks can feel like friction, but they're a sign an app takes safety seriously. More in [are AI boyfriend apps 18+](/faq/are-ai-boyfriend-apps-18-plus/).

## Privacy: the part that really matters

Spicy chats are the most sensitive data you can give a company. If an app is breached, what leaks isn't just an email address; it's what you wrote, linked to who you are. That's exactly what happened with **Muah AI** in 2024: about 1.9 million accounts were exposed, email addresses were tied to users' prompts, and extortion attempts followed. We don't recommend Muah AI.

Other red flags we found in our research:

- **CrushOn AI's** privacy policy allows collecting sensitive information, according to Mozilla.
- **EVA AI's** policy says text and voice messages are used to develop its AI, with no opt-out described.
- **Lookalike scam sites** exist for brands like OurDream AI and Lovescape. Always type the official address.

<Callout type="warning" title="Protect yourself before you get spicy">
1. **Use a separate email** with no real name, and don't sign in with your main Google or Apple account.
2. **Never share identifying details:** your full name, address, workplace, or photos of your face.
3. **Don't upload intimate photos of yourself.** You can't control where they end up.
4. **Prefer apps with clean records** and an identifiable company behind them.
5. **Check billing descriptors** if you share a bank account or card.
</Callout>

## The real cost of a spicy AI boyfriend

Adult-oriented apps are more likely to use token or coin systems, and that's where costs balloon. A cheap Candy AI subscription can turn into a much bigger monthly bill once you start generating images or making calls; reviewers report heavy users spending several times the sticker price. OurDream AI and DreamBF work similarly with coins and tokens.

If you mainly want chat, a flat subscription like Nomi or Kindroid is usually cheaper in practice. If you want images, set a monthly budget before you start. Our guide to [how much an AI boyfriend costs](/faq/how-much-does-an-ai-boyfriend-cost/) has the full comparison.
