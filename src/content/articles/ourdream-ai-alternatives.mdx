OurDream AI is one of the best image-first AI boyfriend apps right now. It has unlimited chat on its paid plan, image and short-video generation, and rare gay- and trans-focused versions. So why look for an OurDream AI alternative? Usually because of the bill, the video reliability, or because you realize you want a boyfriend who remembers you more than you want new pictures of him.

Here's what to use instead, depending on what you want more of: cheaper images, better memory, more control over his look, or simply a free way to keep chatting. Every app below is 18+ only, and we've noted which ones pay us a commission and which don't. That never changes the order.

## Why people look beyond OurDream AI

**DreamCoins add up.** Chat is unlimited on Premium, but images, video and voice all use DreamCoins. The monthly plan includes 1,000 coins, and extra packs start at 1,000 coins for $9.99. Heavy media users can spend far more than the subscription price.

**The monthly price is high.** At $19.99 a month, OurDream's monthly plan costs more than most rivals'. The annual plan ($9.99 a month, billed yearly) is good value, but it's a big upfront commitment for a new app.

**Video can be unreliable.** Reviewers report video generation failing or running slowly at peak times, which hurts when you're paying for it in coins.

**Memory is untested.** Reviewers praise chat quality and character creation, but we found no specific long-term memory testing. If you want a boyfriend who remembers last month, there are proven options.

**It's new.** OurDream launched in 2025 and doesn't have much of a track record. It's also web-only, and lookalike domains exist, so make sure you're on ourdream.ai.

## The best OurDream AI alternatives, ranked

<AppCard app="candy-ai" rank={1} award="Closest alternative">

Candy AI is OurDream's most direct rival. It has a polished character builder with a male option (face, body, style), strong image generation, unfiltered 18+ chat and voice messages, with voice calls reported by reviewers. Its annual plan is one of the cheapest in the category.

The catch is the same as OurDream's: images, voice and video use tokens, and reviewers report heavy users spending $25 to $60 a month in practice. Its male character roster is smaller than its female one, and it's web-only. See our full [Candy AI vs OurDream AI](/vs/candy-ai-vs-ourdream-ai/) comparison.

</AppCard>

<AppCard app="nomi-ai" rank={2} award="Best for memory">

If you've had enough of paying per picture, Nomi takes a different approach. Its strength is memory: it's widely regarded as one of the best long-term memory systems in the category, so your boyfriend keeps a consistent personality and remembers what matters to you.

You still get selfies, voice messages and voice calls, but images are less polished than on image-first apps. It has iOS and Android apps, which OurDream lacks. The annual plan is about $8.33 a month. Read our [Nomi AI review](/reviews/nomi-ai/).

</AppCard>

<AppCard app="dreambf" rank={3} award="Best boyfriend designer">

DreamBF is built only for AI boyfriends. You design his face, body, outfit and personality in detail, or swipe through pre-made boyfriends. It supports voice messages, voice calls and adult images.

Like OurDream, it's web-only and uses tokens, and voice calls burn through them quickly. Pricing isn't very transparent and there are few independent reviews, so use the free daily tokens before subscribing. See our [DreamBF review](/reviews/dreambf/).

</AppCard>

<AppCard app="kindroid" rank={4} award="Most customizable">

Kindroid combines strong memory with selfies and voice calls, and gives you more control over his personality than any other app: backstory, key memories, speaking style, voice.

It's not an image-first app, the free tier is thin, and add-ons make the top tiers expensive. Subscribe on the web for the lower price. Kindroid doesn't pay us. Read our [Kindroid review](/reviews/kindroid/).

</AppCard>

<AppCard app="crushon-ai" rank={5} award="Cheapest paid entry">

If you'd rather browse than build, CrushOn AI has a huge library of user-made male characters, unfiltered chat, image generation and a $5.99/mo entry tier.

Every tier caps messages, which is a step down from OurDream's unlimited paid chat. Mozilla's 2024 review flagged its privacy policy. Read our [CrushOn AI review](/reviews/crushon-ai/).

</AppCard>

<AppCard app="spicychat" rank={6} award="Best free option">

SpicyChat is the best free alternative for unfiltered chat, with a big library of male characters and apps on iOS and Android. It isn't image-first, and free users get ads, queues and short replies.

Paid tiers expand memory clearly by tier, but prices rose in September 2026. SpicyChat isn't an affiliate partner. See our [SpicyChat review](/reviews/spicychat/).

</AppCard>

<AppCard app="lovescape" rank={7} award="For video clips">

If short video is what you love about OurDream, Lovescape is one of the few alternatives that generates video clips of companions, and it has a male category and a cheap annual entry price.

Credit spending is opaque, advanced memory sits behind the Creative PRO plan, and scam lookalike sites exist, so only use the official domain. Lovescape doesn't pay us. Read our [Lovescape review](/reviews/lovescape/).

</AppCard>

## How to switch

1. **Write down what you've built.** Save his appearance settings and personality notes; there's no export between apps.
2. **Use up your coins.** DreamCoins don't transfer, so spend what you have before canceling.
3. **Cancel before renewal.** Our guide on [how to cancel an AI boyfriend subscription](/faq/how-to-cancel-ai-boyfriend-subscription/) covers the steps.
4. **Start on a free tier.** Candy AI, Nomi and DreamBF all let you try before paying.

## What to look for in an OurDream alternative

- **How media is billed.** Coins and tokens make price comparisons tricky. Estimate how many images you make in a month and price it out.
- **Memory.** Images are fun; memory is what makes it feel like a relationship.
- **Track record and privacy.** Favor apps with no breach history and a clear operator. Use a separate email for any 18+ app.
- **Apps vs web.** OurDream, Candy AI and DreamBF are web-only. Nomi, Kindroid and SpicyChat have mobile apps.
