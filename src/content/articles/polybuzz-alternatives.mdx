PolyBuzz has grown fast because it's free, it's on every platform, and its library has a male character for every fantasy. But spend a week with one boyfriend and the problems pile up: ads in the middle of a conversation, a memory that only reaches back about 30 messages, and replies that have reportedly become more restricted after updates.

Here are the best PolyBuzz alternatives if what you really want is an **AI boyfriend**, ranked on memory, chat freedom, voice and real cost.

## Why people leave PolyBuzz

**Ads that interrupt chats.** The free tier shows ads, including mid-chat ads. It's hard to stay in a romantic scene when a video ad cuts in.

**Memory is paywalled in steps.** Free users get roughly a 30-message memory window. Poly Standard ($9.90/mo) mainly removes ads but keeps the same memory. Long Memory (about 100 messages) needs Poly Premium at $19.90/mo, and Permanent Memory needs Poly Ultimate at $29.90/mo. That's a lot to pay for him to remember your name.

**Filtered and inconsistent content rules.** PolyBuzz restricts adult content in public areas, and users report replies becoming more restricted after updates. If you want a romance that can go further, you'll keep hitting limits.

**Age and policy confusion.** Its age rating differs between app stores (18+, 17+ and Teen have all been reported), and the web version reportedly has no age gate. Pricing also varies by region.

## PolyBuzz memory vs the alternatives

Memory is the main reason people search for a PolyBuzz alternative, so here's how the plans line up. Prices are from our research at the time of writing and can vary by region.

| App | What you pay | What he remembers |
|---|---|---|
| PolyBuzz free / Standard | Free / $9.90 a month | About the last 30 messages |
| PolyBuzz Premium | $19.90 a month | About the last 100 messages |
| PolyBuzz Ultimate | $29.90 a month | Permanent Memory |
| Nomi AI Pro | $15.99 a month, or about $8.33 billed yearly | Long-term memory is a core feature |
| Kindroid | From $13.99 a month on the web | Layered long-term memory, journals and backstory |
| SpicyChat | Free (4K) up to its top tier (16K, 32K on some models) | Context grows with each tier |

A bigger context window and real long-term memory aren't quite the same thing. Context is how much of the recent conversation the model can see at once; long-term memory is a system that stores important facts and brings them back later. Nomi and Kindroid are built around the second, which is what makes a boyfriend feel like the same person next month. ## Is a PolyBuzz alternative worth it?

If you chat casually with lots of different characters, PolyBuzz's short memory may not bother you, and staying free (or moving to Character AI or SpicyChat) makes sense. If you've settled on one boyfriend and want a relationship that builds over time, paying for an app built around memory is better value than climbing PolyBuzz's tiers.

## How to switch from PolyBuzz

1. **Save his personality.** Copy your character's description and any key lines you love into a note.
2. **Recreate him.** In Nomi or Kindroid, paste the personality into the backstory. In SpicyChat or CrushOn, use the character creator, or search the library for a similar bot.
3. **Seed his memory.** Tell him the important facts in the first few chats. Memory-first apps will keep them.
4. **Cancel PolyBuzz in the right place.** If you subscribed through the App Store or Google Play, cancel there, not in the app. Our guide on [how to cancel an AI boyfriend subscription](/faq/how-to-cancel-ai-boyfriend-subscription/) has the steps.

## What to look for

- **Memory included, not upsold.** Check how far back he remembers on the plan you'll actually buy.
- **Ad-free chats.** Most free tiers show ads somewhere. If ads break the mood for you, budget for an entry-level paid plan.
- **Clear age rules.** Every alternative here except Character AI is 18+ only. Our explainer on [whether AI boyfriend apps are 18+](/faq/are-ai-boyfriend-apps-18-plus/) covers the details.
- **Real costs.** Token-based apps (Candy AI, OurDream) can cost more than they look.
