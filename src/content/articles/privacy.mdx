This privacy policy explains how Best AI Boyfriend App ("we", "us", "our") handles information when you visit this website. We've tried to keep it short and in plain English.

**Effective date:** October 18, 2026

## The short version

- This is a content website. You don't need an account, and we don't ask you to give us your name, email address or payment details.
- We may use analytics to understand how people use the site, in aggregate.
- When you click a link to an app, that app or its affiliate network may set cookies to track the referral. Those cookies are controlled by them, not us.
- We don't sell your personal information.

## Information we collect

### Information collected automatically

Like most websites, our hosting provider and any analytics tools we use may automatically receive standard technical information when you visit, such as:

- your IP address (which may be shortened or anonymized),
- browser type, device type and operating system,
- the pages you view and how long you spend on them,
- the page that referred you to us, and
- the date and time of your visit.

We use this information in aggregate to understand which pages are useful, fix problems, and keep the site secure.

### Information you choose to send us

If you contact us, for example by email, we'll receive whatever you include in your message, such as your email address. We use it only to reply to you and to improve the site, and we don't add you to any mailing list without your permission.

## Cookies and similar technologies

### Our own cookies

This site may use a small number of cookies or similar technologies for basic functionality and analytics. Some analytics tools work without cookies at all. We don't use cookies to build advertising profiles about you.

### Affiliate and third-party cookies

Some links on this site are affiliate links (see our [affiliate disclosure](/affiliate-disclosure/)). When you click one, you leave our site, and the destination app or the affiliate network it uses may set a cookie or use similar technology on your device. This lets them recognize that you came from our site, so they can credit us if you sign up.

These cookies are set and controlled by those third parties under their own privacy policies, not ours. We don't receive your name, email address, payment details, or the content of your chats with any app. At most, we may receive aggregated reports, such as the number of sign-ups or the commission earned.

### Managing cookies

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, and most browsers let you block third-party cookies specifically. Blocking cookies won't stop you from reading this site. If you block affiliate cookies, the apps you visit will still work normally; we just may not be credited.

## How we use information

We use the information described above to:

- operate, maintain and improve the website,
- understand which content is useful,
- measure the performance of affiliate links in aggregate,
- protect the site against abuse and security threats, and
- comply with legal obligations.

## How we share information

We don't sell or rent personal information. We may share limited information with:

- **service providers** who help us run the site, such as hosting and analytics providers, who process it on our behalf,
- **affiliate networks and partners**, only to the extent described in the cookies section above, and
- **authorities**, if required by law or to protect our rights or the safety of others.

## Data retention

We keep analytics data only as long as it's useful for understanding how the site is used, and messages you send us only as long as needed to respond and keep reasonable records.

## Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, including the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and certain US states such as California, you may have the right to:

- access the personal information we hold about you,
- ask us to correct or delete it,
- object to or restrict certain processing,
- opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information (we don't sell personal information), and
- lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us. We'll respond within the time required by applicable law.
