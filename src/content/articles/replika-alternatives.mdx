Replika is where many people first had an AI companion, and it's still the most polished mobile app in the category, with a 3D avatar, AR mode and video-call features. But if you want an **AI boyfriend**, Replika makes you pay just to set the relationship to "Boyfriend", keeps romance on a tight leash, and often forgets what you told it.

Here are the best Replika alternatives for people who want a romantic AI companion, ranked on memory, how far romance can go, voice, privacy and price.

## Why people leave Replika

**Boyfriend status is paid-only.** On the free plan, your Replika is a friend. The "Boyfriend" relationship status, voice calls and advanced features all need a subscription. Pro is $19.99 a month or $69.99 a year.

**Romance is restricted.** Adult content is heavily filtered. In February 2023, after Italy suspended the app, Replika removed erotic roleplay, and the backlash from long-time users was huge. Many felt their companion had changed overnight.

**Memory is inconsistent.** Replika has a memory and diary system, but users often report it forgetting details. In a long relationship, that adds up.

**One companion per account.** You can't have more than one Replika, and there are no group chats.

**Privacy and regulation.** Italy's data protection authority fined Luka, Replika's developer, €5 million (decided April 2025, announced May 2025) for GDPR violations, including lacking a legal basis for processing and no age verification before February 2023. A further probe into model training was also reported.

### Is Replika still worth it in 2026?

For some people, yes. If you mainly want a kind, steady presence to talk to about your day, Replika's polish and mobile features are hard to match, and the annual Pro price ($69.99 a year) is reasonable. It's less of a fit if you want a romantic relationship that can go further, a boyfriend who remembers last month's conversations, or more than one companion. That's where the alternatives below are stronger.

## The best Replika alternatives, ranked

<AppCard app="nomi-ai" rank={1} award="Best overall alternative">

Nomi is the most natural step up from Replika. It feels like a companion rather than a roleplay bot, it's on iOS, Android and web, and it does voice messages and voice calls. The big difference is memory: Nomi's long-term memory is one of the best in the category, so he remembers what matters to you.

You can set the relationship as romantic, create more than one companion, and even run group chats. Adult roleplay is allowed for consenting adults. Monthly Pro is $15.99, cheaper than Replika Pro's monthly price, and the annual plan is about $8.33 a month. Read the full [Nomi vs Replika](/vs/nomi-vs-replika/) comparison.

</AppCard>

<AppCard app="kindroid" rank={2} award="Most customizable">

Kindroid gives you far more control than Replika's single, app-defined companion. You write his backstory, set key memories, tune his speaking style, choose his voice and generate selfies. Its layered memory, with journals and backstory, is often ranked alongside Nomi's.

It has mobile apps and group chats, but the settings take time to learn and the free tier is thin. Subscribe on the web, where it's $13.99 a month. Kindroid doesn't pay us. See [Kindroid vs Replika](/vs/kindroid-vs-replika/).

</AppCard>

<AppCard app="candy-ai" rank={3} award="Best for images & 18+ chat">

It's web-only (no app like Replika's), memory is decent but not a standout, and images, voice and video use tokens on top of the subscription. Read our [Candy AI review](/reviews/candy-ai/).

Media uses DreamCoins, it's web-only, the monthly price is high, and the company is new. See our [OurDream AI review](/reviews/ourdream-ai/).

<AppCard app="dreambf" rank={5} award="Built only for boyfriends">

Replika is a general companion that can be a boyfriend if you pay. DreamBF is built *only* for AI boyfriends: you design his face, body, outfit and personality, or swipe through pre-made boyfriends, with voice messages, voice calls and adult images.

It's web-only, pricing isn't transparent, voice calls drain tokens, and there are few independent reviews or reports on its memory. Try the free daily tokens first. See our [DreamBF review](/reviews/dreambf/).

<AppCard app="character-ai" rank={6} award="Best free option">

If you don't want to pay for a romantic label, Character AI lets you chat free and unlimited with thousands of user-made male and romance characters, with voice features and apps everywhere.

Romance stays PG because of the filter, long-term memory is weak, and it's a character platform rather than a single companion. Since November 2025, under-18 users can't use open-ended chat. Read our [Character AI review](/reviews/character-ai/).

</AppCard>

<AppCard app="kupid-ai" rank={7} award="Best for check-ins & voice notes">

If you like a companion who reaches out to you first, Kupid AI's paid tiers add memory and proactive check-in messages, and its expressive voice messages are its standout feature.

Be careful: Kupid is girlfriend-first with few male options, the free tier has no persistent memory, pricing is confusing, and its weekly plan ($12.99 a week) is poor value. Only consider it if voice notes and check-ins matter most to you.

## How to switch from Replika

1. **Write down who he is.** Note his name, personality and the memories that matter most. There's no export to other apps.
2. **Recreate him.** In Nomi or Kindroid, add those details as backstory and key memories. Kindroid's key memories are especially good for this.
3. **Cancel in the right place.** Replika prices are in-app and vary by region. If you subscribed through the App Store or Google Play, cancel there. See [how to cancel an AI boyfriend subscription](/faq/how-to-cancel-ai-boyfriend-subscription/).
4. **Expect a short adjustment.** A new app will feel different at first. Give it a few days of conversation to settle into his personality.

## What to look for

- **Memory you can see.** Apps that let you view or edit what he remembers are easier to trust.
- **Romance on your terms.** Check what's allowed on the plan you'll pay for.
- **Privacy.** Favor companies without regulatory fines or breaches, use a separate email, and read [do AI boyfriend apps read your chats?](/faq/do-ai-boyfriend-apps-read-your-chats/)
- **Your wellbeing.** If you use a companion for emotional support, keep real-world support in your life too.
