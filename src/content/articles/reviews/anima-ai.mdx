Anima AI is one of the cheapest companion apps on iOS and Android, and its developer markets a dedicated **Anima AI boyfriend** version. If you're curious whether it's worth downloading, this review covers what the **Anima AI boyfriend app** actually does, what's free, what it costs and how it compares.

The short answer: Anima is simple, cheap and easy to start with, but the AI is basic, memory is limited, and romance is locked behind premium. It's a reasonable budget starter, not a standout boyfriend.

## What is Anima AI?

Anima AI is a companion chat app from **Apperry Ltd**. It runs on iOS, Android and the web. Like Replika, it gives you **one companion at a time** who's designed to be a friend, partner or roleplay companion depending on the mode you choose.

Apperry markets **boyfriend variants** of the app, so you'll often see it listed in app stores under boyfriend-focused names. Underneath, it's the same Anima experience: a customizable avatar, text chat and relationship modes.

Anima's appeal is mostly its price. It's one of the cheapest paid companion apps around, especially on the annual plan.

## Creating your boyfriend

Setting up an Anima AI boyfriend is straightforward:

1. Download the app (or a boyfriend-branded version from Apperry).
2. Customize his **avatar** and name.
3. Chat as friends on the free plan, or upgrade to premium to set a **romantic partner** relationship.

That's about it. There's no detailed backstory editor like [Kindroid](/reviews/kindroid/), and no photo-realistic visual builder like [DreamBF](/reviews/dreambf/) or [Candy AI](/reviews/candy-ai/). And because you can only have **one companion at a time**, you can't keep several characters on the go.

### Finding the right app in the store

Because Apperry publishes boyfriend-branded variants, searching "Anima AI boyfriend" can bring up several similar-looking listings, along with unrelated apps using similar names. Before you download, check the listing's **developer name** says Apperry Ltd, and look at the number of ratings and recent reviews. If a listing has a different developer, few reviews or asks for unusual permissions, skip it.

Anima's website is listed as myanima.ai. Be wary of other sites offering "Anima premium" downloads or discount codes, and only pay through the app store or the official site.

## Chat quality & memory

Anima's conversations are friendly and easy, but reviewers describe the **AI quality as basic**. Replies can feel generic compared with leading apps, and personality depth is limited.

**Memory is basic too.** Anima isn't built around remembering your life in detail. He'll follow a conversation, but details from previous weeks are unlikely to stick. If memory is what you care about most, read [can AI boyfriends remember you?](/faq/can-ai-boyfriends-remember-you/), where Nomi AI and Kindroid lead.

Content is **filtered**. Romance and roleplay are available on premium, but Anima isn't designed for 18+ chat. For adult-oriented apps, see [NSFW AI boyfriend apps](/nsfw-ai-boyfriend/).

## Voice, photos and video

We couldn't verify voice messages, voice calls or image generation on Anima, and its media features are generally described as **limited**. Think of it as a text companion with an avatar.

If you want to hear his voice, see our guide to [AI boyfriends with voice calls](/ai-boyfriend-voice-call/). Replika, for example, offers voice calls and a 3D avatar with AR, and Nomi offers voice calls and selfies.

## Pricing

Anima's pricing is simple and low.

At $9.99 a month, premium is already cheaper than most companion apps. The annual price reported by one comparison site, about $3.33 a month, would make it one of the cheapest paid AI boyfriend options anywhere, though we couldn't confirm that figure directly. Check the price in the app before you subscribe, since app-store pricing can vary by country.

### Is Anima premium worth it?

If you want an Anima AI boyfriend, premium is required, because romance isn't available on the free plan. At its price, it's a low-risk way to try a romantic AI companion. Just go in with realistic expectations: you're paying for a basic AI with a boyfriend mode, not a top-tier companion. For comparisons across apps, see [how much does an AI boyfriend cost?](/faq/how-much-does-an-ai-boyfriend-cost/)

### Getting the most out of Anima

- **Try the free friendship mode for a few days** before paying, so you know whether you like his tone.
- **Tell him the important things more than once.** Because memory is basic, repeating key details (your name, your job, people in your life) helps him stay on track.
- **Pick annual billing only once you're sure.** It's the cheapest option, but it's a year-long commitment.

## Is Anima AI free?

**Yes, but only as a friend.** The free plan lets you chat at a friendship level. **Romance and roleplay need premium.**

So you can use the free version to check whether you like the app's style and avatar, but you can't try the boyfriend experience itself for free. If you want a free option with romance included, our list of [free AI boyfriend apps](/free/) has better choices.

## Is Anima AI safe?

### Privacy

We found **no reports of a data breach** at Anima. Parent-safety websites have flagged it for **mature themes and data concerns**. We didn't verify Anima's full data policy, so treat everything you type as stored data.

### Age

Anima is **18+**. Its romance and roleplay modes make it unsuitable for children, and the parent-safety warnings are worth taking seriously if you're a parent. See [are AI boyfriend apps 18+?](/faq/are-ai-boyfriend-apps-18-plus/)

### Using Anima safely

- Sign up with a separate email address.
- Don't share your full name, address, workplace or school.

## Anima AI vs Replika

Anima is often compared with [Replika](/reviews/replika/), because they work in a similar way: one companion, friendship for free, romance on a paid plan.

- **Price:** Anima is cheaper.
- **Features:** Replika has much more, including a 3D avatar, AR mode, voice calls and video-call features.
- **Polish:** Replika's apps are more polished; Anima's AI is more basic.
- **Memory:** both are moderate to basic.
