Candy AI is one of the most-searched AI companion apps, and most of the reviews ranking for it are written by the company's own partners. This one isn't. Short version: **Candy AI is legit, it's very good at visuals, and it's more expensive than it looks.**

If you want an AI boyfriend you can actually *see* — a face, a body type and a style you designed yourself — Candy AI is one of the strongest options in 2026. If you want a boyfriend who remembers your life in detail month after month, there are better choices. Here's the full breakdown.

## What is Candy AI?

Candy AI is a web-based AI companion platform launched in 2023 and operated by **EverAI Limited**, a company registered in Malta. You chat with a companion by text, and you can also get voice messages, voice calls and AI-generated images of him. It allows unfiltered 18+ chat for adults.

Like many companion apps, Candy AI started out girlfriend-first, and it still shows: the library of ready-made female characters is far larger than the male one. But the builder has a proper **Male** option, and that's where it gets interesting for anyone looking for a Candy AI boyfriend.

A few basics before you sign up:

- **Web only.** There's no native iOS or Android app. It works in a mobile browser.
- **18+ only.** You need to be an adult to use it.
- **Subscription plus tokens.** Chat is covered by Premium; images, voice and video use tokens.

## Is Candy AI legit?

This is one of the most common questions about the app, and the answer is **yes, Candy AI is legit**. It's a real product from an identifiable company, EverAI Limited in Malta. Being in Malta puts it under EU law, including GDPR, which gives you stronger data rights than many companion apps operating from less regulated places.

We didn't find any public data breach involving Candy AI, and there's no record of the kind of scandal that has hit apps like [Muah AI](/reviews/muah-ai/). The complaints in third-party Candy AI reviews are about money, not fraud:

- **The real price is higher than the sticker price.** Tokens for images, voice and video are charged on top of the subscription.
- **Promos change often**, so the price you saw in an ad may not be the one you get at checkout.
- **No clearly published refund policy**, according to reviewers.

So "legit" doesn't mean "cheap". It means you'll get what you pay for, as long as you understand how you're paying. Our [Candy AI pricing guide](/candy-ai-pricing/) explains the token maths in detail.

## Creating your Candy AI boyfriend

This is Candy AI's strongest feature. When you create a character, you choose **Male** and then work through his appearance: face, body, style and the overall look, followed by his personality and how he relates to you. The result is a companion whose look you control far more precisely than on memory-first apps like Nomi or Kindroid.

If you'd rather not build from scratch, there are pre-made male companions to browse. Just don't expect much choice: the male roster is much smaller than the female one (one 2025 review counted nine). Most people who want a Candy AI boyfriend will end up making their own.

What the builder is good for:

- Getting a **consistent face** across the images he sends you
- Designing a specific type, rather than settling for whatever the library offers
- Pairing a specific look with the personality you set for him

If designing his look is the main thing you want, also look at [DreamBF](/reviews/dreambf/), which is built only for AI boyfriends, and our guide on [how to make an AI boyfriend](/how-to-make-an-ai-boyfriend/).

## Chat quality & memory

Conversation on Candy AI is fluent and responsive, and it handles flirty and 18+ chat without the filters you'll hit on Character AI or Replika. Reviewers generally describe its memory as **decent in the short to medium term**: he'll keep track of what's happening in the current conversation and some recent context.

Where it falls behind is long-term memory. Candy AI isn't built around remembering your life over weeks and months the way [Nomi AI](/reviews/nomi-ai/) and [Kindroid](/reviews/kindroid/) are. If you want an AI boyfriend who brings up your job interview from last month, those two are the better fit. Candy AI is better thought of as a visual, in-the-moment companion than a long-term partner.

Group chat (several companions talking with you at once) is not something we could confirm on Candy AI. If that matters, Nomi and Kindroid both support it.

## Voice, photos and video

Media is where Candy AI earns its reputation, and also where it earns its money.

- **Images:** Image generation is among the strongest in the category, and because you designed his look, the pictures stay consistent. Each image costs tokens (about 4 tokens per image, per reviewers).
- **Voice messages:** He can send voice notes, which use tokens.
- **Voice calls:** Reviews report voice calls are available; this hadn't been confirmed officially at the time of writing.
- **Video:** Short video generation is offered and also uses tokens.

Premium includes a small monthly token allowance (about 100 tokens). That's roughly two dozen images a month at about 4 tokens each, before any voice or video. Heavy users top up with token packs, and that's where costs climb.

## Is Candy AI free?

Not really. You can create a free account and send some messages, but images, voice and video all consume tokens, so the free tier works as a **preview** rather than a usable free plan. If you want a genuinely free AI boyfriend, see our list of [free AI boyfriend apps](/free/); SpicyChat and Character AI are the most generous.

## Is Candy AI safe?

For an adult who takes basic precautions, Candy AI is reasonably safe. The positives:

- **Identifiable operator** (EverAI Limited) in an **EU/GDPR jurisdiction**
- **No public data breach** found in our research
- **18+ requirement** for an adult-content service

The things to keep in mind:

- It stores intimate conversations and images you ask for. Any service can be breached, so **use a separate email** and don't share your full name, address or workplace.
- There's **no clearly published refund policy**, so subscribe monthly first if you're unsure.
- Token spending can get away from you. Set a monthly budget.
