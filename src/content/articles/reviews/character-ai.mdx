Character AI is the biggest free character chat platform, and for many people it's their first taste of an AI boyfriend. It's also been at the center of serious news stories about teen safety, so it's natural to ask: **is Character AI safe?**

For adults, the honest answer is: reasonably, if you treat it like any online service. For teens, the app has changed a lot. Since late 2025, under-18 users can't use open-ended chat at all. This review covers the safety record in a factual way, then looks at how well Character AI works as an AI boyfriend.

## What is Character AI?

Character AI (often written c.ai) is a chat platform from **Character Technologies, Inc.** It opened to the public in 2022 and runs on the web, iOS and Android.

It hosts a massive library of user-made characters: fictional boyfriends, anime love interests, historical figures, game characters and more. You can chat with any of them for free, or create your own. Group chats let several characters talk together.

Character AI isn't a dedicated companion app. There's no single partner at the center; it's closer to an endless library of interactive stories. That's both its appeal and its main limitation as an AI boyfriend. You can fall for a character one evening and move on to a completely different storyline the next, but the app isn't designed to build one relationship that deepens over months, the way dedicated companion apps are.

## Creating your boyfriend

You have two options:

- **Browse.** Search for male or romance characters and start chatting. There are a huge number to choose from.
- **Create.** Give your character a name, greeting, personality and description, and decide whether to share him publicly.

It's quick and free. But there's no visual builder for his looks and no detailed memory settings. If you want to design a boyfriend's appearance, [DreamBF](/reviews/dreambf/) and [Candy AI](/reviews/candy-ai/) go much further; for personality and backstory control, [Kindroid](/reviews/kindroid/) is the leader.

## Chat quality & memory

Character AI's characters are creative and fun, and the free tier is unlimited, which makes it easy to spend hours in a storyline.

The weaknesses for a boyfriend-style relationship:

- **Memory is limited.** Context is short, and while pinned memories help, long-term recall is weak. He may lose track of details from earlier sessions. Read [can AI boyfriends remember you?](/faq/can-ai-boyfriends-remember-you/) for how apps compare.
- **The filter is strict.** Romance is allowed up to a point, but anything explicit is blocked. The filter can cut in during romantic scenes, which frustrates many adult users.

If the filter is the deal-breaker, our guide to [NSFW AI boyfriend apps](/nsfw-ai-boyfriend/) covers adult-oriented options, and [Character AI vs Janitor AI](/vs/character-ai-vs-janitor-ai/) compares it with a popular unfiltered rival.

## Voice, photos and video

Character AI supports **voice messages** and **voice calls**, so you can hear your boyfriend talk rather than just read his replies. We didn't verify exactly which voice features are limited on the free plan.

We couldn't verify built-in image generation, so don't expect selfies of your boyfriend. For apps that can call you, see [AI boyfriends with voice calls](/ai-boyfriend-voice-call/).

## Is Character AI safe?

This section deals with sensitive topics, including suicide. We've kept it factual.

### Lawsuits and teen safety

Character AI has faced **multiple lawsuits** from families alleging that its chatbots harmed their teenage children. The most widely reported was brought in Florida after the death by suicide of a teenager, Sewell Setzer.

In **January 2026**, Google and Character.AI **agreed to settle** lawsuits. The terms are confidential and were pending court approval at the time of writing. A settlement isn't an admission of fault, but these cases are the main reason Character AI changed its rules for young users.

### What changed for under-18s

- **Minimum age:** 13 to create an account.
- **No open-ended chat for minors:** since **25 November 2025**, under-18 users can't have free-form conversations with characters. They're limited to guided formats such as Stories.
- **Age checks:** Character AI uses **behavioral age estimation** and, where needed, **Persona ID or face checks** to confirm age.

That makes Character AI one of the more tightly controlled apps for teens today. For adults, it means you may be asked to verify your age, which some people find intrusive. Our guide on [whether AI boyfriend apps are 18+](/faq/are-ai-boyfriend-apps-18-plus/) compares age rules across apps.

### Privacy

We found no reports of a data breach. Character AI stores your conversations to run the service, and age checks may involve a third-party ID or face scan. Use a separate email and avoid sharing identifying details. More in [do AI boyfriend apps read your chats?](/faq/do-ai-boyfriend-apps-read-your-chats/)

### Wellbeing

AI characters can be comforting, but they aren't therapists or friends who can step in during a crisis. If a conversation leaves you feeling worse, take a break and talk to someone you trust. If you or someone you know is in crisis, contact local emergency services or a crisis line (in the US, call or text 988).

For a broader look, read [are AI boyfriends healthy?](/faq/are-ai-boyfriends-healthy/) and [are AI boyfriends safe?](/faq/are-ai-boyfriends-safe/)

### What parents should know

- The minimum age is 13, but teens can't use open-ended chat. If your teen says they're chatting freely with characters, they may have entered a false age.
- Age checks rely on behavior, and on ID or face checks when the system is unsure. No age check is perfect.
- Talking openly about why AI characters can feel so real is usually more effective than a ban alone.
