**Dream BF**, usually written DreamBF, is one of the very few AI companion apps built **only for AI boyfriends**. Most apps in this space are girlfriend-first with a male option added later; DreamBF starts from the boyfriend. You design his face, body, outfit and personality in detail, or swipe through pre-made boyfriends until one catches your eye.

That focus is appealing. But DreamBF is also one of the least-documented apps we cover: there are **few independent reviews**, its **pricing isn't transparent**, and **voice calls burn tokens quickly**. Our advice is to try it on the free daily tokens before paying anything. Here's what we know.

## What is Dream BF?

DreamBF is a **web-based** AI boyfriend app made by the team behind **DreamGF**, its girlfriend-focused sister product. It's built around two ways of finding a boyfriend:

- **Build your own**, choosing his face, body, outfit and personality
- **Browse pre-made boyfriends** with a swipe-style interface

Once you've found him, you can chat by text, receive **voice messages**, make **voice calls**, and request **images**, including 18+ images for adults. There's no native iOS or Android app; it runs in a browser.

We couldn't confirm when DreamBF launched, and we couldn't access its official site during our research, so treat specific details below as approximate.

## DreamBF vs DreamGF

DreamBF comes from the same team as **DreamGF**, an AI girlfriend app. Rather than adding a male option to DreamGF, they built a separate product for boyfriends. That's a meaningful choice: the interface, the pre-made characters and the builder are all designed around men from the start.

It also means DreamBF isn't a solo side project: the team already runs a companion app. The boyfriend product itself, though, still has very few independent reviews, so it has to be judged on its own.

### Who it suits

Because DreamBF is about him rather than about a particular kind of user, it works the same whether you're a woman looking for a boyfriend or a gay or bi man. The builder and the pre-made boyfriends are all male, so you won't be wading through female characters to find one. ## Creating your Dream BF

This is DreamBF's reason to exist. The builder covers:

- **Face**: his features and overall look
- **Body**: his build and type
- **Outfit**: what he wears, with outfit changes available (these use tokens)
- **Personality**: how he talks and treats you

Because the whole app is about men, you're not picking from a short list of male presets hidden behind dozens of female ones, which is the experience on many girlfriend-first apps. That makes DreamBF one of the better options in our ranking for **designing his look**, alongside [Candy AI](/reviews/candy-ai/) and [OurDream AI](/reviews/ourdream-ai/).

If you'd rather not start from scratch, the **swipe-style browsing** of pre-made boyfriends is a quick way to find a starting point. Our guide on [how to make an AI boyfriend](/how-to-make-an-ai-boyfriend/) has tips that apply whichever route you take.

## What to test during your free trial

Because there's so little independent testing of DreamBF, the free plan is your chance to answer the questions reviews can't. In your first few days, check:

1. **The builder.** Can you get him looking the way you imagine? Is the result consistent across images?
2. **His personality.** Does he stay in character, or drift into generic replies after a while?
3. **Memory.** Tell him a few specific things (your job, a plan for the weekend) and see whether he brings them up a day or two later.
4. **Token burn.** Note your balance before and after a voice message, an image and a short call, so you know what a paid month would really cost.
5. **Billing terms.** Before you enter card details, read what renews, when, and how to cancel. Our guide on [cancelling an AI boyfriend subscription](/faq/how-to-cancel-ai-boyfriend-subscription/) has a checklist.

If he passes those tests, a paid plan is a reasonable next step. If not, you've lost nothing.

## DreamBF pricing

DreamBF's pricing is the hardest part of this review to pin down. Here's what we found:

- **Paid tiers** (one is called Gold) start at **$12.99 a month**, according to one directory.
- Another guide gives a range of **about $10–30 a month** across tiers.
- **Tokens** are extra and pay for images, outfit changes and voice calls.

Because the figures come from third-party listings that don't fully agree, **check the price on the checkout page** before you subscribe. And since voice calls use tokens quickly, the subscription price is unlikely to be what you actually spend if you call him often.

### How to keep DreamBF affordable

1. **Use the free daily tokens first** and see how quickly a typical session uses them.
2. **Treat calls as a treat.** Text is the cheapest way to talk to him.
3. **Pick one paid tier and stick to it** for a month before considering an upgrade.
4. **Set a monthly token budget** before buying any top-ups.

## Is DreamBF safe?

Here's what we can and can't say:

- **No reported data breaches** that we found.
- **18+ only.** It allows adult content for adults.
- **Data policy not verified.** We couldn't check how DreamBF stores or uses your chats and images.
- **Few independent reviews**, so there's little outside scrutiny so far.

None of that is a red flag on its own, but together it means you should be cautious. Use a **separate email**, don't share your full name, address, workplace or photos of your face, and be careful with spending until you understand the token system.
