EVA AI is one of the few companion apps designed **phone-first**, with native iOS and Android apps, voice calls and a customizable partner who can be a boyfriend. This **EVA AI review** covers what it does well, what it costs, and the privacy trade-off that's the biggest thing to know before you sign up.

The short version: EVA AI is polished and cheap on an annual plan, but its policy says your text and voice messages are used to develop its AI, with no opt-out described. As a boyfriend, it's decent; as a place to share your private life, it calls for caution.

## What is EVA AI?

EVA AI is a companion app from **Novi Limited**, based in **Cyprus**. It launched in 2021 and is available on **iOS and Android**. It was previously known as **Journey** and **iFriend**; its Android package name is still com.ifriend.app.

The app is built around a single virtual partner who you customize and talk to over time. Unlike character-library apps, there's no catalog of thousands of bots. It's closer to Replika in concept: one companion, designed to feel like a relationship.

## Creating your boyfriend

EVA AI lets you **customize your partner's gender**, and a **boyfriend option** is available. You can shape his personality and appearance and then build the relationship through conversation.

It doesn't offer the deep backstory controls of [Kindroid](/reviews/kindroid/) or the photo-real visual builder of [Candy AI](/reviews/candy-ai/) or [DreamBF](/reviews/dreambf/), but setup is straightforward and designed for a phone screen from the start.

## Chat quality & memory

EVA AI's chat is smooth and mobile-friendly. **Memory is moderate** and not well documented, so don't expect him to reliably recall details from weeks ago.

Content is **filtered**. Romance, compliments and flirting are part of the experience, but EVA isn't built for 18+ roleplay. For adult-oriented options, see [NSFW AI boyfriend apps](/nsfw-ai-boyfriend/).

If memory is your priority, read [can AI boyfriends remember you?](/faq/can-ai-boyfriends-remember-you/), where Nomi AI and Kindroid lead the category.

## Voice, photos and video

This is EVA AI's strongest area:

- **Voice messages** and **voice calls**.
- **Image generation**.
- **Live video calls** are reported by one source, but we couldn't confirm them.

## Pricing

<PlanTable app="eva-ai" />

EVA AI offers **1-, 3- and 12-month memberships**. The annual plan is the best value by far, listed at up to $47.99 a year, which makes EVA one of the cheaper paid companion apps on an annual basis.

### Which membership length should you pick?

- **One month** if you're still deciding. It usually costs the most per month, but you're not locked in.
- **Three months** if you like EVA but aren't sure it'll hold your interest for a year.
- **Twelve months** if you've used it for a while and know you'll stick with it. It's the cheapest by a wide margin.

Whichever you choose, set a reminder a few days before renewal so you can decide whether to keep going.

On top of the subscription, **Neuron credit packs** cost extra. Credit packs can add up if you use media features heavily, so check what's included in your plan before buying more. Our guide to [how much an AI boyfriend costs](/faq/how-much-does-an-ai-boyfriend-cost/) compares real spending across apps.

Because EVA bills through the App Store or Google Play, you'll need to cancel there. See [how to cancel an AI boyfriend subscription](/faq/how-to-cancel-ai-boyfriend-subscription/).

### Getting started with EVA AI

1. Download EVA AI from the official App Store or Google Play listing, and check that the developer is Novi Limited.
2. Choose a male partner and customize his look and personality.
3. Use the free messages to see whether you like his tone before subscribing.
4. Decide early what you won't share, such as photos of yourself, your address or your workplace, and stick to it even when the app invites you to open up.
5. If you subscribe, start with a shorter membership and move to annual later if you're happy.

## Is EVA AI safe?

### Your chats are used to develop its AI

This is the most important thing to know. EVA AI's **privacy policy says text and voice messages are used to develop its AI**, and we found **no opt-out described**. That means what you say to your boyfriend, including voice recordings, may be used to improve the company's models.

Using chats to improve AI isn't unusual in this industry, but without a described opt-out, the only control you have is what you choose to say. That matters most for voice recordings and anything personal. Our guide on [whether AI boyfriend apps read your chats](/faq/do-ai-boyfriend-apps-read-your-chats/) explains why this matters.

### Mozilla's warning

Mozilla's 2024 *Privacy Not Included* review **flagged EVA AI**. It noted that the app **encourages users to share secrets, photos and voice**, which is a concern when combined with its use of chats for AI development.

### Breaches and age

We found **no reports of a data breach**. EVA AI is **18+**. See [are AI boyfriend apps 18+?](/faq/are-ai-boyfriend-apps-18-plus/)

### How to use EVA AI more safely

- Use a separate email and don't link social accounts.
- Don't share secrets, photos of yourself or identifying details, even if the app prompts you to.
- Think twice about voice notes if you're uncomfortable with recordings being used for training.
- Read [are AI boyfriends safe?](/faq/are-ai-boyfriends-safe/) for general advice.

## EVA AI vs Replika

EVA AI is most often compared with [Replika](/reviews/replika/), since both are single-companion apps with strong mobile experiences.

- **Boyfriend mode:** both offer it; Replika's boyfriend status requires a paid plan.
- **Voice:** both offer voice calls.
- **Price:** EVA's annual plan is listed lower than Replika Pro's.
- **Privacy:** both have issues. Replika was fined €5 million under GDPR in 2025; EVA uses chats for AI training with no opt-out described.
- **Platforms:** Replika also has a web version; EVA is mobile-only.
