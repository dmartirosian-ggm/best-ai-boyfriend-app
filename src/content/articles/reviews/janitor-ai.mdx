Janitor AI is one of the most popular free roleplay sites, with a huge library of user-made characters and a reputation for allowing adult content. The question people ask most is **is Janitor AI safe**, partly because of that adult content and partly because of its unusual "bring your own API key" setup.

Short answer: for adults, Janitor AI is reasonably safe if you understand where your chats go and keep personal details out of them. As an AI boyfriend, it's great for text roleplay and weak on everything else: there's no voice, no images and the built-in model's memory is short.

## What is Janitor AI?

Janitor AI launched in 2023 as a web platform for chatting with user-created characters. It's best known for two things:

- **A massive character library**, including a huge number of male characters.
- **Model flexibility.** You can use its free built-in model, **JanitorLLM (JLLM)**, or connect your own API key from a third-party AI provider to power your chats.

The main experience is on the web. Mobile apps have been referenced too, but adult content isn't available in them.

Janitor AI is a roleplay platform, not a companion app. There's no single partner, no avatar and no relationship settings, just characters and conversations. That suits people who think of an AI boyfriend as a character in an ongoing story they co-write. It suits people less well if they want a partner who checks in, sends voice notes and slowly learns their routines, which is what dedicated companion apps are built for.

## Creating your boyfriend

You can browse the library for male characters or create your own by writing his personality, scenario and greeting. The creation tools are flexible, and many character creators write detailed descriptions.

There's no visual builder and no voice. If you want to design how he looks, [DreamBF](/reviews/dreambf/) or [Candy AI](/reviews/candy-ai/) are better fits.

## Chat quality & memory

Chat quality on Janitor AI depends almost entirely on **which model you use**.

- **JanitorLLM (free).** Reviewers say JLLM has a **short context** and tends to get **repetitive after 20 to 30 messages**. It has also had quality and uptime issues. Fine for short scenes, frustrating for an ongoing relationship where you want him to keep track of the story so far.
- **Your own API key.** Connecting an external model can make conversations noticeably better and longer-lasting. The trade-off is cost and complexity: you pay the provider per use, and setup is technical.

For a boyfriend who remembers you without any setup, [Nomi AI](/reviews/nomi-ai/) is far simpler. Read [can AI boyfriends remember you?](/faq/can-ai-boyfriends-remember-you/) for how memory works across apps.

## Voice, photos and video

Janitor AI is **text-only**. There are no voice messages, no voice calls and no image generation.

That's the biggest gap for anyone who wants an AI boyfriend to feel present. If hearing his voice matters, see [AI boyfriends with voice calls](/ai-boyfriend-voice-call/).

## Pricing

Janitor AI's pricing is unusual, and reports conflict.

There are effectively three ways to use it:

1. **Free with JLLM.** No cost, though some 2026 sources mention a cap of around 50 requests a day.
2. **A paid Janitor tier.** A paid plan reportedly launched in June 2026, but its name and price vary between sources, so check the site before paying.
3. **Your own API key.** You pay an AI provider directly, which can cost anywhere from a few dollars to much more per month depending on the model and how much you chat.

If you use an API key, set a spending limit with the provider so a long roleplay session doesn't surprise you.

## Is Janitor AI free?

**Yes.** You can chat with any character for free using JanitorLLM. That's the main reason Janitor AI is so popular. Just expect shorter memory and more repetition than paid apps. See our list of [free AI boyfriend apps](/free/) for other options.

The reported paid Janitor tier sits in between; since its details still conflict between sources, wait until the price is clear on the site itself before subscribing.

## Is Janitor AI safe?

### Age verification and NSFW

Janitor AI is for adults only. **NSFW content requires age verification**, and it has to be **enabled at both the account and the character level**. That two-step setup means you won't stumble into adult content by accident on a fresh account. Adult content isn't available in the mobile apps.

### Where your chats go

This is the most important safety point:

- With **JLLM**, your chats are processed by Janitor AI.
- With **your own API key**, your chats are **also sent to the third-party provider** behind that key, under its own data policy.

So if you use an external model, you're trusting two companies with your conversations. Read the provider's policy and never share identifying details. Our guide to [whether AI boyfriend apps read your chats](/faq/do-ai-boyfriend-apps-read-your-chats/) explains what to check.

### Your API key

Treat an API key like a password. Anyone who has it can run up charges on your account. Use a key with a spending limit, don't share it in chats or with characters, and revoke it if you stop using Janitor AI.

### A safer setup, step by step

1. **Sign up with a separate email** that isn't linked to your real name or social accounts.
2. **Pick a username** that doesn't identify you.
3. **Start on JLLM** before connecting any API key, so you can see whether you like the platform first.
4. **If you add an API key**, create a new one just for Janitor AI, give it a low spending limit, and read the provider's data policy.
5. **Only enable NSFW** if you want it; it stays off until you switch it on at the account and character level.
6. **Keep personal details out of chats**, including your workplace, city and other people's names.

### Content

The library is user-made, so quality and themes vary widely. Stick to characters you're comfortable with, and remember you can leave any chat at any time. For a wider look at risks, see [are AI boyfriends safe?](/faq/are-ai-boyfriends-safe/)
