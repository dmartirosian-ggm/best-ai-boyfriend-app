Joi AI promises AI companions who can send photos, voice and even video, with personalities that adapt to you. This **Joi AI review** looks at what that actually costs, how well it works, and whether it's a good choice if what you want is an AI boyfriend.

The short version: Joi has a lot of media features, but its **Neurons** credit system makes costs hard to predict, memory is short, and we couldn't confirm that it supports male companions. For most people looking for a boyfriend, there are clearer options.

<ReviewSummary app="joi-ai" verdict="Media-rich chat with adaptive personalities, but the Neurons credit system drains fast, memory is short, billing complaints are common and male companions are unconfirmed." />

## What is Joi AI?

Joi AI is a web-based AI companion service focused on **media-heavy chat**: text, voice messages, photos and short videos from your companion. Instead of a simple monthly plan that covers everything, most actions consume **Neurons**, Joi's in-app credits.

There's some confusion around the basics. Reviews reference **joi.com**, while Trustpilot lists **joi.ai**, and we couldn't confirm which is the canonical domain or who operates the service. That doesn't mean anything is wrong, but it's less transparency than we'd like from an app that handles intimate conversations and payments.

## Creating your boyfriend

Joi supports **custom characters**, so in principle you can create a companion with the personality you want. Reviewers describe its personalities as **adaptive**, meaning they adjust to how you talk.

The problem for this site's readers: **none of the sources we reviewed confirmed male companions**. You may be able to create a male character, but we can't promise the builder, images or voices are designed for one.

If a boyfriend is the whole point, apps that clearly support one are a safer bet:

- [DreamBF](/reviews/dreambf/) is built only for AI boyfriends.
- [Candy AI](/reviews/candy-ai/) has a "Male" option in its builder.
- [OurDream AI](/reviews/ourdream-ai/) supports male companions and has gay- and trans-focused versions.

## Chat quality & memory

Reviewers like Joi's adaptive personalities, but they also report **short memory** and **repetitive chats**. In practice, that means he may lose track of things you told him earlier and fall back on similar phrases.

That's a bigger problem on Joi than on most apps, because you're paying Neurons for messages. Repetition doesn't just feel flat, it costs money. If memory matters to you, read [can AI boyfriends remember you?](/faq/can-ai-boyfriends-remember-you/), where Nomi AI and Kindroid lead.

## Voice, photos and video

This is Joi's selling point:

- **Voice messages** from your companion.
- **Photos** generated on request.
- **Video** content.

We couldn't verify voice calls. All of these features consume Neurons, and media is far more expensive than text, which brings us to pricing.

## Pricing

Joi AI's pricing is the most common complaint about it.

<PlanTable app="joi-ai" />

### How Neurons work

Reviewers estimate these costs:

| Action | Approximate cost |
|---|---|
| Reading a message | about 4 Neurons |
| A photo | up to 120 Neurons |
| A video | up to 1,900 Neurons |

So a single video can cost as much as hundreds of messages.

To put that in perspective using those estimates: an evening of 100 messages would use roughly 400 Neurons. Add five photos and you could use up to another 600. One video on top could add up to 1,900 more. The same evening can cost several times more depending on how much media you ask for. If you mostly text, Neurons last a while. If you want regular photos or videos of your companion, costs climb fast, and it's hard to predict a monthly total in advance.

One review cites a **$17.77** headline figure for Premium, but we couldn't confirm it, so check the price on the checkout page before you pay.

### Billing complaints

Trustpilot reviewers report **billing problems**, including Neurons going missing and being **charged for images that failed** to generate. Those are individual user reports, not verified findings, but they're consistent enough to be worth knowing about. If you try Joi:

- Start with the smallest purchase.
- Screenshot your balance before and after generating media.
- Keep your receipts and contact support quickly if something looks wrong.

For a broader look at real costs, see [how much does an AI boyfriend cost?](/faq/how-much-does-an-ai-boyfriend-cost/)

### How to try Joi AI without overspending

If you still want to try Joi, a few habits keep the Neurons economy under control:

1. **Use the free tier for text first.** It's the cheapest way to see whether you like the personalities before any media comes into it.
2. **Buy the smallest Neurons pack** the first time, not the one with the biggest bonus.
3. **Ask for photos sparingly** and save video for when you really want it, since it costs many times more.
4. **Check your balance** before and after each media request, so you notice quickly if Neurons go missing.
5. **Set a monthly budget** in your head or your banking app and stop when you hit it.

Credit systems are designed to make each purchase feel small. Deciding on a limit before you start is the easiest way not to be surprised later.

## Is Joi AI free?

**Sort of.** Joi has a basic free tier that's usable for light chat, but its terms say it can be **revoked**, and almost every feature consumes Neurons. Treat it as a short trial rather than a free app.

## Is Joi AI safe?

### Privacy and transparency

We found **no reports of a data breach**. But we also couldn't verify Joi's privacy policy, operator or even its primary domain. That makes it hard to say how your chats, voice and media requests are stored. Assume they are kept, and share accordingly.

### Age and content

Joi is an **18+** app, and it allows adult content. Never describe anything you'd regret being stored. See [are AI boyfriend apps 18+?](/faq/are-ai-boyfriend-apps-18-plus/)

### Money

For most users, the biggest risk with Joi isn't data, it's **spending more than you planned**. A credit system with expensive media and reported billing glitches calls for caution.

### Safety checklist

- Use a separate email and a payment method you can monitor.
- Double-check the domain before entering card details.
- Don't share your name, address, workplace or photos of yourself.
- Read [are AI boyfriends safe?](/faq/are-ai-boyfriends-safe/) for general precautions.
