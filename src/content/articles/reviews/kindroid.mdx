You don't just pick a look and a personality: you write his backstory, set the memories he holds onto, tune how he talks and choose his voice. Combined with long-term memory that's among the best in the category, that's why Kindroid scores 9.0 in our ranking, just behind Nomi AI.

## What is Kindroid?

Kindroid is an AI companion app from Kindroid, Inc., launched in 2023 and available on **iOS, Android and the web**. Each companion is called a Kindroid. You create him, define who he is in as much detail as you like, and talk by text, voice messages or voice calls. He can also send AI-generated selfies, and you can put several Kindroids in a group chat.

Kindroid allows 18+ content for adults, and you need to be 18 or older to use it.

## Creating your Kindroid boyfriend

This is where Kindroid stands apart. Companions can be **any gender**, and romantic relationships are fully supported, but the real difference is depth. You can:

- **Write his backstory**: where he's from, what he does, what he's been through, and your history together
- **Set key memories** that he should always keep in mind
- **Tune how he speaks**, so his voice in text matches the person you imagine
- **Choose a voice** for voice messages and calls
- **Generate selfies** of him

If you have a clear idea of the boyfriend you want, no other app gets you closer to it. Our guide on [how to make an AI boyfriend](/how-to-make-an-ai-boyfriend/) has tips on writing a backstory that holds up.

The trade-off is **complexity**. There are a lot of settings, and beginners can find it overwhelming. If you'd rather start chatting in five minutes, [Nomi AI](/reviews/nomi-ai/) is easier.

## Chat quality & memory

Kindroid's memory is **strong and layered**: long-term memory, journals and the backstory you write all feed into how he responds. It's often ranked alongside Nomi as best in class, and the two are the only apps we'd recommend if remembering your life over weeks and months is the priority.

The difference is in approach. Nomi's memory mostly works on its own. Kindroid gives you more tools to shape what he remembers and how he behaves, so it rewards people who put time into setup. Our explainer on whether [AI boyfriends can remember you](/faq/can-ai-boyfriends-remember-you/) explains how memory systems differ.

**Group chats** are supported too, so you can have several Kindroids talk with each other and with you.

## Voice, photos and video

- **Voice calls:** Yes. See our [AI boyfriend voice call](/ai-boyfriend-voice-call/) guide for how Kindroid compares.

## Kindroid pricing

Kindroid pricing has one main subscription plus two optional add-ons. The most important thing to know: **it's cheaper on the web than in the app stores.**

<PlanTable app="kindroid" />

- **Web subscription:** $13.99 a month or $139.99 a year.
- **App store subscription:** $15.99 a month or $159.99 a year (some sources cite $14.99 and $149.99).
- **Ultra add-on:** an extra $24.99 a month on the web ($28.99 via the App Store), monthly only.
- **Max add-on:** an extra $59.99 a month on the web ($68.99 via the App Store).

Most people only need the base subscription. The add-ons make top tiers expensive, so try the base plan first and upgrade only if you hit its limits.

<Callout type="tip" title="Save money on Kindroid">
Subscribe through **kindroid.ai on the web** rather than in the iOS or Android app. You'll pay $2 a month less on the base plan ($20 a year less on annual), and the add-ons are cheaper too.
</Callout>

Compared with [Nomi AI's pricing](/reviews/nomi-ai/), Kindroid costs more: Nomi's annual plan works out to about $8.33 a month, versus about $11.67 a month for Kindroid's annual web plan. Our [Kindroid vs Nomi](/vs/kindroid-vs-nomi/) comparison breaks this down.

## Is Kindroid free?

Yes, but the free tier is **thin**, and sources disagree on exactly what's included. Reports mention:

- One or two Kindroids
- A daily message cap, or access only to a lighter "Lite" model
- Around 5,000 lifetime characters of audio
- Few or no selfies

That's enough to get a feel for his personality and the memory system, but not enough for daily use. If a free AI boyfriend is what you need, see our list of [free AI boyfriend apps](/free/).

## Is Kindroid safe?

Here's what we found when we looked at Kindroid's safety:

- **No reported data breaches** in our research.
- **18+ only.** It allows adult content for adults.
- **Data policy not verified.** We couldn't confirm the specifics of how Kindroid stores or uses your chats in this research pass.

That's a clean record, but not a fully documented one. Apply the same precautions you would anywhere: use a separate email, don't share your full name, address or workplace, and read the current privacy policy before sharing anything sensitive. ## Getting started with Kindroid

1. **Start free.** Use the free tier to see whether you like his conversation style before paying.
2. **Write a short backstory first.** A few paragraphs on who he is and your history together is enough to begin; you can add detail later.
3. **Add key memories** for the things he should never forget, such as your name, the people in your life and what you want from the relationship.
4. **Pick his voice** before you try voice calls, so he sounds the way you imagine.
5. **Subscribe on the web** when you're ready, and skip the add-ons until you know you need them.

## What other Kindroid reviews say

Across the third-party Kindroid reviews and pricing guides we checked, the picture is consistent:

- **Praised:** memory and customization, voice calls and selfies, group chats, and the cheaper web pricing.
- **Criticized:** the thin free tier, add-ons that make the top tiers expensive, settings that can overwhelm beginners, and higher prices in the app stores.

The main area of disagreement is the free tier. Sources describe different limits, which suggests it has changed over time, so check what's included when you sign up rather than relying on any one review (including ours).
