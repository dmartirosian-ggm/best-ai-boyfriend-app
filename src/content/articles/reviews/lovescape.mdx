## What is Lovescape?

Lovescape is a web-based AI companion service run by a company in **Cyprus**. It launched in 2023 and focuses on **romance**: its companions are designed with relationship-style personalities rather than as general roleplay bots.

Its standout feature is **generated video**. Your companion can appear in short AI-generated clips, alongside images and voice messages. Most of that media is paid for with credits. Text chat, images, voice notes and video all live in the same account, so the experience is designed around building up a romance with one companion and then seeing him in pictures and clips. It's an appealing idea for people who find text-only apps a bit flat.

Lovescape runs in your browser. We couldn't verify official mobile apps, which is another reason to be wary of unfamiliar "Lovescape" downloads.

## Creating your boyfriend

Lovescape has a **male companions category**, so you can browse ready-made boyfriends, or create a **custom character** with his own look and personality.

The romance focus helps here: companions are written to be partners, not just characters in a story. That makes Lovescape feel closer to a dedicated companion app than to a character library like Character AI or SpicyChat, where you jump between storylines. If you want even more control over his appearance, [DreamBF](/reviews/dreambf/) is built only for boyfriends and has a detailed visual builder.

## Chat quality & memory

Lovescape advertises **long-term memory**, but the **advanced memory is gated behind Creative PRO**, its more expensive plan. On cheaper plans, expect more basic recall.

That's a meaningful catch for a boyfriend app. Memory is what makes a relationship feel continuous, and here it's priced as a premium extra. For comparison, [Nomi AI](/reviews/nomi-ai/) includes its best-in-class memory in the standard subscription. Read [can AI boyfriends remember you?](/faq/can-ai-boyfriends-remember-you/) for more.

Lovescape allows **adult content** for users 18 and over.

## Pricing

Lovescape's pricing is one of its weaker points: the headline annual price looks cheap, but the plans and credit spending are hard to follow.

### What to know before you pay

- **The cheap price is annual.** The low Premium figure is a per-month price on annual billing, so you pay for a year upfront.
- **Memory costs more.** Advanced memory needs Creative PRO.
- **Monthly billing is expensive.** The top monthly plan is $64.99 a month.
- **Credits are opaque.** Reviewers say it's hard to tell how quickly media uses up credits, so video-heavy use can cost more than expected.

### What annual billing means in dollars

Because Lovescape's prices are quoted per month but billed yearly, it helps to do the math before you check out. At $5.99 to $7.99 a month, Premium works out to roughly $72 to $96 paid upfront for the year. Creative PRO at $24.99 a month on annual billing comes to about $300 for the year. That's a big commitment for an app you've only tried for a short trial, so make sure you've seen enough of it first.

## Is Lovescape safe?

### Scam lookalike sites

The biggest practical risk with Lovescape isn't the app itself, it's **fake versions of it**. Scam lookalike sites impersonate the brand; **lovescape.org.uk** is one that has been identified. These sites can be used to take payments or collect personal data.

To stay safe:

- Only use **lovescape.com** or **lovescape.ai**.
- Type the address yourself, or use a bookmark.
- Be suspicious of ads, emails or messages offering "Lovescape discounts".
- Check your card statement after your first payment to make sure the merchant looks right.

### How to spot a fake companion-app site

Lookalike scams aren't unique to Lovescape; other AI companion brands are targeted too. A few signs a site isn't the real thing:

- The domain is slightly different: an extra word, a different ending such as .org.uk, or a misspelling.
- It pushes you to pay immediately with a "limited-time" discount.
- The checkout asks for unusual payment methods, such as gift cards or crypto.
- There's no clear company name, terms of service or privacy policy.
- You arrived from a pop-up ad, a social media DM or a search ad rather than typing the address.

If any of these apply, close the tab and go to the official site directly.

### Privacy and transparency

Treat everything as stored data and avoid sharing anything identifying, including photos of yourself.
