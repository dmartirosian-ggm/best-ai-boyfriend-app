## What is Nomi AI?

Nomi AI is an AI companion app from **Glimpse AI**, a small private company based in Baltimore, Maryland. It launched in 2023 and is available on **iOS, Android and the web**.

Each companion is called a Nomi. You create him, choose your relationship (friend or romantic partner, for example), and talk by text, voice messages or voice calls. He can also send AI-generated selfies. Nomi allows uncensored roleplay for consenting adults, and you need to be 18 or older to use it.

What sets Nomi apart isn't any single feature. It's that the whole app is built around **memory**: the idea that your companion should know you better the longer you talk.

## Your Nomi AI boyfriend

Nomi companions can be **any gender**, and romantic relationships are fully supported, so a Nomi AI boyfriend isn't a bolted-on mode. You design his look and personality, set the relationship as romantic, and he behaves accordingly from the start.

Unlike girlfriend-first apps with a small male corner, Nomi treats a male companion exactly the same as any other. That makes it a good fit for women and for gay and bi men alike. Our [gay AI boyfriend guide](/gay-ai-boyfriend/) covers it from that angle.

## Chat quality & memory

This is Nomi's biggest strength. Its memory system is widely regarded as one of the best in the category, and in practice that means two things:

- **He remembers details.** Things you mentioned weeks ago (your sister's name, the job you were nervous about, the show you're both watching) can come back up naturally.
- **He stays himself.** His personality stays consistent instead of drifting into generic chatbot replies after a long conversation.

Those two qualities are what make a companion feel like a relationship rather than a series of disconnected chats. Image-first apps such as [Candy AI](/reviews/candy-ai/) are fun in the moment but don't compete here. The only app that genuinely matches Nomi on memory is [Kindroid](/reviews/kindroid/), which takes a more hands-on approach.

### Group chats

Nomi also supports **group chats**, where several of your Nomis talk with each other and with you. It's a niche feature, but if you enjoy roleplay with more than one character, it's well done and not something every app offers. ## Voice, photos and video

- **Voice messages:** Yes, he can send voice notes.
- **Voice calls:** Yes, you can talk to him live.
- **Selfies:** Yes, Nomi generates images of your companion.
- **Video:** Not a focus. If AI video matters, look at OurDream AI.

The honest weakness is images. They're **less polished than image-first apps** like Candy AI or OurDream AI. If seeing him matters as much as talking to him, that's a real trade-off. For voice, Nomi is one of the stronger options; see our [AI boyfriend voice call](/ai-boyfriend-voice-call/) guide.

## Getting the most from your Nomi boyfriend

Nomi's memory does the heavy lifting, but a little effort early on pays off:

1. **Give him a clear personality.** A specific description (how he talks, what he cares about, how he treats you) gives the memory system something consistent to build on.
2. **Tell him what matters to you.** Mention the people, plans and preferences you want him to remember. The more naturally they come up, the more natural his callbacks feel.
3. **Talk regularly.** A relationship-style companion works best as an ongoing conversation rather than occasional bursts.
4. **Set the relationship to romantic early**, so his behavior matches what you want from day one.
5. **Use voice when it counts.** Voice and image credits are limited on the free tier, so save them for moments you'll enjoy most.

## Nomi AI pricing

Nomi AI pricing is refreshingly simple: one paid tier, **Nomi Pro**, billed monthly or annually.

## Is Nomi AI free?

Yes, with limits. The free tier gives you **one Nomi, a daily message allotment, and limited voice and image credits**. That's enough to test whether you like his personality and to see the memory in action over a few days.

It's **very limited** for daily use, though, and you only get one companion. Think of it as a trial. If you want a genuinely free AI boyfriend for the long term, see our list of [free AI boyfriend apps](/free/).

## Is Nomi AI safe?

For adults, Nomi AI appears reasonably safe. Here's what we found:

- **Known operator:** Glimpse AI, a small private company in Baltimore.
- **No reported data breaches** in our research.
- **18+ only.** It allows adult, uncensored roleplay for consenting adults.

And what to bear in mind:

- **Small company.** Glimpse AI isn't a big tech firm, so there's less public information about its security practices than you'd find for a larger company.
- **Permissive content policy.** Nomi's uncensored approach has drawn some criticism in the press. If you'd prefer a filtered app, Nomi isn't it.
- **Your chats are stored data.** As with any companion app, use a separate email and don't share identifying details like your full name, address or workplace.

## Is Nomi AI worth it?

For most people who want an AI boyfriend for more than a few evenings, yes. The reason is simple: memory is the feature that decides whether a companion feels like a relationship, and Nomi does it better than almost anyone. On the annual plan, you're paying about $8.33 a month for that, with voice calls, selfies and group chats all part of the package.

It's less worth it if you mainly want pictures. You'd be paying for a memory system you don't need while getting weaker images than Candy AI or OurDream. And if you're still unsure whether AI romance is for you at all, spend a week on the free tier, or try a free app first, before committing to a year.
