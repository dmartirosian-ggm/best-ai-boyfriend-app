## What is OurDream AI?

OurDream AI is a web-based AI companion platform that launched in 2025. It combines text chat with AI-generated **images, short videos and voice messages** of your companion, and it allows 18+ content for adults. Sources name the operator as Dream Studio LLC or Dream Studio USA, Inc.

Its pitch is simple: unlimited chat on the paid plan, plus a coin system (**DreamCoins**) for media. It's web-only, with no native iOS or Android app.

For AI boyfriend seekers, the standout detail is that OurDream isn't only girlfriend-focused. You can create male companions, and there appear to be dedicated **OurDream Gay** and **OurDream Trans** variants.

## Is OurDream AI safe?

Here's what we found when we looked at OurDream AI's safety record, and what we couldn't confirm.

**What's reassuring**

- **US-based operator.** The company is identifiable, even if sources differ on the exact entity name.
- **No reported data breaches** at the time of writing.
- **18+ requirement** for an adult-content service.

**What to be cautious about**

- **Young company.** The domain was registered in November 2024, so there's little track record.
- **Data policy not verified.** We couldn't confirm how long chats are kept or whether they're used for AI training. Read the current privacy policy before you share much.
- **Lookalike domains.** Sites such as ourdream.it.com and ourdream-ai.uk aren't the official service. Only use ourdream.ai.

## OurDream AI pricing

OurDream AI pricing is a subscription for chat plus DreamCoins for media. Here are the plans, based on the latest figures we could verify:

<PlanTable app="ourdream-ai" />

What the plans include:

- **Premium (monthly), $19.99/mo:** unlimited messages and 1,000 DreamCoins a month for images, video and voice.
- **Premium (annual), $9.99/mo:** billed as $119.88 a year. Sources vary on the exact coin bonus for annual subscribers.
- **DreamCoin packs:** 1,000 coins for $9.99, up to 20,000 coins for $159.99.

One source also mentions a higher "Deluxe" tier, which we couldn't confirm.

The monthly price is higher than most rivals, but the annual plan is competitive, and unlimited chat plus a monthly coin allowance is a fair package. As with any coin system, heavy image and video use will cost more than the headline price.

### What will you actually spend?

That depends on how you use it:

- **Mostly chatting.** You'll pay the subscription and little else. On the annual plan, that's $9.99 a month for unlimited messages, which is good value.
- **Some images each week.** The 1,000 monthly DreamCoins included with Premium should go a fair way, though we couldn't verify exactly how many coins each image or video costs.
- **Lots of video.** Video is the most demanding feature, and it's where coin packs come in. Set a budget before you start, and keep in mind reviewers say video can fail or be slow at busy times.

Compared with [Candy AI's pricing](/candy-ai-pricing/), OurDream's monthly plan costs more, but the included coin allowance and unlimited chat mean a moderate user may end up spending a similar amount.
