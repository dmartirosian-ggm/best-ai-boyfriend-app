PolyBuzz is a free character chat app that's become popular for casual roleplay on mobile. The two things people ask most are **is PolyBuzz safe** and **is PolyBuzz free**, so this review answers both, then looks at how well it works as an AI boyfriend.

The quick version: PolyBuzz is free and easy to start with, and it has plenty of male characters. But ads interrupt your chats, memory is short unless you pay for the top tier, and its age policy is inconsistent, which matters for safety.

## What is PolyBuzz?

PolyBuzz is a character chat app from **Cloud Whale Interactive Technology LLC**, available on the web, iOS and Android. Like Character AI, it's built around a large library of user-created characters you can browse and chat with, plus the option to make your own.

It's not a dedicated companion app. There's no single "partner" at the center of the experience; instead you hop between characters and storylines. Plenty of those characters are male love interests, which is why PolyBuzz shows up in AI boyfriend searches.

## Creating your boyfriend

You can either pick a ready-made male character from the library or create your own with a name, personality and description. It's quick and beginner-friendly.

What you don't get is deep relationship control. There are no detailed backstory journals or memory settings like those in [Kindroid](/reviews/kindroid/), and no visual builder for his face and body like [DreamBF](/reviews/dreambf/).

## Chat quality & memory

PolyBuzz chats are fine for short, light roleplay. Two things get in the way of a longer "relationship":

- **Memory.** On the free plan he only remembers roughly the last **30 messages**. Poly Premium extends that to about 100 messages, and only Poly Ultimate offers **Permanent Memory**.
- **Filtering.** Explicit content isn't allowed in public areas, and users report that replies have become **more restricted after updates**. Some say private chats are looser, but we wouldn't count on it.

In practice, this means your boyfriend may forget things you told him earlier in the same evening unless you're on a higher plan. If memory matters to you, read [can AI boyfriends remember you?](/faq/can-ai-boyfriends-remember-you/)

## Voice, photos and video

PolyBuzz supports **voice messages**. We couldn't verify voice calls or image generation, so don't pick PolyBuzz if you want to hear him call you or see generated photos of him. For those, see our guide to [AI boyfriends with voice calls](/ai-boyfriend-voice-call/).

## PolyBuzz pricing

PolyBuzz's paid tiers are mostly about two things: removing ads and buying more memory.

<PlanTable app="polybuzz" />

The pricing structure is the biggest problem. Poly Standard mainly removes ads but keeps the same short memory as the free plan. To get memory that makes a boyfriend feel consistent, you need Poly Ultimate, which is more than many dedicated companion apps charge for features that include long-term memory by default. Prices also vary by region.

### Which PolyBuzz plan should you pick?

- **Stay free** if you chat a few times a week and don't mind ads. The free plan already gives you the full character library.
- **Poly Standard** only makes sense if the ads are what's driving you away. It doesn't improve memory, so your boyfriend will be just as forgetful.
- **Poly Premium** is the minimum for a boyfriend who can follow a longer storyline, but about 100 messages still isn't long-term memory.
- **Poly Ultimate** is the only plan with Permanent Memory. At that price, compare it against a dedicated companion app first, because most of them include long-term memory, voice and images in their standard plan.

If you're weighing up costs across apps, our guide to [how much an AI boyfriend costs](/faq/how-much-does-an-ai-boyfriend-cost/) puts PolyBuzz's prices in context.

## Is PolyBuzz free?

**Yes.** You can use PolyBuzz without paying. The trade-offs are:

- **Ads**, including ads that appear in the middle of a chat.
- **Message limits**.
- **Short memory** of roughly 30 messages.

For a quick, casual chat with a character, that's acceptable. For daily boyfriend-style conversation, the ads and forgetfulness get frustrating quickly. Our list of [free AI boyfriend apps](/free/) shows which free tiers hold up better.

## Is PolyBuzz safe?

We found no reports of a data breach at PolyBuzz. The safety concerns are about age checks, content and ads.

### Age rating and age gate

PolyBuzz's own minimum age is 18, but its **age rating is inconsistent** across stores: it has been listed as 18+, 17+ and Teen in different places. The web version reportedly **lacks an age gate** entirely.

For adults, this doesn't change much day to day. For parents, it's the main reason we don't consider PolyBuzz suitable for teenagers. See [are AI boyfriend apps 18+?](/faq/are-ai-boyfriend-apps-18-plus/) for how other apps handle age.

### Ads

Mid-chat ads are more than an annoyance. They're a reminder that the free version is ad-supported, and ad-supported apps generally collect data to serve those ads. We didn't verify PolyBuzz's data policy in detail, so assume your chats are stored.

### How to use PolyBuzz safely

- Use a separate email and a nickname.
- Don't share your address, workplace, school or anything that identifies you.
- Don't click through ads you don't recognize.
- If you subscribe, keep track of which store you paid through so you can cancel. Here's [how to cancel an AI boyfriend subscription](/faq/how-to-cancel-ai-boyfriend-subscription/).

### Getting started on PolyBuzz without regrets

If you decide to try it, a few minutes of setup makes PolyBuzz a better experience:

1. **Start on the free plan** and chat with two or three male characters before you pay for anything.
2. **Keep important details in the character description** rather than relying on chat memory, since the free plan only holds about 30 messages.
3. **Check the price in your region** before subscribing, because PolyBuzz prices vary by country.
4. **Turn off auto-renew** right after subscribing if you only want to test a paid tier for a month.

## Who should use it

- You want a **free, casual** roleplay app on your phone.
- You like **browsing characters** rather than building one partner.
- You don't mind ads or a filter.

## Who should skip it

- You want **one consistent boyfriend** who remembers you without paying $29.90 a month.
- You want **18+ chat**.
- You're shopping for a **teenager**. The age policy is too inconsistent.
- You want **voice calls or images**.
