If you've been searching for "Roger AI boyfriend", you're probably looking for **Roger**, also called **ChatRoger**: an AI boyfriend app built specifically for gay, bi and queer men. It runs at chatroger.com, and the product calls itself "Roger – Your AI Boyfriend".

Here's what it does, what it costs, whether it's safe, and how it compares with the bigger AI boyfriend apps.

<ReviewSummary app="roger-ai" verdict="The most gay-focused AI boyfriend app we know of, with a big library of male companions, voice and images, held back by memory that trails the category leaders." />

## What is Roger (ChatRoger)?

Most AI boyfriend apps are marketed at straight women and treat gay users as an afterthought. Roger flips that: every companion is male, and the whole product is designed around gay, bi and queer men.

According to the site and third-party reviews, you get:

- **A large community library** of male companions, plus the option to create your own boyfriend
- **Text chat, voice notes and audio calls**
- **Image generation** in chat, plus short AI video clips
- **An 18+ mode** for adult chat
- A **free tier** to try it before paying

Roger is web-based. We couldn't confirm an official iPhone or Android app, so treat store apps using the name with caution (more on that below).

## Creating your Roger boyfriend

You can start chatting straight away with a companion from the library, or build your own: his look, personality and the kind of relationship you want. If you've used [Candy AI](/reviews/candy-ai/) or [OurDream AI](/reviews/ourdream-ai/), the flow will feel familiar. The difference is that everything, from the default characters to the examples, is written for men who want a man.

## Chat quality and memory

This is where Roger is weaker. Reviewers rate it below the top apps on polish and memory: he may forget details you shared earlier in a longer relationship. If a boyfriend who remembers your life week to week matters most to you, [Nomi AI](/reviews/nomi-ai/) and [Kindroid](/reviews/kindroid/) are still the leaders. Both let you create a male companion and set a romantic relationship, whatever your gender. See [can AI boyfriends remember you](/faq/can-ai-boyfriends-remember-you/).

## Voice, photos and video

Voice notes and audio calls are included, which puts Roger ahead of text-only apps like Janitor AI. Images and short clips use credits, so heavy media use costs extra on top of the subscription, the same model Candy AI and OurDream use. If calls are your priority, compare it with our [AI boyfriend apps with voice calls](/ai-boyfriend-voice-call/).

## Roger AI pricing

<PlanTable app="roger-ai" />

Third-party sources list premium at about $14.99 a month or $89.99 a year (roughly $7.50 a month billed annually). Prices in this category change often and can differ by region, so check the in-app upgrade screen.

## Is Roger AI free?

Partly. **Roger Free** lets you browse and chat with companions at no cost, which is enough to decide whether you like it. Premium features and media (images, clips) need a subscription or credits. For other options, see [free AI boyfriend apps](/free/).

## Is Roger AI safe and legit?

Roger appears to be a legitimate, working product, and we found no data-breach reports. The site says chats are **private and encrypted** and can be **deleted on request**, but that's the company's own description. A third-party listing names the operator as Life AI Technologies, LLC.

Being an app for gay men adds one extra consideration: if you're not out, a leak would hurt more. Follow the basics:

- Sign up with an email that doesn't include your real name
- Keep your surname, workplace, city and photos of yourself out of chats
- Pay through a method you're comfortable seeing on your statement
- Read the privacy policy before you share anything sensitive

<Callout type="warning" title="Watch out for lookalikes">
Because "Roger AI" is a popular search, other apps and sites use the name. Unrelated products called Roger exist too (including a desktop productivity tool). Only sign up through **chatroger.com** itself, and be wary of store apps or download sites using the name that aren't clearly from the same company. Never pay through a link in an ad or DM.
</Callout>

## Who should use Roger

- **Gay, bi and queer men** who want an app made for them, not adapted for them
- People who like browsing ready-made male characters
- Anyone who wants voice and images without paying on day one

## Who should skip it

- If **long-term memory** matters most, Nomi AI or Kindroid will feel more like a real relationship
- If you need a **native mobile app**, Nomi and Kindroid have iOS and Android apps
- If you want the **most realistic images and video**, OurDream AI and Candy AI are more established
