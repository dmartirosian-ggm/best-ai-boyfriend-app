SpicyChat is the go-to free platform for unfiltered AI roleplay, with a huge library of user-made characters, including plenty of male ones. Before you sign up, it's sensible to ask **is SpicyChat safe**, especially for an app built around 18+ chat.

Our answer: for adults, SpicyChat is reasonably safe if you keep personal details out of it. We found no breach reports, but we also couldn't verify its data policy. As an AI boyfriend, it's the best free option for spicy roleplay, but free replies are short and queued, and the good memory is on the paid tiers.

## What is SpicyChat?

SpicyChat (spicychat.ai) is an AI roleplay platform that launched in 2023. It runs on the web, iOS and Android, and it's known for allowing **uncensored NSFW chat** for adults, even on the free plan.

Like Character AI, it's built around a large library of user-created characters, plus the option to make your own. Unlike Character AI, it doesn't filter adult content. Group chats are supported too. That combination, a giant free library with no content filter, is the main reason SpicyChat is so popular, and why it ranks as our best free 18+ option in our [best AI boyfriend apps](/) list.

SpicyChat isn't a dedicated companion app. You hop between characters rather than building one long-term partner, though paid memory upgrades make longer relationships more workable. It also doesn't pay us anything: SpicyChat has no affiliate program, so everything in this review is based purely on how it compares with the alternatives.

## Creating your boyfriend

You can:

- **Browse** the huge library of user-made male characters, across every genre from romance to fantasy.
- **Create your own** with a personality, scenario and greeting.

There's no visual builder for his looks. If you want to design your boyfriend's face and body, [DreamBF](/reviews/dreambf/) or [Candy AI](/reviews/candy-ai/) are better at that. SpicyChat does support image generation, but the core experience is text roleplay.

## Chat quality & memory

SpicyChat's chat quality depends heavily on your plan, because **memory scales by tier**:

| Plan | Context memory |
|---|---|
| Free | 4K |
| Get a Taste | 4K |
| True Supporter | 8K |
| I'm All In | 16K (32K on some models since September 2026) |

Paid tiers also add **semantic memory**, which helps him recall relevant details. On the free plan, replies are **short (about 180 tokens)**, and with a 4K context he'll lose track of longer storylines.

One quirk: users report the interface **sometimes misreports memory size**, so don't rely on the number shown in the app alone.

For a boyfriend who remembers your life over weeks, [Nomi AI](/reviews/nomi-ai/) is still ahead. Read [can AI boyfriends remember you?](/faq/can-ai-boyfriends-remember-you/) for a comparison.

### The September 2026 price rise

On **8 September 2026**, the True Supporter tier rose from **$14.95 to $19.95** a month, according to one review site. No new features were added, and the change drew criticism from users. The I'm All In tier is listed at $24.95 to $29.95, and we couldn't confirm its post-increase price.

### Which plan should you choose?

- **Free:** fine for trying characters and casual roleplay if you can live with ads, queues and short replies.
- **Get a Taste:** worth it if ads and queues are the only thing bothering you. It doesn't improve memory.
- **True Supporter:** doubles memory to 8K, but after the price rise it's less of a bargain than it was.
- **I'm All In:** the best memory and the only tier with voice. At this price, compare it with dedicated companion apps that include long-term memory and voice in their standard plans.

## Is SpicyChat free?

**Yes, and generously.** SpicyChat's free tier offers **unlimited chat, including 18+ roleplay**. The trade-offs:

- **Ads** and **queue waits** at busy times.
- **4K context memory**.
- **Short replies** of around 180 tokens.

### Getting more from the free tier

- **Pick well-written characters.** A detailed character description gives him a consistent personality even when memory is short.
- **Keep storylines focused.** With 4K of context, shorter scenes hold together better than sprawling ones.
- **Recap occasionally.** A one-line reminder of where things stand helps him pick the thread back up.
- **Chat outside peak hours** if queue waits bother you.

## Is SpicyChat safe?

### Privacy

We found **no reports of a data breach** at SpicyChat. We weren't able to verify its privacy policy for this review, so we can't tell you exactly how chats are stored or used. Assume everything you type is kept, and act accordingly. Our guide to [whether AI boyfriend apps read your chats](/faq/do-ai-boyfriend-apps-read-your-chats/) explains what to check in any policy.

### Age

SpicyChat is **18+**. It's built around adult roleplay, so it isn't appropriate for anyone younger. See [are AI boyfriend apps 18+?](/faq/are-ai-boyfriend-apps-18-plus/)

### Content

Characters are **user-made**, so quality and themes vary widely, and the platform's lack of a content filter means you're responsible for steering conversations where you want them. Stick to characters you're comfortable with, skip anything that crosses a line, and remember you can leave any chat at any time.

### Billing

The September 2026 price rise is a reminder that subscription prices can change. Check your renewal price before it bills, and know [how to cancel an AI boyfriend subscription](/faq/how-to-cancel-ai-boyfriend-subscription/) through the store or site you paid with.

### Safety checklist

- Use a separate email and a username that doesn't identify you.
- Don't share your full name, address, workplace or photos of yourself.
- Keep spicy chats fictional rather than about real people.
