"AI boyfriend Talkie" is one of the most searched companion-app phrases right now, and it's easy to see why: Talkie is built around **voice**. You pick a character, hear him talk back, and collect cards as the story goes. But people also want to know whether **Talkie AI is safe**, given its privacy warnings and its habit of disappearing from app stores.

Short answer: Talkie is fine for casual, filtered, voice-first chat if you keep personal details out of it. It isn't the best home for a long-term AI boyfriend, because its memory is only moderate and its availability has been unpredictable.

## What is Talkie AI?

Talkie AI is a character chat app published by **SUBSUP PTE. LTD.**, a Singapore company backed by **MiniMax**, an AI company based in Shanghai. It launched in 2023 and runs on the web, Android and iOS (in the US, the iOS app is now called **Talkie Lab**).

Instead of building one companion and talking to him for months, most people use Talkie like a story app: browse a library of user-made characters, start a conversation, and listen to voiced replies. A collectible card system adds a light game layer on top.

It's not a dedicated boyfriend app, but the library has a lot of male characters, from fantasy love interests to modern-day romance setups, which is why "ai boyfriend talkie" gets so many searches.

## Creating your boyfriend

You have two routes:

- **Pick an existing character.** The fastest way in. Search the library for male characters in the genre you like and start chatting.
- **Create your own.** Talkie supports custom characters, so you can write his name, personality and backstory and choose how he sounds.

The builder is simpler than what you get in [Kindroid](/reviews/kindroid/) or [Nomi AI](/reviews/nomi-ai/), where you can shape backstory, memories and relationship type in detail. On Talkie, your boyfriend is more of a character in a story than a partner you're building a relationship with.

## Chat quality & memory

Conversations are quick and lively, and the characters are designed to stay in role. The weakness is **memory**. Talkie isn't a memory-focused app, and reviewers describe recall as moderate: he'll follow the current conversation, but don't expect him to bring up your sister's name or last week's job interview without prompting.

Romance is also **filtered**. Flirting and slow-burn storylines work, but Talkie isn't built for 18+ roleplay. If that's what you're after, see our guide to [NSFW AI boyfriend apps](/nsfw-ai-boyfriend/).

If memory is the thing you care about most, read [can AI boyfriends remember you?](/faq/can-ai-boyfriends-remember-you/). The short version: Nomi and Kindroid are well ahead of Talkie here.

## Voice, photos and video

Voice is where Talkie shines. Characters reply with voice messages, and you can make **voice calls**, although free users get short calls of about two minutes. Talkie also supports image generation, and the collectible cards add a game-like layer as your chats progress.

There's no video calling. For a full rundown of apps that can actually call you, see [AI boyfriends with voice calls](/ai-boyfriend-voice-call/).

## Pricing

Talkie's paid plan is called **Talkie+**, and it's one of the cheaper entry points in the category. Prices vary by region, and some countries see much lower local prices.

## Is Talkie AI free?

**Yes, with limits.** The free tier gives you about **50 messages a day** and short voice calls of roughly two minutes. That's plenty to test whether you like a character's voice and personality.

If you chat every day, you'll hit the cap fast, which is the point at which Talkie nudges you toward Talkie+. If you'd rather not pay at all, our list of [free AI boyfriend apps](/free/) compares which free tiers are actually usable.

## Is Talkie AI safe?

This is the question most people searching for Talkie are asking, so here's everything we found.

### Privacy

Mozilla's 2024 *Privacy Not Included* review gave Talkie a **privacy warning label** and a **content warning**. That puts it in the same bucket as several other companion apps, but it's still a reason to be careful about what you share.

Talkie is published by a Singapore company backed by **MiniMax** in Shanghai. Ownership on its own isn't proof of a problem, but some users are uncomfortable sending voice recordings and personal conversations to a Chinese-backed company. That's a personal call.

### The App Store removal and Talkie Lab

Talkie has a patchy availability record:

- **December 2024:** Talkie disappeared from the US App Store. The company cited "technical reasons".
- **February 2025:** It returned on iOS under a new name, **Talkie Lab**.
- **April 2026:** It was reportedly pulled from Google Play for a short time, and was back by May.

None of this means the app is unsafe to use today, but it does mean your characters, chats and subscription could be disrupted again. If you pay for Talkie+, monthly billing is safer than annual for that reason.

### Age

We couldn't verify an official minimum age for Talkie, and Mozilla flagged its content. It's a romance and roleplay app, so treat it as adults-only. Our guide on [whether AI boyfriend apps are 18+](/faq/are-ai-boyfriend-apps-18-plus/) explains how age limits work across apps.

### How to use Talkie more safely

- Sign up with an email you don't use for anything important.
- Don't share your full name, address, school or workplace.
- Skip voice notes if you're uneasy about recordings being stored.
- Cancel from the app store you subscribed through. Here's [how to cancel an AI boyfriend subscription](/faq/how-to-cancel-ai-boyfriend-subscription/).

It depends on what "boyfriend" means to you. Here's how Talkie stacks up on the things that matter most in a relationship-style chat:

| What you want | How Talkie does |
|---|---|
| He remembers you | Moderate. Fine within a conversation, unreliable over weeks |
| You can hear his voice | Strong. Voice replies and calls are the core feature |
| You can see him | Yes, through image generation |
| Spicy or 18+ chat | No. Romance is filtered |
| Value | Good. Talkie+ Standard is one of the cheaper paid plans |
| Privacy | Weak. Mozilla warning label, Chinese-backed ownership |

So Talkie works best as a **flirty voice-drama app**: you dip into different storylines, listen to characters, and don't mind starting over. It works less well if you want one consistent partner whose personality and memories build up over months.
