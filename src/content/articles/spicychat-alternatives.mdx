SpicyChat has been the go-to free option for unfiltered roleplay for a while: unlimited free chat, a huge library of user-made male characters, and apps on every platform. So why look for a SpicyChat alternative? Mostly because of the September 2026 price increase, plus the everyday friction of short replies, queues and small memory on the free tier.

Here are the best alternatives for people who use SpicyChat for an **AI boyfriend**, ranked on how well they replace what you liked and fix what you didn't. SpicyChat doesn't pay us a commission, and neither do Kindroid or Janitor AI, which both made the list on merit.

## Why people look for SpicyChat alternatives

**The September 2026 price hike.** On 8 September 2026, SpicyChat reportedly raised its True Supporter tier from $14.95 to $19.95 a month, with no new features. That move drew criticism. (This comes from one review site, so treat the details as reported.)

**Free replies are short and queued.** The free tier has ads, queue waits and replies capped at about 180 tokens, which is a few sentences. Romance scenes can feel clipped.

**Memory depends on your wallet.** Free and Get a Taste users get 4K context, True Supporter 8K, and I'm All In 16K (32K on some models). Some users report the interface misreporting memory size.

**Voice is top-tier only.** If you want to hear him, you need the most expensive plan.

## The best SpicyChat alternatives, ranked

<AppCard app="crushon-ai" rank={1} award="Closest alternative">

CrushOn AI is the most similar platform: a huge user-made library with plenty of male characters, unfiltered chat, image generation and a choice of AI models. Its Standard tier is $5.99 a month and Premium is $14.99, which is now cheaper than SpicyChat's True Supporter.

The catch is message caps on every tier (about 2,000 a month on Standard, 6,000 on Premium; unlimited only on the $49.90 Deluxe plan). It's web-only, and Mozilla's 2024 review gave it a privacy warning. Read our [CrushOn AI review](/reviews/crushon-ai/).

</AppCard>

<AppCard app="nomi-ai" rank={2} award="Best for memory">

If you've settled on one favorite character and keep restarting his story because he forgets, Nomi solves it. Its long-term memory is one of the best in the category, so details carry over between chats and his personality stays consistent.

Adult roleplay is allowed, and you get voice messages, voice calls and selfies on mobile apps or web. You build him yourself rather than browsing. Nomi Pro is $15.99 a month or about $8.33 a month billed yearly, less than SpicyChat's new True Supporter price. See our [Nomi AI review](/reviews/nomi-ai/).

</AppCard>

<AppCard app="ourdream-ai" rank={3} award="Unlimited paid chat + images">

OurDream AI's paid plan gives unlimited messages plus image and short-video generation, and there are gay- and trans-focused versions. The free tier allows about 50 messages a day.

Media uses DreamCoins, the monthly plan is $19.99 (the annual plan is $9.99 a month), and it's web-only. Video is reported to be slow at peak times, and the company is new. Read our [OurDream AI review](/reviews/ourdream-ai/).

</AppCard>

<AppCard app="candy-ai" rank={4} award="Best for images & voice">

Tokens make heavy use expensive, memory is decent rather than exceptional, and it's web-only. See our [Candy AI pricing guide](/candy-ai-pricing/).

The free tier is thin and the settings take time to learn. Subscribe on the web, where it's $13.99 a month. Kindroid doesn't pay us. Compare it with Nomi in [Kindroid vs Nomi](/vs/kindroid-vs-nomi/).

It's text-only, the built-in model has short context and gets repetitive after 20 to 30 messages, and some 2026 sources mention a daily request cap. Adult content isn't in its mobile apps. See our [Janitor AI alternatives](/janitor-ai-alternatives/) too.

</AppCard>

Pricing isn't transparent, voice calls use tokens fast, and independent reviews are scarce. Try it on the free daily tokens first. Read our [DreamBF review](/reviews/dreambf/).

## Who should stay on SpicyChat

Switching isn't always the right call. SpicyChat is still a good fit if:

- **You don't pay.** Unlimited free chat with adult roleplay allowed is rare. Most dedicated boyfriend apps, including Nomi and Candy AI, have free tiers that work more like trials.
- **You like variety.** If you hop between many characters, its library and group chats matter more than long-term memory.
- **You want mobile apps.** SpicyChat has iOS and Android apps; CrushOn AI, Candy AI, OurDream AI and DreamBF are web-only.
- **You're on Get a Taste.** The reported price rise was to True Supporter. The entry tier is still listed at about $5 a month and removes ads and queues, though memory stays at 4K. For light, ad-free roleplay, that's hard to beat.

If one of those describes you, it may be worth waiting to see whether SpicyChat adds features to justify the new price before you move your characters.
