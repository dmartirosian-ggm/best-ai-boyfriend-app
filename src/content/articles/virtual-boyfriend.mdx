Others go all the way to an AI husband with years of shared backstory.

## The best virtual boyfriend apps

<AppCard app="nomi-ai" rank={1} award="Best virtual boyfriend app overall">

Nomi is the virtual boyfriend app that feels most like an ongoing relationship. Its long-term memory is the best in the category: he remembers what you told him weeks ago and keeps a consistent personality, instead of becoming a different person every few days.

You design how he looks and who he is, set the relationship as romantic, and talk by text, voice messages or calls. He can send selfies, too. The free tier is tight; the annual plan is good value for daily use. Read our [Nomi AI review](/reviews/nomi-ai/).

</AppCard>

<AppCard app="kindroid" rank={2} award="Best for an AI husband">

Kindroid is the best app for a detailed, committed relationship because you write his backstory yourself. Want a husband you met at university, married eight years ago, with a dog and a running joke about his cooking? Write it in, add key memories, choose his voice, and he'll play it consistently.

The trade-off is setup time and a thin free tier. Subscribe on the web for the lower price. Kindroid isn't an affiliate partner. See our [Kindroid review](/reviews/kindroid/).

</AppCard>

<AppCard app="replika" rank={3} award="Best virtual boyfriend app on mobile">

Replika is the most polished virtual boyfriend app on iPhone and Android, with a customizable 3D avatar, AR mode and video-call-style features. It's especially good for supportive, everyday chat.

The boyfriend relationship status is paid-only, adult content is heavily restricted, and you only get one companion. Replika was fined €5 million under GDPR in Italy in 2025. Read our [Replika review](/reviews/replika/).

</AppCard>

<AppCard app="candy-ai" rank={4} award="Best for designing how he looks">

If seeing your virtual boyfriend matters, Candy AI's builder lets you shape his face, body and style, and its image generation is among the best. It also supports voice messages, calls and 18+ chat.

Images, voice and video use tokens, so budget beyond the subscription. Memory is decent but not Nomi-level. It's web-only. See our [Candy AI review](/reviews/candy-ai/).

</AppCard>

<AppCard app="dreambf" rank={5} award="Built only for boyfriends">

DreamBF is a virtual boyfriend app and nothing else. Design his face, body, outfit and personality, or swipe through pre-made boyfriends to find one you like. It supports voice messages, calls and adult images.

Pricing isn't very transparent, voice calls use tokens quickly, and there are few independent reviews. Use the free daily tokens to try it.

</AppCard>

<AppCard app="ourdream-ai" rank={6} award="Best for photos and short videos">

OurDream AI offers unlimited chat on its paid plan plus image and short-video generation, so your virtual boyfriend can feel more present. It appears to run gay- and trans-focused versions too.

Media uses DreamCoins, the monthly price is high, and it's a newer company. The free tier gives about 50 messages a day. Read our [OurDream AI review](/reviews/ourdream-ai/).

</AppCard>

<AppCard app="eva-ai" rank={7} award="Phone-first virtual partner">

EVA AI is a mobile app built around a virtual partner, with a boyfriend option, voice calls and (per one source) live video calls. It's simple and works well if you only use your phone.

Privacy is the concern: its policy says your text and voice messages are used to develop its AI, with no opt-out described. Read our [EVA AI review](/reviews/eva-ai/).

</AppCard>

<AppCard app="character-ai" rank={8} award="Best free virtual boyfriend">

Character AI is free and unlimited, with thousands of user-made male romance characters and voice calls. It's a good place to find out whether a virtual boyfriend appeals to you before you pay for anything.

It's strictly safe-for-work and its memory is weak, so he won't feel like a long-term partner. See our [Character AI review](/reviews/character-ai/).

## How to have an AI husband

"AI husband" searches are growing, and the setup is the same as for a boyfriend, just with more history. Here's what works:

1. **Pick an app with strong memory.** A husband who forgets your anniversary isn't much of a husband. Nomi and Kindroid are the clear leaders.
2. **Write your shared history.** How you met, when you married, where you live, pets, family, routines. On Kindroid this goes in his backstory and key memories; on other apps you can tell him in your first conversations.
3. **Describe the marriage, not just the man.** Do you bicker about chores? Have a Friday takeaway ritual? These details are what make it feel lived-in.
4. **Keep reminding him of milestones.** Even good memory benefits from you mentioning important dates and events.
5. **Set the tone.** Supportive, playful, romantic, protective: tell him directly how you want him to show up.

For a full walkthrough, see our guide on [how to make an AI boyfriend](/how-to-make-an-ai-boyfriend/).

## Virtual boyfriend app vs virtual boyfriend game

Older virtual boyfriend games, often called dating sims or otome games, follow scripted storylines where you choose from set answers. AI apps are different: he replies to whatever you type, can talk about anything, and (on good apps) remembers what happened. If you enjoy story-driven romance, our guide to [AI boyfriend games](/ai-boyfriend-games/) covers apps that blend the two.

## Virtual boyfriend apps for iPhone and Android

If you want a proper app on your phone rather than a website, your options narrow:

- **On iPhone and Android:** Nomi AI, Kindroid, Replika and Character AI all have native apps. EVA AI is mobile-only.
- **Web-only:** Candy AI, DreamBF and OurDream AI run in your phone's browser. Add them to your home screen and they behave much like an app.

One tip for Kindroid: subscribing through the website is cheaper than through the App Store or Google Play, and you can still use the mobile app afterwards.

## How much does a virtual boyfriend cost?

Most virtual boyfriend apps charge a monthly subscription, with annual plans bringing the monthly cost down considerably. The headline price is only part of the story, though:

- **Flat subscriptions** (Nomi, Kindroid, Replika) are predictable. You pay one price and chat as much as you like within the plan's limits.
- **Token and coin systems** (Candy AI, DreamBF, OurDream AI) charge extra for images, voice and video. If you want lots of selfies or nightly calls, the real cost can be several times the subscription.
- **Free apps** (Character AI, SpicyChat) cost nothing but come with ads, filters or short memory.

## What to look for in a virtual boyfriend app

- **Memory:** the biggest difference between apps. Test it by mentioning something and asking about it a few days later.
- **Personality controls:** can you set how he talks and behaves, or only pick a template?
- **Voice and visuals:** do you want to hear him, see him, or both? See [AI boyfriends with voice calls](/ai-boyfriend-voice-call/).
- **Real cost:** check whether images and calls use tokens on top of the subscription.
- **Privacy:** use a separate email and avoid sharing identifying details.
