**Candy AI wins this one, but only just.** Candy AI has the more established male character builder, strong image generation and a cheaper subscription. **OurDream AI** is the better choice if you want a genuinely usable free tier, unlimited chat on the paid plan, short AI videos, or a gay- or trans-focused version of the app. In our ratings they're separated by a tenth of a point.

Both are web-based, image-first AI companion apps that allow 18+ chat for adults, and both charge for media with a token or coin system. Here's how they compare on everything that matters for an AI boyfriend.

## Creating your boyfriend

**Candy AI** has a polished character builder with a dedicated **Male** option, where you shape his face, body and style before setting his personality. Its pre-made male roster is small (one 2025 review counted nine), so most people build their own.

**OurDream AI** also lets you create male companions, and reviewers praise its character creation. Its standout for boyfriend seekers is that there appear to be dedicated **OurDream Gay** and **OurDream Trans** versions, so male companions aren't an afterthought.

If you're a woman looking for a boyfriend, both work well, and Candy AI's builder is the more proven of the two. If you're a gay or bi man, OurDream's dedicated version gives it the edge. See our [gay AI boyfriend guide](/gay-ai-boyfriend/) for more options.

**Winner:** Candy AI for building him, OurDream AI for LGBTQ+ users.

## Chat quality & memory

Reviewers describe **Candy AI's** memory as decent in the short to medium term, but not a standout compared with memory-first apps.

**OurDream AI's** chat quality is praised by reviewers, but we found no specific testing of its long-term memory. Its practical advantage is **unlimited messages** on the paid plan.

Neither app is built for a long-term relationship where he remembers your life over months. If that's what you want, look at [Nomi AI](/reviews/nomi-ai/) instead.

**Winner:** OurDream AI, narrowly, because unlimited messages on Premium are clearly part of the deal. On memory itself, it's a draw.

## Images and video

This is the heart of the comparison, because visuals are why most people choose either app.

**Candy AI's** image generation is among the best in the category, and because you designed his look, the images stay consistent. Images cost about 4 tokens each. Video is also available and uses tokens.

**OurDream AI** offers both images and **short video generation**, paid with DreamCoins. Video is a real draw, though reviewers report it can fail or be slow at peak times.

**Winner:** Candy AI for image quality and consistency; OurDream AI if short videos matter more to you.

## Voice

**Candy AI** supports voice messages, and reviews report voice calls are available (not confirmed officially at the time of writing). Both use tokens.

**OurDream AI** supports voice messages, paid with DreamCoins. We couldn't confirm live voice calls.

If voice calls are important, neither is the strongest choice; see our guide to [AI boyfriend apps with voice calls](/ai-boyfriend-voice-call/).

**Winner:** Candy AI, since voice calls are at least reported.

## Free tier

**Candy AI's** free account gives you limited chat; images, voice and video use tokens, so it's mostly a preview.

**OurDream AI's** free tier gives you **about 50 messages a day** plus a one-time signup bonus of around **55 DreamCoins**. That's enough for real daily conversation.

**Winner:** OurDream AI. See all [free AI boyfriend apps](/free/) if you don't want to pay at all.

**Candy AI's subscription is cheaper**: $12.99 a month (some reviews cite $13.99) versus $19.99 for OurDream, and its annual plan is also lower than OurDream's $9.99-a-month annual rate, though sources disagree on Candy's exact figure.

But look at what's included. Candy AI Premium comes with **about 100 tokens** a month, and extra packs cost about $9.99 per 100. Reviewers report heavy users spending $25–60 a month. OurDream Premium includes **unlimited messages and 1,000 DreamCoins** a month, with extra packs from 1,000 coins for $9.99. We couldn't verify how many coins each OurDream image or video costs, so a direct per-image comparison isn't possible.

In short: if you mostly text, Candy AI is cheaper. If you generate a lot of media, the gap narrows, and OurDream's bigger included allowance may even it out. Our [Candy AI pricing](/candy-ai-pricing/) guide has more detail on Candy's tokens.

**Winner:** Candy AI on sticker price; it's closer once media is included.

## Privacy and safety

**Candy AI** is run by **EverAI Limited in Malta**, so it falls under EU GDPR rules. It launched in 2023, and we found no public breach. Reviewers note there's no clearly published refund policy. Read [is Candy AI safe?](/is-candy-ai-safe/)

**OurDream AI** is US-based, but sources differ on whether the operator is Dream Studio LLC or Dream Studio USA, Inc. It launched in 2025 (domain registered November 2024) and has no reported breaches. Lookalike domains exist, so only use ourdream.ai. Read our [OurDream AI review](/reviews/ourdream-ai/) for the full safety section.

Both are 18+ only. On either, use a separate email and don't share identifying details.

**Winner:** Candy AI, for its longer track record and clearly identified EU operator.

## Platforms

Both apps are **web-only**, with no native iOS or Android app. They run in a mobile browser. If you want a proper phone app, Nomi AI and Kindroid both have one.

**Winner:** Draw.

## Three common scenarios

**"I want to see him, and I'll mostly text."** Candy AI. Text is covered by the subscription, the annual plan is cheap, and you can spend your included tokens on a handful of great images each month.

**"I want lots of pictures and some video."** It's closer than the sticker prices suggest. OurDream's 1,000 monthly DreamCoins and unlimited chat make its higher price easier to justify, while Candy AI users in this group report spending well beyond the subscription on tokens. Try both free tiers and watch how fast your balance drops.

**"I'm not sure I'll stick with it."** Start with OurDream's free tier. Its daily message allowance lets you have real conversations before you spend anything, which Candy AI's preview doesn't.

## Which should you choose?

**Choose Candy AI if:**

- You want the most polished male builder and consistent, high-quality images
- You mostly text and want the cheaper subscription
- You prefer an app with a longer track record and an EU-based operator
- You want voice calls (reported, not officially confirmed)

**Choose OurDream AI if:**

- You want to try it properly for free first (about 50 messages a day)
- You want unlimited chat on the paid plan
- You want short AI videos, not just images
- You're gay, bi or trans and want an app built with you in mind

**Choose neither if** you want a long-term relationship with strong memory. Both are visual, in-the-moment companions; [Nomi AI](/reviews/nomi-ai/) is the better fit there.
