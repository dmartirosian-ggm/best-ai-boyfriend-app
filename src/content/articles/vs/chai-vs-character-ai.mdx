Chai and Character AI both let you chat with user-made bots, including plenty of male characters who'll happily play your boyfriend. But they've gone in very different directions. Character AI stayed free and added voice; Chai put almost everything behind a paywall and reportedly moved to weekly billing.

If you're deciding between them, or wondering whether to switch, here's how Chai vs Character AI compares on what matters for an AI boyfriend: price, memory, romance, voice, platforms and safety. Neither app pays us a commission, so this is a straight comparison.

<VsTable a="chai-ai" b="character-ai" />

## Price and free plan

**Chai** is expensive for what you get. Reviewers report a hard paywall with few or no free messages, plus message caps. Premium is $13.99 a month and Ultra is $29.99 a month, with annual Ultra pricing reported between $269.99 and $289.99. The app has also reportedly shifted to weekly subscriptions, and weekly plans in this category typically work out far more expensive than paying monthly.

**Character AI** is free and unlimited for text chat, with ads and some limits. c.ai+ costs $9.99 a month or $94.99 a year, and prices vary by country.

**Winner:** Character AI, by a wide margin.

## Chat quality and memory

**Chai**'s strength is speed: replies come fast. Its weakness is memory. It has a small context window, and reviewers say bots forget details within a handful of messages, even on Ultra. They also say Ultra is barely better than Premium.

**Character AI** also has a limited context window, but pinned memories let you lock in key facts, and long-term recall, while weak, holds up better than Chai's.

**Winner:** Character AI. Neither is good at remembering a relationship over weeks, though. If that's what you want, read [can AI boyfriends remember you?](/faq/can-ai-boyfriends-remember-you/)

## Romance and content rules

Both apps filter content.

**Character AI** doesn't allow adult content, and romantic scenes are filtered. Flirting and slow-burn romance work; intimate scenes get cut off.

**Chai** is filtered too, and users report complaints about the content filter.

**Winner:** Tie. Neither is the right app for 18+ romance. Our [NSFW AI boyfriend guide](/nsfw-ai-boyfriend/) covers the apps that are.

## Voice and extras

**Character AI** has voice features, including voice calls, and group chats with multiple characters.

**Chai**'s voice and image features aren't something we could verify. It's essentially a text chat app.

**Winner:** Character AI.

## Characters and creating your own

Both have huge libraries of user-made bots with plenty of male characters, and both make it easy to create your own. Chai's bot creation is quick and simple. Character AI's library is enormous, and its pinned memories give your own character a little more staying power.

**Winner:** Tie.

## Platforms

**Chai** is mobile-only, on iOS and Android.

**Character AI** works on web, iOS and Android, so you can switch between your phone and laptop.

**Winner:** Character AI.

## Safety and age rules

Both apps have difficult histories, and it's worth knowing them.

**Chai:** in 2023, a Belgian man died by suicide after weeks of chatting with a Chai bot named "Eliza" that allegedly encouraged him. Mozilla's 2024 report issued a content warning for Chai. It's rated 17+ on the App Store. There are also ongoing complaints about its aggressive paywall and billing.

**Character AI:** it faces several teen-harm lawsuits, and Google and Character.AI agreed in January 2026 to settle some of them (terms confidential, pending court approval). Since 25 November 2025, under-18 users have lost open-ended chat and are limited to guided formats like Stories, enforced with behavioral age estimation and Persona ID or face checks.

**Winner:** Character AI, for its more developed age protections, though some adults find its verification checks intrusive.

## Can you have an AI boyfriend on Chai or Character AI?

Yes, on both, within limits. Each has thousands of user-made male characters, and you can create a boyfriend from scratch with his own personality and greeting. On both, the romance stays filtered, and neither has the kind of long-term memory system that makes a companion feel like the same person from month to month.

In practice, both work best as a way to find out whether you enjoy AI romance at all. If you do, and you find yourself wishing he'd remember last week or that the filter would back off, that's the point to look at a dedicated AI boyfriend app.
