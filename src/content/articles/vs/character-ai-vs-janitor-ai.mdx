Character AI and Janitor AI are the two big free roleplay platforms, and if you want an AI boyfriend without paying, you've probably tried at least one. They look similar on the surface: huge libraries of user-made characters, plenty of male love interests, and free chat. Underneath, they're built for different people.

The short version: **Character AI** is the polished, safe-for-work option with voice features. **Janitor AI** is the text-only, unfiltered option that rewards a bit of tinkering. Here's how they compare in detail.

## Romance and content rules

This is the biggest difference, and for many people the only one that matters.

**Character AI** doesn't allow adult content, and romantic scenes are filtered. You can flirt, go on story dates and have a slow-burn romance, but intimate moments get cut off or redirected. For adults who want a romantic relationship to feel real, that's the most common frustration with the app.

**Janitor AI** allows NSFW roleplay on the web. You need to verify your age and switch it on at both the account and the character level. Adult content isn't available in its mobile apps.

**Winner:** Janitor AI, if you're an adult who wants romance without a filter. If you prefer things PG, Character AI's filter isn't a downside.

## Chat quality and memory

Neither app is great at remembering you long-term.

**Character AI** has a limited context window. Pinned memories help you keep key facts in play, but long-term recall is weak, so a boyfriend you've talked to for weeks can lose track of details.

**Janitor AI**'s free built-in model, JLLM, has an even shorter context and tends to get repetitive after 20 to 30 messages. Reviewers also report quality and uptime issues. The workaround is to connect a stronger external model with your own API key, which can make replies much better, at a cost.

**Winner:** Character AI out of the box. Janitor AI can beat it if you connect a good model, but that takes setup and money. If memory matters most, neither is the right tool; see [can AI boyfriends remember you?](/faq/can-ai-boyfriends-remember-you/)

## Voice, images and extras

**Character AI** has voice features, including voice calls, plus group chats with multiple characters. It isn't built for generating images of your boyfriend.

**Janitor AI** is text-only: no voice messages, no voice calls, no image generation.

**Winner:** Character AI, clearly.

## Character library

Both have enormous libraries of user-made characters, including thousands of male and romance bots, from anime love interests to fantasy princes. Both let you create your own.

Character AI's library leans safe-for-work because of its rules; Janitor AI's includes adult-oriented characters. The size and variety are similar enough that this comes down to what kind of stories you want.

**Winner:** Tie.

## Price

**Character AI** is free and unlimited with ads. c.ai+ costs $9.99 a month or $94.99 a year (prices vary by country) for perks.

**Janitor AI** is free with its built-in model, though some 2026 sources mention a cap of about 50 requests a day. A paid tier (reported as Pro or Janitor+) reportedly launched in June 2026, with conflicting prices between $9.99 and $12.99 a month. If you use your own API key, you pay the model provider per token, which reviewers say can range from a few dollars to $15 to $50 a month depending on the model.

**Winner:** Character AI, for being free with predictable pricing. Janitor AI can be free too, but costs become harder to predict once you chase better replies.

## Safety, privacy and age rules

**Character AI** accounts start at 13. Since 25 November 2025, users under 18 have lost open-ended chat and are limited to guided formats like Stories. It uses behavioral age estimation and can ask for a Persona ID or face check, which some adults find intrusive. The company faces several teen-harm lawsuits, and Google and Character.AI agreed in January 2026 to settle some of them (terms confidential, pending court approval).

**Janitor AI** requires age verification for NSFW. The main privacy point is that if you connect a third-party model, your chats are sent to that provider as well as Janitor AI.

**Winner:** Tie. Character AI is stricter about minors but can ask adults for ID or face checks; Janitor AI is adults-only for NSFW but spreads your chats further if you use API keys. Either way, use a separate email and avoid sharing identifying details. Our [AI boyfriend safety guide](/faq/are-ai-boyfriends-safe/) covers the basics.

## Ease of use

**Character AI** is the easier app. Sign up, pick a character, and start talking on web, iOS or Android.

**Janitor AI** is simple at first, but getting the best experience means understanding models, API keys and settings. Reliability of the free model also varies.

**Winner:** Character AI.

## What they have in common

It helps to know what you won't escape by switching between the two:

- **Both are character platforms.** You browse or build characters and start chats, rather than developing one ongoing relationship.
- **Both have weak long-term memory** on their default settings.
- **Both are free to start** and have huge libraries of user-made male characters.
- **Neither is built for pictures.** If you want to see him, you need an image-first app.

## Which should you choose?

- **Choose Character AI if** you want free, unlimited, safe-for-work romance with voice features and zero setup.
- **Choose Janitor AI if** you're an adult who wants unfiltered roleplay, you're happy with text only, and you don't mind tinkering with models.
- **Choose neither if** you want one boyfriend who remembers you, voice calls with adult chat, or pictures of him. See below.
