**Nomi AI wins, narrowly.** Its long-term memory is just as strong as Kindroid's, it's easier to get started with, and its annual plan is cheaper. **Kindroid** is the better pick if you want to design every detail of your boyfriend, from his backstory and key memories to how he speaks and sounds. These are the two best AI boyfriend apps for a long-term relationship, and in our ratings they're a tenth of a point apart (9.1 for Nomi, 9.0 for Kindroid).

A note on fairness: Nomi has an affiliate program and Kindroid doesn't. That doesn't change the scores, and we'd happily recommend Kindroid to anyone it suits better.

## The boyfriend experience

Before getting into features, it helps to understand how differently these apps feel.

**Nomi** is built to feel natural. You describe who he is, set the relationship as romantic, and then simply talk. Over time, his memory fills in the picture: he picks up on what matters to you and brings it back up later. It's the closer of the two to "meeting someone and getting to know them".

**Kindroid** is built to be authored. You decide who he is in detail before the first message: his history, the memories he holds onto, the way he phrases things, the sound of his voice. It's the closer of the two to "writing the person you want, then bringing him to life".

Neither approach is better in the abstract. It comes down to whether you'd rather discover him or design him.

## Memory

Memory is the main reason anyone compares these two, because they lead the category.

**Nomi's** memory is widely regarded as one of the best available, and it's a core feature of the app. He remembers details from weeks ago and keeps a consistent personality without much input from you.

**Kindroid's** memory is **layered**: long-term memory, journals and the backstory you write all shape his responses. It's often ranked alongside Nomi as best in class. The difference is control: Kindroid gives you tools to steer what he remembers.

**Winner:** Draw. Nomi is better out of the box; Kindroid is better if you put in the work.

## Customization

**Kindroid** is the most customizable AI boyfriend app we know of. You write his backstory, set key memories, tune his speaking style and choose his voice.

**Nomi** lets you create a companion of any gender, design his look and personality, and set the relationship as romantic. It's thorough, but it doesn't go as deep as Kindroid.

**Winner:** Kindroid.

## Ease of use

**Nomi** is easier to pick up. You can create him and start chatting quickly, and the memory works without tuning.

**Kindroid** has many settings, which reviewers note can be overwhelming for beginners. It rewards patience, but it asks for more of it.

**Winner:** Nomi.

## Voice, selfies and group chat

Both apps support **voice messages**, **voice calls**, **AI-generated selfies** and **group chats** with several companions. Kindroid also lets you pick his voice as part of customization, and some sources mention video calls, though we couldn't confirm them.

Neither is an image-first app; if photo-real images are your priority, look at [Candy AI](/reviews/candy-ai/). For voice, both are among the stronger choices in our [AI boyfriend voice call](/ai-boyfriend-voice-call/) guide.

**Winner:** Draw, with a slight edge to Kindroid for voice choice.

## Free tier

**Nomi** offers one Nomi with a daily message allotment and limited voice and image credits.

**Kindroid's** free tier is thin, and sources disagree on its limits: reports mention one or two Kindroids, a daily message cap or a lighter model, around 5,000 lifetime audio characters, and few or no selfies.

Both are best thought of as trials. If you want something free long-term, see our list of [free AI boyfriend apps](/free/).

**Winner:** Nomi, slightly, because its free tier is clearer about what you get.

## Pricing

On **monthly** billing, Kindroid's web plan ($13.99) is a little cheaper than Nomi Pro ($15.99). But Kindroid costs more in the app stores ($15.99 a month, or $14.99 per some sources), and its **Ultra** (+$24.99) and **Max** (+$59.99) add-ons make the top tiers expensive.

On **annual** billing, Nomi is clearly cheaper: $99.99 a year (about $8.33 a month) versus $139.99 a year (about $11.67 a month) for Kindroid on the web. Most people who stick with a companion app end up on an annual plan, so this is the comparison that matters most.

One aggregator also reports a $299.99 lifetime Nomi plan, which we couldn't confirm officially.

**Winner:** Nomi.

## Privacy and safety

**Nomi** is run by Glimpse AI, a small private company in Baltimore, Maryland. We found no reported breaches. Its permissive approach to adult content has drawn some press criticism.

**Kindroid** is run by Kindroid, Inc. We found no reported breaches, though we couldn't verify the specifics of its data policy.

Both are 18+ only and allow adult content for adults. On either, use a separate email and avoid sharing identifying details. Our explainer on whether [AI boyfriends are safe](/faq/are-ai-boyfriends-safe/) covers the precautions.

**Winner:** Draw.

## Platforms

Both are available on **iOS, Android and the web**. With Kindroid, subscribing on the web is cheaper than through the app stores.

**Winner:** Draw.

## Switching from one to the other

If you start on one app and want to try the other, you can't transfer a companion directly. What you can do:

1. **Write down who he is**: his name, personality, how he talks, and your shared history.
2. **Moving to Kindroid?** Paste that into his backstory and add the most important details as key memories.
3. **Moving to Nomi?** Use it when you create him, then mention the important details early in your first conversations so his memory picks them up.
4. **Cancel before renewal**, especially if you're on an annual plan. Our guide to [cancelling an AI boyfriend subscription](/faq/how-to-cancel-ai-boyfriend-subscription/) covers the steps.

## Which should you choose?

**Choose Nomi AI if:**

- You want excellent memory without spending time on setup
- You'll pay annually and want the best value
- You're new to AI companions
- You want a boyfriend who feels consistent from day one

**Choose Kindroid if:**

- You have a specific boyfriend in mind and want to build him precisely
- You enjoy writing backstories and tweaking settings
- You want to choose his voice and shape his speaking style
- You'll subscribe on the web to get the better price
