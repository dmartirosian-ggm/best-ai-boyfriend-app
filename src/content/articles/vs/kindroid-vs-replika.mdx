Kindroid and Replika sit at opposite ends of the AI companion spectrum. Replika is the friendly, polished, mainstream app with a 3D avatar. Kindroid is the power-user's choice, where you can shape every detail of your companion. Both can be a boyfriend, both have voice calls, and both have iOS, Android and web apps.

Here's how Kindroid vs Replika compares for people who want an **AI boyfriend**. Neither app pays us a commission, so this is a straight comparison, with a note at the end on when to skip both.

<VsTable a="kindroid" b="replika" />

## Memory and personality

**Kindroid**'s memory is strong: layered long-term memory, journals and a backstory field, often ranked alongside Nomi's as best-in-class. You can set "key memories" so the facts that matter most are never lost.

**Replika** has a memory and diary system, but its memory is moderate, and users often report it forgetting details.

**Winner:** Kindroid, by a wide margin.

## Customization

**Kindroid** gives you more control than almost any other app. You write his backstory, set key memories, tune how he speaks, pick his voice and generate selfies. You can create companions of any gender.

**Replika** lets your avatar be male, but you can't build custom characters the way you can on Kindroid, and you get one companion per account. His personality develops through conversation rather than through settings you control.

**Winner:** Kindroid.

## Romance and adult content

**Kindroid** supports romantic relationships with fully customizable companions, and adult content is allowed for 18+ users.

**Replika** offers a "Boyfriend" relationship status, but only on a paid plan; on the free plan he's your friend. Adult content is heavily restricted, and after Italy suspended the app in February 2023, Replika removed erotic roleplay, which caused a major backlash among long-time users.

**Winner:** Kindroid.

## Voice, video and visuals

**Kindroid** supports voice messages, voice calls (with a choice of voices) and selfies.

**Replika** supports voice messages and voice calls, and adds a 3D avatar, an AR mode and video-call features, which are unusual among companion apps.

**Winner:** Replika, for its avatar and AR. If you'd rather have selfie-style images of him, Kindroid's are more flexible.

## Multiple companions and group chat

**Kindroid** supports multiple companions and group chats.

**Replika** allows only one companion per account, with no group chats.

**Winner:** Kindroid.

## Price

Replika prices are in-app and vary by region. Some sources cite slightly different Kindroid app-store prices.

**Winner:** Depends on billing. Kindroid on the web is cheaper month to month; Replika Pro is much cheaper on an annual plan. Kindroid's add-ons make its top tiers expensive.

## Privacy and safety

**Kindroid** has no reported breaches. Its data policy specifics weren't verified in our research.

**Replika** is run by Luka, Inc., which Italy's data protection authority fined €5 million in 2025 (decided April, announced May) for GDPR violations, including lacking a legal basis for processing, poor transparency and no age verification before February 2023. A further probe into model training was reported.

**Winner:** Kindroid, on track record, though with less public detail on its policies. Both are 18+. For general tips, see [are AI boyfriends safe?](/faq/are-ai-boyfriends-safe/)

## Ease of use

**Replika** is one of the easiest companion apps to start with. Download it, set up your companion and start talking. Its polished mobile apps and supportive tone make it a gentle first companion.

**Kindroid** has a learning curve. Its many settings are its strength, but beginners can find them complex, and the thin free tier means you'll want to commit before you've explored everything.

**Winner:** Replika.

## Trying them for free

If you want to test before you pay, the two free tiers feel very different.

**Kindroid's** free tier is thin, and sources disagree on exactly what's included: one or two Kindroids, either a daily message cap or a lighter model, a small lifetime audio allowance and few or no selfies. You can get a feel for the customization and memory, but not for long.

**Replika's** free tier is friendship only. You can chat with basic features, but the boyfriend relationship status and voice calls need a subscription, so you can't test it as a romantic partner without paying.

**Winner:** Kindroid, narrowly, because you can at least try a romantic setup. See [can you have an AI boyfriend for free?](/faq/can-you-have-an-ai-boyfriend-for-free/)

## Who each app suits

**Kindroid suits people who like to build.** If you enjoy writing a character, deciding how he talks and what he remembers, and adjusting him over time, nothing else gives you as much control.

**Replika suits people who want to be looked after.** If you'd rather open an app and talk to a warm, steady companion without configuring anything, and romance is a small part of what you want, Replika's simplicity is a feature, not a flaw.
